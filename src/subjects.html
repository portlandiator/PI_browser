About this collection

A window onto the Bahá'í Writings.

This site offers different ways to explore the original texts and translations attributed to the Central Figures of the Bahá'í Faith: the Báb, Bahá’u’lláh, and ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, which are accessible in the public domain. This is a personal project and accuracy is not guaranteed; please visit the official website at https://www.bahai.org/library for fully authenticated texts.

Catalog view

Search by word or phrase (putting exact phrases in quotation marks). Search ignores diacritics and common Persian/Arabic character variants. Enter a filename ID for direct lookup. Use the menu at left to narrow your search by date, recipient, and other metadata.

Subject view

Texts in the catalog have been tagged with human-generated subject categories. Some tagging is human generated and some is AI-assisted. Browse by subject by searching in the left-hand panel, or use the knowledge graph in the center panel to navigate to a related topic. Relevant passages touching on the subject appear below the knowledge graph.

Volume view

The texts have been arranged in rough chronological order and provisionally grouped into 192 volumes. The volumes can be read in-browser or downloaded in PDF format.

About the sources

Texts and metadata are derived from the Partial Inventory project (version 6.01), an individual initiative not affiliated with the Bahá'í World Centre. Translations in blue text are authorized, those in black text are provisional and in most cases are AI-generated. Dates may use different calendars or include uncertainty. Some records have no text or are missing the original language text. For a more complete introduction to the Partial Inventory project see blog.loomofreality.org

To interact with this collection via LLM, see the separate Gemini Notebook instances in English and Persian/Arabic

This is a static collection browser. It has no accounts or tracking. Reading preferences and search history stay on your device.
