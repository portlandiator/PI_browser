About this collection

A window onto the Bahá'í Writings.

The Partial Inventory browser brings together original texts and translations attributed to the Central Figures of the Bahá'í Faith: the Báb, Bahá’u’lláh, and ‘Abdu’l-Bahá and accessible in the public domain. Accuracy is not guaranteed; please visit the official website at https://www.bahai.org/library for fully authenticated materials.

Reading in two languages

English appears on the left and Persian/Arabic (where available) on the right. On mobile phone screens, each English paragraph is followed by its original; tilting the screen may restore the two-column format.

Searching the collection

Search whole words in any order, or put an exact phrase in quotation marks. Search ignores diacritics and common Persian/Arabic character variants. Enter a filename ID for direct lookup. To interact with this collection via LLM, see the Gemini Notebook instances in English and Persian/Arabic.

About the source metadata

Dates, titles, recipients, and other metadata are supplied from the Partial Inventory project (version 6.01), an individual initiative not affiliated with the Bahá'í World Centre. Dates may use different calendars or include uncertainty. Some records have no text or are missing the original language text. Translation status is shown as the source metadata code when provided; inclusion here does not establish that a translation is authorized. For more on the Partial Inventory project see blog.loomofreality.org

This is an independent collection browser. It has no accounts or tracking. Reading preferences and search history stay in your browser.
