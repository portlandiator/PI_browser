About this collection

Windows onto the Bahá'í Writings.

This site offers three different means of exploring those original texts and translations attributed to the Central Figures of the Bahá'í Faith -- the Báb, Bahá’u’lláh, and ‘Abdu’l-Bahá -- which are accessible in the public domain. It is a personal project and accuracy is not guaranteed; please visit the official website at https://www.bahai.org/library for fully authenticated texts.

Catalog view

Search by word or phrase for a specific text, or use the menu at left to narrow your search by date, recipient, catalog ID, and other metadata. View a text in the original Arabic/Persian, in English translation (authorized translations are displayed in blue, provisional in black), or side by side where available.

Subject view

Browse by subject by searching in the left-hand panel, or use the knowledge graph in the center panel to navigate to a related subject. All subjects and relationships are human-generated; the brief subject summaries are AI-generated. Relevant passages touching on the subject -- some human generated and some selected with the help of AI and lightly vetted -- appear below the knowledge graph. These passages can be downloaded to pdf for offline reading.

Volume view

The 29,000+ texts have been arranged in approximate chronological order and provisionally grouped into 192 volumes. The volumes can be read in-browser or downloaded to pdf.

About the sources

Texts and metadata are derived from the Partial Inventory project (version 6.01), an individual initiative not affiliated with the Bahá'í World Centre. Some records have no text or are missing the original language text. For a more complete introduction to the Partial Inventory project see blog.loomofreality.org

To interact with this collection via LLM, see the separate Gemini Notebook instances in English and Persian/Arabic

This is a static collection browser. It has no accounts or tracking and runs entirely on your browser. Reading preferences and search history stay on your device.
