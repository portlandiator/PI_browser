About this collection

A window onto the Bahá'í Revelation.

The Partial Inventory browser brings together original texts and translations attributed to the Báb, Bahá’u’lláh, and ‘Abdu’l-Bahá, with their English translations. Accuracy of texts and translations is not guaranteed; please visit the official website at www.bahai.org for fully authenticated texts.

Reading in two languages

English appears on the left and Persian/Arabic on the right. Paragraphs are paired by their supplied order when counts match; this does not certify editorial alignment. On small screens, each English paragraph is followed by its original.

Searching the collection

Search whole words in any order, or put a phrase in quotation marks. All terms must occur in the same language version. Search ignores diacritics and common Persian/Arabic character variants. Text spelling is preserved. Enter an exact filename ID, with or without .txt, for direct lookup.

About the source data

Dates, titles, and attribution are supplied catalogue information from the Partial Inventory project. Dates may use different calendars or include uncertainty. Some records have no text or are missing a language version. Translation status is shown as the source metadata code when provided; inclusion here does not establish that a translation is authorized. For more on the Partial Inventory project see blog.loomofreality.org

This is an independent collection browser. It has no accounts or tracking. Reading preferences stay in your browser.
