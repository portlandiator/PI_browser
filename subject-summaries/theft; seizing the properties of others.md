# theft; seizing the properties of others

Another person's property must be respected, regardless of need, religious difference, or judgments about the owner's character. Faith demands trustworthiness and generosity toward the poor; it never licenses appropriation in religion's name. The Kitáb-i-Aqdas also prescribes penalties for theft. Their detailed application belongs to the Universal House of Justice and a future condition of society, distinguishing the enduring moral prohibition from the later implementation of its penal provisions.
