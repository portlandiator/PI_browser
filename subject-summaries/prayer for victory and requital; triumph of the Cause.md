# prayer for victory and requital; triumph of the Cause

Prayers for victory ask that the Cause advance, believers gain strength, and its message reach new people despite reverses and opposition. Light overcoming darkness expresses confidence in eventual triumph. Appeals for requital can also voice the anguish of persecution in forceful, historically situated language. Such imagery should not be mistaken for a general mandate: hope for the Faith’s progress joins petitions for divine justice and protection with courage to continue its work.

The hope of victory is framed by the [forces of light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994), where crises may be followed by renewed strength, and by the practical work of [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), through which the Faith’s message advances.
