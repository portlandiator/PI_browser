# praise of Eastern values; culture

Bahá’í writings express affection for Eastern peoples and traditions, praising qualities such as hospitality, intelligence, learning, spirituality, and historical achievement. Praise also appears in hopes for Iran’s future and recognition of the contributions of particular communities. These affirmations honor real cultural strengths without implying that every custom is ideal or that Eastern societies are alike.

Iran’s past, present, and future is the closest historical connection: the passages especially celebrate Persia’s heritage and anticipate its contribution to humanity. The neighboring subject links this praise to Iran’s changing circumstances, rather than treating Eastern culture as a single uniform tradition.
