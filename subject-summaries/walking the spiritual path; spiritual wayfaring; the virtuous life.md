# walking the spiritual path; spiritual wayfaring; the virtuous life

Walking the spiritual path takes courage, radiant good humor, independent judgment, and the perseverance to finish what one begins. A teaching attributed to Bahá’u’lláh names these four qualities; another counsel asks believers to embody even one teaching fully, while other passages speak of angelic character and keeping the heart’s fire alive. Wayfaring thus joins inner devotion to visible conduct and sustained action.

The path takes concrete form in the [characteristics of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), including [honesty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), [courtesy and culture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF1F45263A44466C9655EE1714DE1B33), [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946), [chastity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FEC681A8D62B4EA3B73B57371D4A34C7), [piety](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E5E0436334E8467AA2757EF389C2161A), [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [compassion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=210D276A06CCE27ECA75FDC0B8F5205C), [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450), [courage](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91DA577AA068EEADBB6D0F598A93F0CC), [forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), and [excellence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5B4432116D03EDAB41C16C80226D731C).
