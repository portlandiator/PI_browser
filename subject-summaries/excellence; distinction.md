# excellence; distinction

Excellence means bringing diligence, skill, and integrity to whatever one undertakes. It can appear in craftsmanship, study, commerce, the arts, and character; persistent effort and love of one’s vocation make improvement a source of joy. Distinction is not chiefly wealth or ancestry but moral and spiritual quality. Children and students should strive to develop their capacities fully, while each person’s work should honor the Cause through competence, sincerity, and service.

Excellence in one’s vocation can make [work worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D), and cultivated capacities begin with [children’s education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4). The pursuit belongs to a [virtuous spiritual life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549).
