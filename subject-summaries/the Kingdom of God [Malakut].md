# the Kingdom of God [Malakut]

The Kingdom of God, or Malakut, is the spiritual realm of divine life, knowledge, and nearness. Its growth resembles rain and rising light; rebirth by the Spirit opens this state and distinguishes spiritual life from mere physical existence. The Kingdom is both a reality toward which the soul turns and a present inward condition made accessible through guidance and transformation.

The Kingdom concerns the [station of the true believer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9A8742BB523FE7870795B9A71B370B0A), [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), and [recompense for belief and right action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70243FE151214450B3A87930FD5DF192). It appears through [wine as transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102), the [inmost heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6843E7D91F1248EE96FCE5A4E6B06155), and the passage from [evanescence to eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70850E6FB45E400FA41773E2557B5CF3), including the [realm of similitudes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4B07E7C749314DBEB8C4158C26A02453) and [living waters](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992). Its mysteries invite [spiritual discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623) and [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), as the [Manifestation as Sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) brings [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and awakens [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B). The Kingdom is approached along the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), through [life in the spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AE0799C6978C430E8B793BC7101E1A12), and beyond the limits of [death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313) and material separation (n16). It calls for [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), offers [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), and reorients the heart from [transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), [worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85), and attachment through [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C). Access depends on [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), [second birth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8C84F900ED34DA98391B4A8593684E1), and [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8); it is sustained by [transcendence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=73709CA9F1694378BFE128A306C8D7CC) and answered with [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B). The soul’s [higher and lower natures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5410D4DAE5454E8D81B13EC3E9DA2F23) meet within this spiritual reality. It connects the visible and spiritual through [the material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61).
