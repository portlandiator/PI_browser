# consorting with all; being kind; loving to all

Kindness calls for treating every person with dignity, including strangers, the sick, and those whose beliefs differ from one’s own. Bahá’í counsel urges friendly association across religious and social lines, without contempt or avoidance, and asks believers to become a refuge and source of happiness to others. Love for all humanity is not the same as intimate personal affection for everyone; it is a principled goodwill expressed through courtesy, compassion, and useful care.

Kindness is a defining [quality of believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and shapes the [meaning of Bahá’í identity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7). Its reach includes [loving enemies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D614A134412E9DA43B77542E47091BD), affirming the [unity of religions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), and practicing [compassion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=210D276A06CCE27ECA75FDC0B8F5205C). This conduct serves religion’s [personal and social purpose](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=29AF567C76864D208EA3BD39BC5CC7CF), broadens [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700), and becomes practical [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed in a closely related topic on [human betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80). It supports [unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) and counters [conflict](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48768953025E40D1B1F978FF61180A24), strengthening [fellowship among believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A) while extending companionship to the [wayward](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C8C2297FED2AE873B7C3387A09CF2ADB). This universal regard is also a [prerequisite for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) and part of the [virtuous spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549); it informs both [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) and its [methods](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=369DAA81C51F4587B8F070614833A216).
