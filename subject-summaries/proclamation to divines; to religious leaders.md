# proclamation to divines; to religious leaders

Proclamation to divines challenges religious leaders to judge a new revelation fairly rather than rely on inherited assumptions. They are urged to fear God, set aside vain imaginings, and reconsider the evidence by which they accepted earlier faith and now turn away. The appeal also confronts clerical authority directly, calling its members to relinquish the pen when divine revelation is moving upon the Tablet of Destiny.

Religious leadership faces [rejection, opposition, and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), since resistance to a new claim can intensify pressure against its adherents.
