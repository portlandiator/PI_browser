# prayer for pilgrims

Pilgrimage is a journey undertaken to visit a sacred place and seek spiritual nearness. Prayers for pilgrims acknowledge the effort of crossing distance, often by land and sea, and ask that the visitor’s longing be accepted, the journey protected, and its benefits carried home. The physical arrival at a shrine matters as an act of devotion, while the deeper aim is presence, remembrance, and a life renewed by the encounter.

Pilgrimage petitions rely on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), accompanying the traveler’s physical journey with supplication for protection, acceptance, and spiritual benefit.
