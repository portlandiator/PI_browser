# independent investigation of truth; reality

Independent investigation of truth asks each person to examine reality rather than accept inherited beliefs through blind imitation. It relies on reason, observation, and spiritual discernment, and requires patience and freedom from prejudice. The aim is not private opinion for its own sake, but a clearer grasp of reality that can support unity and sound action.

The method draws on [rational arguments](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12FD56FFF2C74384AF52D04E7CEE255F) and the patience described in [the condition of search](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA). Its social context includes [freedom of thought and action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3AF5CD058F82471FA2236DF51BDF9F38), [oneness of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), and the principles gathered in [enumerations of Bahá’í teachings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7). It also challenges [blind imitation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E0F43FD317AEC54131AD435E2317B05) and supports [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D). The responsibilities of discovery connect with [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) and the shared commitments of [unity of thought and belief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A4AA0909671F483EB793112083AAB33D).
