# religion as basis of morality; of divine civilization

Religion provides a moral foundation for civilization by cultivating virtues that sustain trust, justice, compassion, and concern for others. Its laws and teachings can support social order and public tranquillity, while spiritual education helps people act with integrity and serve humanity. Religion is not merely private belief: its civilizing force appears in ethical conduct and institutions. When faith weakens, disorder may grow; when it is joined with reason and unity, it can contribute to a peaceful and flourishing society.

Religion’s social role follows its [personal and public purpose](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=29AF567C76864D208EA3BD39BC5CC7CF) and must harmonize with [science](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=99656E1B1D7344D8930ACA530FDE210B). It supports [justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34), [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), and the [oneness of religions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), while reward and consequence are also linked to its moral framework ([reward and punishment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF5256DAFAB846958221CF1F4616DFCA)). It should accord with [reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3C0575BFA942CA95AB98EA453F1294), shape the [virtuous path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and contribute to [world peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675).
