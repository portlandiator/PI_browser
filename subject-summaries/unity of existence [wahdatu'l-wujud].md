# unity of existence [wahdatu'l-wujud]

Unity of existence asks how one reality relates to the many forms of the world. Sea-and-wave imagery can express unity and multiplicity, while Bahá’í theological cautions distinguish God’s unknowable Essence from the realm of Command and created beings. The term belongs to a field of philosophical debate: some formulations are criticized for identifying God too closely with creation, and the differences among explanations should remain visible rather than be flattened into one settled formula.

The subject’s critical debate meets [pantheism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EDC6DF43384BE2AA8442091F0CC6BE13), since some accounts reject interpretations that collapse the Creator into created forms, while others use language of one reality manifested in many. The link to [interconnectedness; all things involved in all things; chain of being](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2FC11A99D6A34DFD93993D59F31450E8) reflects a related emphasis on the dependence and relation of beings within creation. Interconnection does not itself establish identity between God and the world; that distinction is central to the differing accounts grouped here.
