# the divine emanation; the all-pervading bounty of God

Divine emanation is the outpouring of bounty that sustains creation and spiritual life. Like light, rain, and nourishment, this grace reaches every being according to its capacity, while God remains exalted beyond creation. Revelation renews the heart and makes divine guidance accessible; the gifts that sustain existence reveal the same generosity throughout the world. Recognizing this bounty awakens gratitude and invites a more receptive response to the life and guidance continually bestowed upon us.

The language of outpouring naturally joins [forgiveness, grace, bounty, and mercy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5CF47B6B008F461986A90EF7E6BF176E), which gathers the devotional vocabulary of divine generosity, and [the human soul as a mirror of divine light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F), where the gifts of God are latent and become manifest through spiritual receptivity. The claim that bounty continues without end is explicit in [ceaseless and eternal divine emanation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=68137F318C6144AAAF17C8290F33061E). [The Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853) is connected in accounts of spiritual confirmation and mercy, while [divine attributes within all things](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B996EA76F6EC401A9148E1F0507C179A) treats creation as bearing traces of divine influence. The technical distinction between emanation and disclosure is explored in [emanation versus manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26BFCA2FC4E44906BAAB903F56228AB0), and the stages of outpouring are further specified by [the holy and most holy outpourings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5A1748B3FB14D8EA1B9992E6E0409BF).
