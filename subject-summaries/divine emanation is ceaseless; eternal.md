# divine emanation is ceaseless; eternal

God's creative bounty and guidance never cease. Like sunlight continually streaming from its source, divine grace sustains existence without a first moment or final interruption. Successive revelations and the renewal of creation express this enduring generosity in changing forms. The capacities of its recipients vary, but the source does not run dry. Confidence in that continuity gives spiritual life an open horizon: divine guidance and the possibility of renewal are never exhausted.

The continuity of bounty raises the question of creation’s relation to God, developed in [God’s attributes requiring the existence of objects](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E64D5F25E34797A55642F2919C4317), and is expressed more broadly in [divine emanation as all-pervading bounty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9). It also intersects with [eternality versus origination](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BFA8DCA4C0C24066BAF0F869C88CF522) and [infinity and eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6BB666C0D9224ED7AE3400FC770A3496), where the beginninglessness of creation or divine activity is considered. The changing forms of the world are addressed by [continual renewal of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C213F67401F9445F8429A5F2621563E5), while [emanation versus manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26BFCA2FC4E44906BAAB903F56228AB0) distinguishes ongoing divine radiance from its particular disclosures. Across these subjects, perpetual outpouring does not mean that created forms are fixed; enduring grace meets changing recipients and manifestations.
