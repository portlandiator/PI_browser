# disregarding bodily comfort

Disregarding bodily comfort describes perseverance in service despite cold, hardship, or inconvenience. Writing may continue despite numb fingers; faith calls into question the pursuit of ease and refusal to work. Such resolve can express detachment and devotion, though bodily limits remain real; the topic concerns willingness to endure discomfort in a meaningful cause rather than a rule to neglect health.

This discipline appears in [Abdu’l-Baha’s autobiographical narrative](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710) and relates to [detachment and renunciation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C).
