# importance of prayer

Prayer is a means of communion with God, spiritual remembrance, and alignment of the heart with divine purpose. Obligatory prayer stands alongside fasting, and prayer is both a law and a spiritual state sustained through mystical feeling. Prayer also calls for reflection, study, and good deeds, allowing inward devotion to shape conduct and sustain spiritual life.

Prayer is linked to [contemplation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DA67DEF4ED6E470BA9C5A666A3697E80), [study](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAA956A41DDB45EEBE390F1FD8D20C55), and [reading scripture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DB9913EDE9DAEFE01D3272B608032691). It is a source of [spiritual power](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), follows the [law of obligatory prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B7A38539E06DEF99A0FEFEB648DF4ABB) and the [law of fasting](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0C5DDE6CAB1F402695B2717CF1EC0833), and should be embodied in [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946). Its inward [state and dynamics of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7329FDEC013DEAD42EBB1EE8D4348E33) also merit reflection.
