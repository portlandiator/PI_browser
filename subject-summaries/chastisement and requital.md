# chastisement and requital

Chastisement and requital describe moral consequences that follow from human choices. Spiritual punishment can mean deprivation from divine bounty rather than an externally imposed penalty; reward is associated with right action and faith. These images concern the moral order and the soul’s condition, not simply physical retribution.

Requital concerns [the soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), [prophecy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E), and the symbolic [Manifestation as sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35). Its moral context includes [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), [persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), and [suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A), alongside [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and [the transient world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C). The response is expressed in [calls to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), [reward for belief and right action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70243FE151214450B3A87930FD5DF192), and [proclamation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F9036AE7751AE9E7820A58D34211FEFC), with [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946) and [trust in God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D1CA07BA971AE54EB4BCE0ABB94841E7) as practical orientations. Other links include [Iran’s history](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4B7D2672283AE883B6CC489FBC7D4E7A), [worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85), [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), and [divine justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9170438CF5C0E2D84968B1DE41C36F0F).
