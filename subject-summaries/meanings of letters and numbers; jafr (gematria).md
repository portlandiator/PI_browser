# meanings of letters and numbers; jafr (gematria)

Jafr, or gematria, assigns symbolic or numerical significance to letters and their combinations. In some religious and mystical traditions, letter values are used to explore hidden correspondences, dates, or meanings in scripture. Such readings are interpretive practices and should be distinguished from ordinary linguistic meaning or historically established fact.

The search for symbolic correspondences connects with [mysteries and their discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623), where spiritual meanings are approached through interpretation and insight.
