# pairs in creation; male and female; B and E; agent and patient

Complementary relationships appear throughout accounts of creation: active and receptive principles, masculine and feminine forms, and the letters joined in the creative command “Be.” These pairings express different kinds of relationship and cannot all be understood in the same way. Physical sex does not simply define spiritual reality. Attending to the kind of pairing involved makes it possible to explore cooperation, receptivity, and creative action without confusing bodies with symbols.
