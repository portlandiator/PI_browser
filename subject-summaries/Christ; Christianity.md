# Christ; Christianity

Jesus is revered as a Manifestation of God, and the Gospel belongs to the sacred heritage of humanity. Bahá’í teaching places Christ within progressive revelation: the divine source is one, while each Manifestation brings guidance suited to a new age. Some Gospel images are therefore read symbolically, a difference in interpretation that does not diminish Christ’s station.

[Quotation from or interpretation of the Bible](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1EBA961BF35048008A08B489196F1A6E) addresses how Christian scripture is read, including symbolic meanings. [Progressive revelation; renewal of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A868B8CCFDF44B2890211A0FAFE9F2B9) places Christ within a sequence of divine teachings. [Truth of past religions and sacred scriptures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=193E825FA96F443BBA9ABC0E163E0F11) affirms the enduring spiritual truth of earlier faiths, and [Oneness; unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD) connects their shared foundation. [Historical episodes and the lessons of the past](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EFCE6C7ADF264310AFD8C5A360881252) and [Birth of Christ; purity; virginity of Mary](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B34A98AB07FA4A878A55CB4FA12363CC) ground the topic in history and narrative. [Christian doctrine and practice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3923920E5182EE6A6F0687657820B538) concerns distinctive teachings, while [The Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) relates Christ to divine revelation. [Suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) addresses persecution, and [Definitions of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7) situates interfaith identity. Together these links connect scripture, history, doctrine, and the unity of religions.
