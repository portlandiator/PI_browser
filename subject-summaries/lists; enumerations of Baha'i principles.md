# lists; enumerations of Baha'i principles

Bahá’í principles unite spiritual renewal with the transformation of society. The oneness of humanity stands at their center, joined by the search for truth, equality, education, justice, and peace. Familiar enumerations offer several routes into this interconnected vision; they do not exhaust its teachings. Their purpose is practical: to overcome prejudice, reconcile knowledge and faith, and turn love for humanity into individual conduct and collective institutions.

The [oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives these principles their organizing purpose. [Independent investigation of truth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30A42A15DD044876BA9A0BA8D1D60330) and the [harmony of science and religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=99656E1B1D7344D8930ACA530FDE210B) shape how knowledge is sought, while [religion as reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3C0575BFA942CA95AB98EA453F1294), the [unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), and [religion as a source of love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FFC8B795919D48FD82F6DD702B6A4A3E) explain faith's unifying role. Overcoming [racial prejudice and class distinction](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57), achieving the [equality of men and women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55D2FC30AFA84B35ACE7F2ADC2E8DD0E), extending [universal education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=94AE855C19A34B0D9F7D09FA46079FB3), and moderating [wealth inequality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A8B7BAB865C1EDFF1E086B0B1C1C8B38) translate that vision into social relationships. A [universal language](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30894349063B41739E0027F705C40651) and [supreme tribunal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A34C2D89A870E552CC295C80AFFD6794) provide means toward [universal peace and world unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675). The [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7) require these principles to be lived through [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and [work for the betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80). Their combined spiritual and social purpose helps explain the claimed [greatness and influence of the Cause in this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7).
