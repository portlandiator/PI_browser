# nearness and remoteness

Nearness and remoteness describe spiritual relation to God rather than physical distance. Nearness means recognition, love, and conformity with divine guidance; remoteness describes heedlessness or estrangement from those teachings. The language expresses a change in the soul’s orientation, not movement toward or away from a place.

Spiritual nearness involves [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and is pictured through [fire and light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7112D8A18D3C4C7BB98681C6DE3D94E0), while [detachment and patience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) describe conditions that support it. The experience of closeness relates to [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), and [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B).
