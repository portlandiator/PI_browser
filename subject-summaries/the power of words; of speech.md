# the power of words; of speech

Speech can praise God, awaken the lost, teach spiritual truth, and move hearts—but it can also wound, mislead, or inflame conflict. The tongue’s power therefore calls for wisdom, loving manner, and alignment between utterance and conduct. Eloquent words may open a path, yet deeds must confirm them. Speech directed toward the Cause is likened to a light that can travel beyond its speaker, while melody and song can also give voice to devotion.

The central [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) is distinguished from ordinary [media and publications](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2D71B43F6FD6403083AC1BCB025C195E), and its utterance animates [devotional gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) and [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF). Speech can contribute to [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), but must not become a warrant for [religious violence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C22F29949870425F8FF923F17E47096D); it should answer the [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) through [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) and a [virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). The image of [love as a searching fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D) and the need for [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B) underscore the weight of utterance. Words must match [deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A), relying on [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and directed to [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). They can also convey [prophecy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797), stir [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), embody the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), offer [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and invoke the [Greatest Name](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C01E406DEFE6475ABF35B7FABEA3D636).
