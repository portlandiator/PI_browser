# realms of being; three realms; five realms

Realms of being distinguish the divine reality, revelation, and the created world. A threefold account names God, the world of Command or Primal Will, and creation; other classifications distinguish additional spiritual planes. These different schemes express relationships and degrees of existence, rather than a single map of physical locations. They also draw attention to the limits of perception: a created being encounters reality through the capacities proper to its own station.

The three-realm account is presented through symbols such as paired [material and spiritual existence; two books](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), offering a related way to distinguish dimensions of reality. The [Greatest Name](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C01E406DEFE6475ABF35B7FABEA3D636) is linked to three planes of God, Revelation, and Creation, while [degrees of spirit and the kingdoms of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D03BB2731AA9495289A5738021BFA48E) describes graded forms of life within creation. Together these topics illuminate layered existence from different angles: symbolic, theological, and developmental. The source material also preserves other classifications, including three, five, or innumerable realms, so the connections should not be taken to make every scheme identical.
