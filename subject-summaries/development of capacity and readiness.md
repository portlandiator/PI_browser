# development of capacity and readiness

Development of capacity and readiness allows spiritual qualities to become visible in a person’s life. Persistent endeavor and prayer allow divine bestowals to be manifested; believers are urged to value and protect the bounty they have received. Capacity is neither fixed nor self-generated: it is cultivated through readiness, learning, guidance, and the grace that enables latent potential to flower.

Readiness begins with the [condition of search](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA), is aided by [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), and is awakened by the [Manifestation as the Sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35). The [soul as a mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) can reflect divine attributes, while [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and the proper understanding of [dreams and visions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3F5DFEF17B6E4935A76DA6520A5504AE) help remove veils. Development must account for [differences in human capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790) and be guided by [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B).
