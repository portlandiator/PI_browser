# present and future expansion of arts and sciences; of technology

Knowledge and technology will continue to expand, revealing new properties of nature and enabling arts, sciences, and industry to develop. Bahá’í writings welcome this progress while placing it within a wider account of human education and spiritual advancement. Scientific discovery can enlarge humanity’s abilities, but its purpose and use depend on moral understanding. The future thus holds material and spiritual learning together rather than setting them against each other.

The high station of learning explains why education and scholarship matter to this expansion. Astronomy and the celestial spheres connect with scientific inquiry into the cosmos, while writings on life throughout the universe widen the horizon of discovery. Maturity of mankind relates technological growth to humanity’s collective development. The broader subject of material progress includes these arts and sciences but also education, industry, and social advancement.
