# accounts of dreams and visions

Dreams and visions may involve symbolic or spiritual perception, but a dream report alone does not establish prophetic significance. Distinguishing vision from ordinary dreaming calls for attention to context and interpretation.

Narratives of dreams belong to [stories and anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8), which situate reported experiences within a broader tradition of remembered events.
