# quotation from or interpretation of the Bible

Biblical passages can carry historical, moral, and symbolic meanings. Bahá’í interpretation values the Bible as sacred scripture while often reading prophecy and miracle narratives in spiritual rather than strictly literal terms. Interpretation requires attention to language, context, and the purpose of a passage. A single verse may invite more than one level of meaning, and the reader’s task is to seek the truth it conveys rather than rely only on surface wording.
