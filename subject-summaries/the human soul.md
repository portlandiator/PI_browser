# the human soul

The human soul is an enduring spiritual reality associated with consciousness, identity, and the capacities that make a person human. It is distinguished from the changing body, while remaining connected with embodied life during earthly existence. The soul is also described as capable of growth in understanding and orientation. These claims make the soul central to accounts of human nature, but they do not reduce it to a physical organ or a single mental faculty.

[Relationship of the soul to the body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=52B1C3F2B9574C37BAF0ECCC8D5AB8C1) addresses how the soul appears in embodied human life. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) connects the soul with ethical development and concern for others, while [Soul is the essential reality of man](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D9D74C90C8344208B1361977A8FC544) states its centrality to human identity. [The mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C) focuses on rational inquiry, a capacity associated with the soul but not simply identical to it. [Microcosm and macrocosm](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EA626E1123AE4CA9BDB62193BD55DE62) compares inner human reality with the larger cosmos, and [Mind as intermediary between soul and body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E5D97C507CBE9BC48712BC3E937BE35) considers how mental activity relates the spiritual and bodily dimensions. Together these links distinguish the soul’s identity, its embodied relation, and its expressions through thought and action.
