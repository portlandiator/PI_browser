# rational arguments

Rational arguments use reason and intelligence to examine spiritual questions, test claims, and clarify evidence. Careful thought can dispel difficulties and reveal underlying realities; intelligence is a gift that can pierce material darkness. Reason has a real but bounded role: it assists investigation and supports proofs, yet questions about ultimate reality may exceed the unaided intellect.

Reason relates to the [arts and sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8D8CB2AB7034F7DB07173698FD9C979), the [independent investigation of reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30A42A15DD044876BA9A0BA8D1D60330), and [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218). It also concerns [human distinction from the animal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB), the [limits of the intellect](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=048D1BC9980545A2BF50BCE956D33C2C), and [proofs for God’s existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=41DF1CE89518406AB97E142EE368CE54).
