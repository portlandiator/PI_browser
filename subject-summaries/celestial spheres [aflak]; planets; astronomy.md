# celestial spheres [aflak]; planets; astronomy

Planets move in orbits through vast space, while “celestial spheres” can refer to different cosmological ideas. Older schemes pictured nested heavens; another interpretation takes scriptural spheres as planetary orbits within a greater system. Historical cosmologies and symbolic readings belong to different explanatory settings and should be distinguished from present-day astronomy.

[Infinity and eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6BB666C0D9224ED7AE3400FC770A3496) places the scale of celestial space beside claims about creation’s unbounded duration; its cited passage says creation has neither beginning nor end. [Present and future expansion of arts and sciences; of technology](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB8A8971F2EF480B9179519B95690878) connects astronomy to changing knowledge, with an example anticipating further discoveries in both material and spiritual inquiry. These links help frame the astronomy passages as part of a wider conversation about cosmic scale and learning, while the excerpts themselves acknowledge that understanding of the heavens has developed and remains incomplete.
