# competition vs. cooperation; the struggle for existence

Competition arises where living beings contend under conditions of need, but cooperation reveals their dependence on one another and the advantages of association. Bahá’í discussions question whether conflict alone governs life, especially when biological analogies are extended to human society. Human conduct involves moral choice, so appeals to the struggle for existence cannot settle how people ought to treat one another.
