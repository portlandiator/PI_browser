# prayer for praise and gratitude

Praise recognizes divine greatness; gratitude acknowledges gifts received and the capacity to respond to them. These prayers celebrate guidance, revelation, protection, and the opportunity to serve, while admitting that human thanks can never fully match the generosity being praised. Gratitude is more than a feeling of contentment: it directs the worshipper toward remembrance, obedience, and action. The language of praise can be personal and intimate or voiced among a gathered community.

Praise and gratitude draw on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), acknowledging both the gift of communion and the spiritual capacities it awakens through [divine grace and confirmation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E). Thankfulness can itself encourage [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), while the poetic images of light, ocean, and garden use [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450) to express realities beyond ordinary speech.
