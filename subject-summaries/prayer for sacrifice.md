# prayer for sacrifice

Sacrifice gives up comfort, possessions, or even life for love, loyalty, and a purpose judged greater than oneself. These prayers ask for the willingness to serve at cost, endure hardship, and surrender self-interest; some use stark imagery of blood and martyrdom to express total devotion. Such language is aspirational and devotional, not a command that every believer seek death. Its central concern is fidelity and generous action in the path of faith.

The willingness to sacrifice is cultivated through the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), and the most extreme historical form of sacrifice is remembered in [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977).
