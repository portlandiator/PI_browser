# transmutation of the soul by the divine elixir

The divine Elixir is a metaphor for spiritual power that can transmute character. Human life is crude metal changed into pure gold; love can transform even the meanest person into a heavenly soul. The image conveys deep change rather than surface improvement: a new spiritual quality alters the person’s former nature and way of living.

The Elixir’s transforming force is directly related to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
