# purpose of religion in the world (personal and social)

Religion serves both the inner life and the common good. It cultivates knowledge of God, moral character, love, and fellowship, while guiding people toward human unity, peace, and civilization. Its purpose is not fulfilled by private belief alone: the spiritual life should translate into kindness, mutual support, and work for the welfare of nations and peoples. Freedom from material impulses and destructive conflict is part of this social vision, which calls people of different faiths to cooperate.

Religion’s civic role includes a [moral foundation for civilization](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4F32835BDB1F4C02956E92DB1AE38C5F) and a [source of love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FFC8B795919D48FD82F6DD702B6A4A3E), rooted in [human oneness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) and the Manifestation’s role as [educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=54135CB53EEE434D8B77F1C91F81A26C). It calls for [kindness across communities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80), affirms the [unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), and leads to [service for human progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed in a related [betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80). The goal includes [true liberty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=344830F10FF3E024ED058D859E65EC8D).
