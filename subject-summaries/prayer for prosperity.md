# prayer for prosperity

Prosperity includes material security, livelihood, health, and the means to meet life’s needs, alongside the spiritual riches that give possessions their purpose. Prayers for prosperity ask for relief from poverty, open opportunities, and a good outcome in this life and the next. Their appeals do not reduce wealth to accumulation: many pair sustenance with generosity, service, and concern for families or communities, treating resources as blessings to be used well.

These petitions are part of the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), bringing concrete needs such as livelihood and financial ease into spiritual supplication.
