# work as worship

Work becomes worship when carried out honestly, conscientiously, and for the good of others. A craft or profession is a legitimate path to livelihood and dignity, and it need not compete with spiritual service: daily occupation itself can draw a person nearer to God. Excellence matters, but no honest vocation is inherently lesser than another. Trust, truthfulness, and care give labor its spiritual quality, whether the work is farming, teaching, trade, or service.

Worshipful labor calls for [excellence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5B4432116D03EDAB41C16C80226D731C) and integrity in [business and finance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8EDE6B0C407DE4EE770AB1CEFF0BCA15), with [agriculture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BCBBE51E0C534AA7A0EF24D3B5C199CD) and other crafts contributing to collective well-being. [Wealth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C265864F8406449F9A2770021D2BBB3C) can serve philanthropic ends, while [service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) and [useful occupations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C307803DE6C7E45E17BD19C083DE4620) connect vocation to society. Work is performed with [trust in God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D1CA07BA971AE54EB4BCE0ABB94841E7), while avoiding unhealthy [asceticism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70B67FBB46F446D9835324D644050094); stories and anecdotes offer concrete examples of its practice (see [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8)).
