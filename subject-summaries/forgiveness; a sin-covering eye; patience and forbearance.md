# forgiveness; a sin-covering eye; patience and forbearance

Forgiveness releases resentment and answers injury with patience, mercy, and good will. A sin-covering eye looks past another person’s faults without denying that harm occurred; it restrains reproach and seeks a response that heals rather than humiliates. This discipline can be tested by repeated offense, injustice, or persecution. Divine pardon offers its model, while human forbearance makes compassion visible in daily relations and keeps conflict from hardening into hatred.

Forgiveness shapes the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and sustains [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700), especially under [opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E). It can offer [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) and joins the command to [love enemies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D614A134412E9DA43B77542E47091BD), even through [tests and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4). In this way it counters [conflict](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48768953025E40D1B1F978FF61180A24) and belongs to the [virtuous spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), including the moral character needed for [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) and [fellowship among believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A). Forgiveness must be held together with [justice and mercy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BE8FE142DAF5EE1D0C63BB50722C39AF); anecdotes can make this practice concrete in [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8). Its wider end is [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), grounded in divine [forgiveness, grace, and compassion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5CF47B6B008F461986A90EF7E6BF176E).
