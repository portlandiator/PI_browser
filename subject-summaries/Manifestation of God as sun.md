# Manifestation of God as sun

The Manifestation of God is likened to the sun whose light illuminates minds and hearts, while the divine source remains beyond the reach of creation. The metaphor conveys the Manifestation’s spiritual centrality and the way divine guidance becomes visible in successive cycles. As the sun’s light enables growth and sight, revelation awakens capacities and makes spiritual realities discernible.

The sun image spans spiritual effects such as [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [capacity and readiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8867A9B4875C48BB846B800856E218D7), and [grace and spiritual capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), as well as [light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B68A1DE8700E8697ABFF0E02CF7F845). It is tied to [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), [the unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), [proofs of the Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55581A433C3D45CEB183D21F214B5DB3), and [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5). The metaphor also intersects with [color and light symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5DF1FBB2026CE6E1050C257923FD3853), [rejection and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), [suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A), and [chastisement and requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118). Devotional responses include [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), [lamentation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), [calls to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), and [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F); historical and eschatological dimensions appear in [the greatness of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7), [prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797), and [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417). The relationship to God is further explored in [identity of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB).
