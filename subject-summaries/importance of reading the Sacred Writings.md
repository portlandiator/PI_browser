# importance of reading the Sacred Writings

Reading the Sacred Writings is a source of guidance and an invitation to act. Readers are urged to take up the Holy Books of humanity, hold fast to the Book of God, and recite it as a path toward an exalted station. Reading here is more than information gathering: it should orient conduct and help the reader follow the Word’s spiritual direction.

Reading the Sacred Writings is linked to the [importance of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAA2181CBE0F4BFFAAFD92CC0FCB75B4) and to the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), whose guidance gives scripture its centrality.
