# education and the development of capacity

Education develops what is latent in a person, though it cannot make every learner identical or erase differences in aptitude. Material, intellectual, moral, and spiritual forms of education cultivate different capacities. Effective education draws out potential and helps direct it toward useful and ethical purposes. The distinction between capacity and its development explains why both a person’s starting abilities and the quality of their learning environment matter.

[Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) connects developed capacities with service to others. [Differences in human capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790) recognizes variation among learners, and [Need for an educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A9FB0DFA63874B4E9177CA70ED30CFEA) emphasizes the role of guidance. [Soul; spirit after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313) relates education to spiritual understanding, while [Self-improvement; self-perfection; discipline](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B33E14F5074E73B319D8CE6A17DDFAC) describes the learner’s own effort. Together these links show development as an interaction among individual potential, education, spiritual orientation, and disciplined action.
