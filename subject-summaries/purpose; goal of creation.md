# purpose; goal of creation

Creation is purposeful: human beings are brought into existence to know and worship God, recognize divine revelation, and develop virtues that benefit the world. It is also an expression of divine mercy and bounty, with human life directed toward spiritual fulfillment beyond material survival. Formulations emphasize service to God’s Cause, education, moral character, and recognition of the Manifestation; these aims are related but not identical.

Knowledge and recognition of God form one stated [knowledge; recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218) purpose, while [happiness; joyfulness; joy and sorrow](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26) describes the spiritual joy associated with attaining that end. The recurring contrast of illumination and ignorance links the topic to [light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B68A1DE8700E8697ABFF0E02CF7F845). The [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) connects purpose with the relationship between Creator and human beings, appearing as both the source of creation and a goal of spiritual life. Together these themes describe purpose through knowledge, emotional fulfillment, symbolic contrast, and love.
