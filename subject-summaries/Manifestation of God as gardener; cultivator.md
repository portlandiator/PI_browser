# Manifestation of God as gardener; cultivator

The Manifestation of God as gardener or cultivator represents the education and spiritual development of humanity. Divine teachings prepare the soil of human hearts, cultivate latent capacities, and bring forth virtues, much as a gardener tends plants. The metaphor connects revelation with both individual growth and the formation of communities.

The cultivation image joins [the soul as soil](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D5D98C8A2EDA475EBC48A0E84E2AFE1D) with [education of children](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4), showing how spiritual capacity requires preparation and nurture. Its outcomes appear in [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), while guidance for younger people is related to [prayer for children and youth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6193A5AE5579E4039E4D0589AE21DACA). Encouragement to cultivate and share growth connects with [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) and [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF).
