# begging; mendicancy; idleness and sloth

Begging as a chosen profession and habitual idleness are discouraged; people are urged to earn a living through useful work. This prohibition is paired with a clear obligation on the community to provide for those who cannot work or face dire poverty. The distinction matters: inability, disability, or unsuccessful efforts to find employment are not treated as laziness. The aim is to prevent dependency without abandoning the vulnerable, balancing personal responsibility with organized provision and generosity.
