# Islamic laws and jurisprudence

Qur’anic law regulates marriage, property, and religious duties, and its application depends on interpretation as well as wording. These selections preserve differing judgments about plural and temporary marriage, the treatment of property across religious legal systems, and the role of reason in religious law. Some criticize jurists’ fine distinctions when they displace more consequential questions; they offer particular perspectives, not a single comprehensive account of fiqh.

[Laws of marriage and dowry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FE310A28E520E13E6B27445A16249BA5) is a specific area governed by Islamic jurisprudence. Its example shows how religious law addresses family relations and financial obligations. The connection places marriage rules within the broader system of legal interpretation and practice.
