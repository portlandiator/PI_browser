# House of Justice

The Universal House of Justice is the international governing institution of the Bahá’í Faith, responsible for guiding the community and legislating on matters not explicitly addressed in the sacred texts. It operates through consultation and elected membership, with authority defined by the Covenant. Its role links spiritual principles with the practical administration of a worldwide community, while remaining distinct from the station of the Manifestation of God.

[Holy Days and the Bahá’í calendar](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1FED56E998B24C76A6E68CC456452598) connects the institution to the organization of communal observance. [Shoghi Effendi and the Guardianship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DAE90333756E439B9E3B55F9D3EC62CA) relates the House to the administrative order and succession. [Station, mission, authority of Abdu’l-Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315) and [Power; greatness; centrality of the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA) place it within the Covenant’s framework. [Importance of authoritative interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1512FD2C8B8042FA84CBA88AE4FD2A7C) distinguishes interpretation from legislation, while [Elections](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8E30B938D8A9455F8C4FABC5769EBBF4) describes how members are chosen. [Equality of men and women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55D2FC30AFA84B35ACE7F2ADC2E8DD0E) reflects the institution’s concern with social principles, and [Labor and management](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7494D3CC7B7FE1DCACD73621D5B5AE75) connects it to economic questions. [Infallibility; sinlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=46FD5CCF1C43454DB91FC516E5D3B7F7) clarifies the nature and limits of institutional authority. Together these links show the House in relation to the Covenant, election, interpretation, and community guidance.
