# interpretation of words and passages in scripture

Interpreting words and passages in scripture involves grammar, historical setting, symbolic language, and the spiritual purposes of revelation. A passage’s meaning cannot always be reduced to its surface wording, but interpretation must remain accountable to the text and its context. Reading carefully helps reconcile apparent differences and uncover the principles a passage conveys.

Interpretation draws on [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450) and awareness of [grammatical discrepancies in sacred writings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A1F5E22D1649ECA14B265BA4AE195779). It engages [Qur’anic and Hadith interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=19377C1C79EB4023BAD88C9FEA93B741) and [Biblical interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1EBA961BF35048008A08B489196F1A6E), including [prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) and [the birth of Christ and Mary](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B34A98AB07FA4A878A55CB4FA12363CC). The relation of surface and spiritual sense is treated in [outward and inward meanings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3568B942A8FE439CBD3238E92AE8D80E), while [the resurrection of Christ](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=46F9949EEF0B4004B93D712D5C0A4366) and [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) illustrate how interpretation informs theological and ethical understanding.
