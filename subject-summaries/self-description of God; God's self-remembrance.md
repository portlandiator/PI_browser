# self-description of God; God's self-remembrance

God alone knows and describes Himself without limitation. His remembrance and praise of His own being are eternal, independent of every creature's recognition. Human words can never equal that perfect self-knowledge, yet revelation gives us a language through which to turn toward Him. Prayer therefore begins in humility: we praise God with the names and words He gives us, while acknowledging that only His own praise is fully worthy of Him.

[God's love for His own Essence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=592F9B4AA5B5491F827732B4D4675681) joins self-knowledge with love: the One who knows Himself perfectly also beholds His own beauty. The connection with the [Primal Will and the Word, Remembrance, and Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA) turns from this inaccessible self-knowledge toward the revelation through which creatures recognize their Lord. Remembrance here can name the bearer of revelation as well as the act of remembering. [The self-generation of the Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C470397E9BCC44ECB45F21E1521E18C1) explores the origin of that creative agency without picturing the divine Essence descending into the world. [Continual renewal of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C213F67401F9445F8429A5F2621563E5) extends the discussion to the changing world sustained by divine command. Together, these subjects distinguish God's eternal sufficiency from the ceaseless disclosure of His signs in revelation and creation.
