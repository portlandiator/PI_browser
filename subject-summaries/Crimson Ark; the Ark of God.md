# Crimson Ark; the Ark of God

The Crimson Ark or Ark of God is a symbolic image of divine protection, covenant, and spiritual guidance amid trial. To enter the Ark signifies turning toward the revealed Cause and remaining faithful to its covenant, while the image of an ark evokes safety through turbulent conditions.

The Ark’s protective symbolism connects with [firmness in the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633), where steadfastness is associated with divine assistance and spiritual security.
