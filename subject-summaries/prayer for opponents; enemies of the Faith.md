# prayer for opponents; enemies of the Faith

Prayer for opponents asks that those who deny, slander, or persecute the Faith turn toward truth, repent, and become just and trustworthy. It makes forgiveness and compassion active disciplines: the petitioner seeks a change in the adversary rather than vengeance against them. This does not deny the harm caused by oppression; some prayers plead for protection of the vulnerable even as they ask that wrongdoers awaken, abandon hatred, and seek pardon.

These petitions express the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) by directing supplication toward reconciliation and moral transformation, including for people whose actions have caused real injury.
