# the mind's power of discovery

The rational mind discovers properties and relations that are not immediately visible, bringing hidden realities into human understanding. Its power supports science, arts, invention, and the investigation of nature, while remaining limited to the realities and signs of created things. Discovery expands knowledge without giving the mind command over every domain of existence.

The mind’s capacity to investigate relates to [the power of mind over nature](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9239D6E43FFEECDA3B3AC738EC10194E), while [the higher encompassing the lower](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BF27EF1B5543078EA9B3F7DF6B586C) describes the reach and limits of comprehension. Effective discovery also depends on [focus and concentration](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6D2F37920646424E8CC0A9ECB49AC676) and can support [the arts and sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8D8CB2AB7034F7DB07173698FD9C979). Its social and moral implications connect with [the harmony of science and religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=99656E1B1D7344D8930ACA530FDE210B), [the human soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=65B32249C87F46A6BEA80B4FE5F9C1EE), [human distinction from animals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB), and [proofs for God’s existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=41DF1CE89518406AB97E142EE368CE54); [the activity of the human spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F97B3874575041F7B031336C8EEF15B5) locates this power within spiritual life.
