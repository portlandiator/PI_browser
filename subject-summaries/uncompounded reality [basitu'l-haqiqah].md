# uncompounded reality [basitu'l-haqiqah]

The “uncompounded reality” is a philosophical formula for a reality that is simple, undivided, and free of composition. Some interpretations say that this simple reality encompasses all perfections; others reject conclusions that identify God’s Essence with created things or place multiplicity within it. Certain accounts also distinguish an absolute simplicity belonging only to God from a relative simplicity applied to lesser realities. The formula appears within a debate over unity, creation, and divine transcendence.
