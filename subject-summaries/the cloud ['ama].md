# the cloud ['ama]

The cloud, or ‘ama, is a mystical image associated with the hidden divine realm from which manifestation and creation emerge. It evokes concealment and unknowing rather than a physical cloud, marking a boundary between the inaccessible Essence and the forms through which divine realities become perceptible.

The image is interpreted through [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450) and the [transcendence and unknowability of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4), which together distinguish the hidden divine reality from its symbolic expression.
