# acknowledgment of gift; of monetary contribution

Acknowledging a gift or contribution recognizes the thought, devotion, or practical help behind it. Thanks for received sums, affection for cherished presents or remembrance, and recognition of gifts as signs of sincerity and love give gratitude a personal form. It strengthens service and mutual care while honoring the giver, whether the offering is material or primarily a token of regard.

Gifts help sustain the [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41) and the care of [shrines](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=69863262A551E3763501D5846A98329B); they can embody [service to the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) and support [the poor, sick, and orphaned](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B118E279E521421DA6DE76C3D326B7A6). Material giving intersects with [business and property](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8C309A3E20044543A9A72F36981F7D2A), while particular acknowledgments may be conveyed through [personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2). Gratitude and encouragement appear in [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), and [thankfulness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD4052A3136042C4B86176AB2F552C55), with [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977) underscoring the spiritual commitments that gifts may express. The [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and the [Manifestation speaking with divine authority](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25) form the devotional setting in which such generosity is valued.
