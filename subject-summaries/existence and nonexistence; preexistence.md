# existence and nonexistence; preexistence

Pre-existence can mean existence without a cause or existence without a beginning in time. Absolute, essential pre-existence belongs to God; the world remains contingent and dependent even when its duration is said to have no beginning. Absolute nothingness is also distinguished from a prior or relative state that may be called non-existence. These distinctions allow an eternal creation without identifying creation with God’s own Essence.

This account is related to the [preexistence of the Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAC65ADBDE024CAFA9F155EDDCCA0C1F), whose texts distinguish a Manifestation’s eternal spiritual reality from His human station, which has a beginning. The connection brings the broader distinction between essential and temporal pre-existence into a particular theological case, while retaining the separation between divine Essence, revelation, and created existence.
