# prayer for women

Prayers for women address believers as handmaidens of God and ask for their protection, knowledge, steadfastness, and opportunity to serve the Cause. Many speak in a woman’s own voice; others bless a named woman or a community of women, affirming their spiritual capacity and nearness to God. Their recurring language of guidance and recognition links devotional life to active contribution, while retaining the source texts’ gendered forms of address.

These petitions participate in the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), asking for the [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) needed for spiritual life. They often concern [specific individuals or groups](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343) and ask for their [protection](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B0211B4DCD4EA86BB1FC4BB6ADCD7C3), joining personal devotion with communal care.
