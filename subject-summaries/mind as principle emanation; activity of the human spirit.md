# mind as principle emanation; activity of the human spirit

The human mind is an activity or power of the spirit, not a separate material substance. Through reason and imagination it apprehends realities, forms ideas, and discovers properties in the world. Its operations are among the ways the human spirit becomes active in embodied life.

The mind’s investigative role is developed in [the power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C), while [the soul as the essential reality of man](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D9D74C90C8344208B1361977A8FC544) places mental activity within a wider account of human identity. Together these subjects distinguish the power of thought from the body that serves as its instrument.
