# conference of Badasht

The Conference of Badasht in 1848 brought together Bábí leaders during a decisive period in the movement’s emergence. The gathering marked a public break with aspects of Islamic law and signaled the Bábí community’s distinct religious identity. Tahirih’s unveiling became a powerful symbol of this transition, though the conference involved a wider group and complex deliberations. Badasht stands as a turning point in the history of the Bábí dispensation.
