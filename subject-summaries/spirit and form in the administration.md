# spirit and form in the administration

Bahá’í administration unites institutions and procedures with the spiritual principles they serve. The Administrative Order is intended as a pattern for future society, and its effectiveness depends on consultation, unity, justice, and service. Form without these qualities risks becoming empty machinery; spiritual aspiration without organized structures cannot sustain coordinated community action. The writings call for both institutional development and fidelity to the Faith’s underlying purpose.

[Teaching the Cause; call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) connects administrative work with the community’s spiritual purpose and outward service. This link highlights that administration is a means for carrying out the Faith’s aims, not an end in itself.
