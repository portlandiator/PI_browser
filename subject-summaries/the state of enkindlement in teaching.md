# the state of enkindlement in teaching

A teacher’s inner fire gives warmth and force to the message. Love of God, spiritual renewal, and clear understanding can make the face and words luminous, attracting hearts without coercion. Enkindlement is more than excitement: it grows from self-regeneration, detachment, prayer, and immersion in spiritual teachings. The teacher who has not been inwardly awakened cannot readily awaken another; inward transformation therefore supports outward service and persistent teaching.

Enkindlement is rooted in [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) and can accompany [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). It energizes the [call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), depends on [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), and is related to the broader [spiritual prerequisites of teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67). The metaphor of [love as fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D) and the role of [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) illuminate this ardor.
