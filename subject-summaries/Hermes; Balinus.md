# Hermes; Balinus

Hermes and Balinus appear in traditions that associate ancient wisdom with philosophy, alchemy, and esoteric learning. The names carry layered histories, and later writers sometimes attribute texts or teachings to legendary figures. Their significance is therefore partly historical and partly symbolic. With only two excerpts available, this introduction stays cautious about identity and avoids treating every attribution as established biography.
