# heaven and paradise; heaven and hell

Heaven and paradise signify states of nearness, joy, and divine bounty; hell can signify separation and heedlessness. Eternal Paradise is open to all, while hell signifies turning away from God. These images describe spiritual conditions as well as future destiny: orientation toward divine love brings life and joy, while heedlessness deprives the soul of that bounty.
