# fulfillment of true potential

Fulfillment of true potential is the awakening of spiritual qualities latent within human beings. The Manifestations nurture humanity like trees with living waters, awaken hidden virtues, and reveal capacities that material nature alone cannot develop. Human fulfillment therefore combines inner growth with guidance and education, enabling people to become more fully capable of spiritual recognition and service.

True potential rests on a [universal human capacity for spiritual recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=58CCB959C2C14BCA91979DA42D866EC8), present in every person.
