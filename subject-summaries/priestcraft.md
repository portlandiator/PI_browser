# priestcraft

Priestcraft turns religious authority into control over others or a means of personal advantage. Bahá’í writings criticize clerical domination and the use of ritual or claimed expertise to demand obedience, while distinguishing this from sincere learning and service. Spiritual guidance should awaken understanding and responsibility rather than make people dependent on an intermediary who claims power over their conscience.

The abolition of priestly domination accompanies [the renewal of religion and abandonment of dogmas](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B6DA84931BE1A9E2A91755E047538C), as communities are called to return to spiritual principles rather than entrenched authority.
