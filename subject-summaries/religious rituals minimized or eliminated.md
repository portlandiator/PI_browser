# religious rituals minimized or eliminated

Ritual forms are not the essence of religion. Bahá’í writings criticize practices that elevate ceremony, inherited custom, or priestly authority above spiritual reality, while preserving prayer and worship as meaningful when they express devotion rather than empty repetition. A new Revelation may set aside former symbols and customs as it renews their spiritual purpose; the inward reality matters more than the outward form.
