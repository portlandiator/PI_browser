# race unity; racial issues

Racial unity requires rejecting prejudice and recognizing people of every race as members of one human family. Historical inequality and racial hostility are social conditions to overcome, not divisions grounded in human worth. Unity does not require erasing cultural identity; it calls for justice, friendship, and cooperation across racial lines. The work includes confronting discrimination and building relationships that make the oneness of humanity visible in daily life.

[Unity; oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives the foundational principle that all races belong to one human community. [Prejudice; racial prejudice; class distinction](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57) focuses on discriminatory barriers, and [Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34) connects unity to fair treatment and social well-being. [Unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) clarifies that shared belonging can preserve distinct identities. [Predictions and prophecies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E) places this work within a future-oriented hope for social change. Together these links join racial unity with justice, anti-prejudice, and respect for diversity.
