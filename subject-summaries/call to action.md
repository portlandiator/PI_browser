# call to action

A call to action turns spiritual conviction into purposeful effort. It may urge teaching, service, education, or collective work, and it pairs prayer with concrete steps toward change. The call is not simply urgency for its own sake: it directs people to act with courage, wisdom, and concern for the well-being of others.

The call may respond to [calamities and war](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8CDDA7D99E83E1421D3C3542A49272FA) by mobilizing [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) and guarding [the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42).
