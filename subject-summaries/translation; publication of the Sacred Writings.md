# translation; publication of the Sacred Writings

Translation and publication make Sacred Writings available across linguistic communities. Effort in translation is commended; committees expert in several languages are recommended; rendering Tablets not yet sufficiently translated into German is difficult. Language study and broad publication allow more people to approach the writings, while the work’s complexity calls for careful, competent collaboration rather than casual substitution.

Translation also concerns [the Bayan, its laws, and their abrogation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0C9ECE2BFDC3E344AC68F7EF3A7101F5), where Bahá’u’lláh’s revelation is compared with the earlier text, and [language as a structure of reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E846CC474C3649F5A86C027F93FA44B2), a topic that gives linguistic choices philosophical and theological weight.
