# progress and the continual ascent of material civilization

Humanity’s material civilization advances through education, science, invention, industry, and the growing ability to shape the natural world. Such progress can ease life and expand knowledge, yet it needs moral and spiritual development to serve human well-being. Bahá’í writings describe advancement as continual and call for material achievements to be joined with justice, education, virtue, and service to humanity.

Science and religion are both needed to guide discoveries, while arts and technology give visible form to humanity’s expanding capacities. Service to humanity directs those capacities toward the common good. Praise of Western culture reflects achievements in learning and industry, but progress must also include spiritual development and the growth of the Cause.
