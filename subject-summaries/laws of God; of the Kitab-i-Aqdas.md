# laws of God; of the Kitab-i-Aqdas

The laws of God guide worship, personal conduct, and the ordering of community life. The Kitáb-i-Aqdas presents commandments as part of a path toward safety, love, and peace, while obedience is linked to recognition of the Manifestation. Law is not only outward compliance: humility, trust, and ethical conduct should give it lived meaning. Some provisions address changing social needs, including financial matters entrusted to the House of Justice, so their application must account for wisdom and circumstance.

Divine law joins the [duties of recognition and obedience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF1460687A9249CCAFEDE564FF3B9F06) with [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703) and the aim of attaining God’s [good pleasure](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=034E6E7433614B24B4A7683E29D0C241). Its provisions also extend to [business and property](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8C309A3E20044543A9A72F36981F7D2A), where action should accord with principle ([words and deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A)).
