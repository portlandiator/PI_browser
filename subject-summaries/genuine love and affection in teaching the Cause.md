# genuine love and affection in teaching the Cause

Genuine love and affection make teaching credible before a word is spoken. Warm friendship, patience, and kindness can ease apprehension and create trust; only then may the message be offered gradually and with wisdom. The aim is not to use affection as a tactic, but to embody the spirit one hopes to share. Unity among believers also matters: a community marked by real love and mutual care can invite others to listen and seek truth.
