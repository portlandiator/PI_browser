# conduct in finance and business

Business conduct should combine competence with honesty, fairness, and concern for the people affected by economic decisions. Work and commerce are legitimate parts of human life, yet their value depends on how they are carried out and what ends they serve. Trustworthiness sustains relationships among workers, employers, customers, and communities. Ethical business therefore joins material activity with moral responsibility.

[Status of material wealth; wealth and poverty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C265864F8406449F9A2770021D2BBB3C) considers the purposes and conditions under which wealth can be beneficial. [Work as worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D) connects productive labor with spiritual meaning. [Business; financial; property matters](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8C309A3E20044543A9A72F36981F7D2A) addresses the practical rules of economic activity, while [Honesty; truthfulness; trustworthiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3) supplies an ethical foundation for dealing with others. Together these links relate business practice to wealth, work, legal conduct, and trust.
