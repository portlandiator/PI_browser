# composure; tranquillity; serenity

Composure, tranquility, and serenity are forms of steadiness amid uncertainty and distress. The guidance asks believers to preserve dignity and calm through patience, keep their gaze fixed on God’s horizon, and avoid being disturbed by events. Serenity is not passivity: it supports steadfastness and helps a person meet trials without losing spiritual orientation.

Composure is sustained by [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) and tested through [trials and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4).
