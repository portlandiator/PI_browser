# prayer for nearness to God

Nearness to God is an inward approach marked by love, recognition, humility, and a wish to align one’s will with divine purpose. It is not a physical distance to cross; prayers describe it as turning the heart, loosening attachment to distractions, and becoming receptive to guidance. The longing can bring joy or ache, especially when a person feels remote, but both become occasions to seek deeper communion and a life more closely shaped by faith.

Prayer is a chief means of seeking this closeness, through the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687). The desire to know God connects with [spiritual recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8); turning wholly toward the divine is inseparable from [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and animated by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649). The longing may include [grief and yearning](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), while also expressing the heightened [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) that accompany devotional life.
