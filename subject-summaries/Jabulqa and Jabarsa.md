# Jabulqa and Jabarsa

Jabulqa and Jabarsa are imagined cities in traditions that place the hidden Imam or the Promised One in a distant realm. Bahá’í passages reject them as literal geography and interpret expectations of a concealed or cloud-borne return symbolically. The names also appear in comparisons with Jewish and Christian expectations. Their importance lies in debates over literal and symbolic readings of prophecy, not in verified places.

[Prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) connects Jabulqa and Jabarsa with expectations about future events and spiritual realities. This link points to their use in prophetic imagery while leaving their precise identification open.
