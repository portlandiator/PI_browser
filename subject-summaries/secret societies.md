# secret societies

Bahá’ís are counseled to withdraw from secret societies and associations whose obligations conflict with the Teachings or divide loyalty. The concern is not that every group lacks worthy ideals: even organizations with high principles may be drawn into political divisions, and secrecy can invite suspicions of hidden power or become a tool for factionalism. Religious service calls for an independent, united community whose commitments can be openly understood.
