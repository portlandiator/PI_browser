# promotion of commerce and industry

Commerce and industry can contribute to a country’s prosperity when they are developed alongside agriculture and the welfare of the population. Bahá’í guidance encourages useful economic activity and the expansion of productive capacities, while treating commerce as one part of broader social development. Industry and trade should serve people and support general well-being, rather than become ends detached from the needs of society.

Agriculture is closely related as another productive foundation of prosperity. The two fields are complementary: commerce and industry expand exchange and production, while agriculture sustains communities and provides essential resources.
