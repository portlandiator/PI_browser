# proclamation to Muslims

Proclamation to Muslims calls the people of the Qur’an and Furqan to recognize the promised Truth and judge fairly. The address argues that the Qur’an itself points toward the One promised in an earlier divine Tablet, and that reflection on even a single verse can guide the seeker toward the “Most Great Announcement.” Its appeal combines reverence for scripture with a challenge to heed its promise.
