# spiritual emotions and susceptibilities

Spiritual emotions and susceptibilities include love, reverence, joy, humility, and responsiveness to spiritual influence. These feelings are not treated as ends in themselves; they can deepen prayer, fellowship, service, and transformation when joined with sound judgment and action. The heart’s receptivity is cultivated through spiritual practice and ethical conduct.
