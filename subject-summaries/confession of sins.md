# confession of sins

Repentance is a private turning to God, not a public confession or a request for absolution from another person. Bahá’í guidance rejects the humiliation of disclosing sins before people and contrasts this with the Catholic sacrament of confession, which it places among religious practices Bahá’u’lláh suppressed. The emphasis falls on divine forgiveness and personal accountability before God.
