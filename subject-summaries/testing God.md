# testing God

Testing God means demanding a sign or proof on one’s own terms rather than responding to the evidence already available. Spectacular events cannot compel recognition, especially for those who were not present to witness them; spiritual truth is not a contest devised to satisfy curiosity. Faithful inquiry remains open to reason without prescribing conditions to God.

The limits of signs connect with [miracles and their interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E78A8B4557E4886A5D3BC8F84BFB09E): marvels do not serve as universal proof for people in every age. [The station and authority of ‘Abdu’l-Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315) is another matter of recognition grounded in the Covenant and its documented appointment, not a test invented by individual demand.
