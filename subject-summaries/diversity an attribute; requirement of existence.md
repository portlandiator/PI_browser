# diversity an attribute; requirement of existence

Existence unfolds in countless forms, species, and degrees; no single kind could display the full order and beauty of creation. Differences among parts can contribute to a coordinated whole, like the distinct limbs and organs of a body, while antagonism tears it apart. Variety therefore supports perfection when distinct capacities work together in harmony.

[Perfection; imperfection of nature](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C6BB86F3DB8D4B58AF903A5ABB8B052F) links difference to a coordinated whole: its example says created beings influence one another and that their connections contribute to universal arrangement. [Differences in human capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790) narrows the question to unequal aptitudes, while noting education’s role in developing what a person can do. [Unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) brings variation into a social and cultural frame, where distinct identities can remain within a shared unity. [One universal law; attractive power of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D190D773F56F4F3CBA7AD064E2098780) adds the idea that connection and attraction can bind diverse realities. Together these links show several distinct ways difference matters—in nature, persons, and society—without equating them.
