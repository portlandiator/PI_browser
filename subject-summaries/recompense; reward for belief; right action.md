# recompense; reward for belief; right action

Recompense and reward affirm that actions have spiritual consequences. Each deed has a corresponding recompense, while spiritual perfections gained during life differ from rewards after death. Good action is not futile: it yields fruit, while the deprivation of divine bounty is itself a form of loss. Recompense connects moral responsibility in this life with the soul’s continuing destiny.

Reward is associated with [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) and with [chastisement and requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118).
