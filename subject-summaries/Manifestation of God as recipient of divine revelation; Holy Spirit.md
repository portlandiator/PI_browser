# Manifestation of God as recipient of divine revelation; Holy Spirit

The Manifestation of God receives revelation through the Holy Spirit and gives divine teachings voice in the human world. This station explains the source of the Manifestation’s knowledge and utterance, while preserving the distinction between the divine source and the human recipient. The Holy Spirit is also associated with spiritual confirmation more broadly, but the Manifestation’s role as recipient of revelation is unique.

The nature of spiritual power is treated in [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853), while [the Manifestation as mediator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=83C2E82C31DB4115A3CDF5884D0CB19E) describes how revelation reaches creation. [Special knowledge of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E500F3DB980EEA00468CEB52774C33B7) concerns the knowledge conveyed through this station, and [speaking in the voice of Divinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25) addresses the authority of revealed speech. The image of [the Manifestation as pen](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=72E10246A92341B09224346EC3B1C47C) presents that speech as an instrument of divine expression.
