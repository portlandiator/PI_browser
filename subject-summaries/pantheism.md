# pantheism

Pantheism identifies God with the world or treats creatures as parts of the divine reality. Bahá’í explanations instead maintain a distinction between the unknowable, independent Creator and imperfect creation, with the Manifestations of God as the channels through which divine guidance reaches humanity. This rejects the idea that God becomes limited in the changing forms of the world while still affirming a relation between Creator and creatures.

This question is closely related to [unity of existence (wahdat al-wujud)](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0858B3C3177BE8300A0B98D4DF4EBB10), a philosophical expression whose interpretations differ. The Bahá’í account in these excerpts resists identifying the divine Essence with created things and describes the Manifestations as mediators rather than parts of God.
