# Islamic rituals; holy days; practices; etc.

Islamic fasting begins at dawn and ends at sunset; Ramadan joins abstention with prayer, remembrance, and the joy of breaking the fast. Daily prayer, prostration, Qur’anic recitation, almsgiving, pilgrimage, and observances of sacred days also shape Muslim devotional life. Outward practice is meaningful when it expresses inner recognition, not when reduced to empty repetition.
