# compassion; kindness

Compassion notices suffering and moves toward it with practical tenderness. Kindness comforts the grieving, encourages the discouraged, relieves need, and treats relatives, strangers, and even adversaries with patience. It can extend to animals as well as people. More than a pleasant feeling, compassion becomes service: a healing presence, a generous act, or a word that restores hope. Its reach is universal, while wisdom helps discern how care should be offered.

Compassion is part of the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and the lived [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), guided by [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B). Its concern extends to [the rights and care of animals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=484B9ADCF51B49ABAC2901C126EE1E4E), and grows into [kindness and fellowship across communities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80).
