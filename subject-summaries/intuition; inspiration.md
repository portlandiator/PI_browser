# intuition; inspiration

Intuition and inspiration name forms of inward perception that can disclose spiritual meaning beyond ordinary observation. Sanctified hearing accompanies inner and outer sight; freedom from vanity and worldly desire shapes perception. Inspiration is not an excuse for arbitrary claims; it calls for receptivity to spiritual counsel and careful contemplation of the evidence of revelation.

Inward perception opens onto [mysteries and mystical vision](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623).
