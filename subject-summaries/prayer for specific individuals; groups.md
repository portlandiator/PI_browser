# prayer for specific individuals; groups

Prayer can hold a particular person by name, asking for that individual’s protection, confirmation, comfort, or progress. Many passages address someone directly—spouse, relative, friend, teacher, or believer—while others gather a whole group into the petition. This particularity makes devotion relational: the petitioner asks for another’s good, sometimes in hardship or distance, and entrusts that person’s needs to divine care rather than speaking only about personal concerns.

These petitions rely on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), often paired with [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) or [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) for a person in difficulty. Their concern for others can express [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), trust in [grace and capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), and hopes for [Covenant firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633) and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Images of the [wine of transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102) convey the spiritual renewal sought for loved ones. Some petitions support those who [teach the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), acknowledge the affective bonds of [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and accompany people facing [tests and sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4). Naming carries special weight in [the naming of individuals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B71D6E7CFCC8E05A303BFE9F83981E31). Such petitions may seek another’s [spiritual progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3), reflect [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and include [women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FE19F5905B90E62AF750E4476660CAEC). Concern for others also becomes [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and, in the closely related topic [serving humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80), constructive work for social betterment. The petitioner’s stance is one of [submission](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA); prayers may be voiced in [gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), ask for [Covenant firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C988A6875D85E5BE715D95DDF7075DB4) and [protection](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B0211B4DCD4EA86BB1FC4BB6ADCD7C3), and sustain [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) and [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668). Their outward aim is consistent with the [unity of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D).
