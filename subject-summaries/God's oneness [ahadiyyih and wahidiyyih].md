# God's oneness [ahadiyyih and wahidiyyih]

The terms ahadiyyih and wahidiyyih mark two ways of describing divine oneness. In the gathered texts, ahadiyyih points to undifferentiated oneness beyond distinction, while wahidiyyih names oneness as expressed through names, attributes, and known realities. The distinction allows discussion of multiplicity in creation and divine knowledge without treating multiplicity as division in God. Some excerpts draw on specialized philosophical language, including provisional translations, so their technical formulations should be read in their stated context.

The account of differentiated oneness opens into [the names and attributes of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9AB88215A3AC410692D350AE3BEB3A54), whose unity and manifestation are discussed across the excerpts, and [essential versus actional attributes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B8ED66ABF42C4C94B1950954F56B46F0), which distinguishes qualities belonging to divine essence from those described through action. It also relates to [oneness as unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), where unity concerns the common foundation of revelations, and to [divine unity and its degrees](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0CA85F9D98D4467C8454CE42CA5BB10D), which treats unity as a spiritual and theological path. The technical vocabulary of archetypal realities appears in [eternal archetypes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C9B2EC0371C49F7A3566365EC1285B8), while [the holy and most holy outpourings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5A1748B3FB14D8EA1B9992E6E0409BF) offers a related account of emanation and the stages through which unity is articulated.
