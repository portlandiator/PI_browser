# proclamation to Christians

Proclamation to Christians speaks through Gospel imagery and the language of Christ’s return, presenting a new Cause as the fulfillment of expectation. Bethlehem, the “people of the Spirit,” and the gate of reunion frame a call to recognize the One whose appearance renews spiritual knowledge. The address asks Christians to consider the evidence and not let doubt or inherited assumptions keep them from reunion with God.
