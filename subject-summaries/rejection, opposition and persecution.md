# rejection, opposition and persecution

Rejection and persecution occur when individuals or communities oppose a religious claim through criticism, exclusion, violence, or legal pressure. These responses may test believers and shape the public history of a revelation, but they do not establish whether its claims are true. The topic distinguishes opposition from the spiritual and social responses it provokes.
