# prayer for strength

Strength is the capacity to endure, act, and remain faithful when fear, weakness, opposition, or grief threatens to overwhelm. These prayers ask God to fortify the body and spirit, restore someone who feels fallen, and enable service despite resistance. Strength is not self-sufficiency: the petitioner openly acknowledges dependence on divine power. In several passages its purpose is communal, giving believers courage to teach, protect the law, and support one another.

Strength is sought through the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), which makes dependence on divine aid a source of courage for action and endurance.
