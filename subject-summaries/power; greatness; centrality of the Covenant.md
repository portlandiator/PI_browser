# power; greatness; centrality of the Covenant

The Covenant is the central bond and authority of the Bahá’í Dispensation, preserving unity and giving the community a means to remain faithful to the teachings after the Manifestation’s passing. It supplies strength, protection, and spiritual confirmation, while loyalty to it calls for steadfast conduct and service. Its significance is made clearer by the existence of opposition and Covenant-breaking; firmness is not mere allegiance to a person, but commitment to the divinely established order and its unifying purpose.

The Covenant continues the [primordial Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=32700D0B9FBB474CA2DF2E334114BC5B), and its power is tested by [Covenant-breaking](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42) while calling for [firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633) and service to the [House of Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A801789EBB804ABB86C36BB8CAB50FED). It has historical roots connected with [Judaism and the Torah](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=93585856EDADED995488E210D42FFF2E), and sustains [perseverance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Its force animates the [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), draws on [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), and supports [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF). The related station of [‘Abdu’l‑Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315) connects with [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), the struggle of [light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994), [healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55), [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). The Covenant also engages [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) and must be understood amid [opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E).
