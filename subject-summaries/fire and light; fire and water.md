# fire and light; fire and water

Fire and light, and their contrast with water, are recurring spiritual metaphors. Fire can signify love, purification, or consuming trial; light can signify guidance and knowledge; water can represent life-giving bounty. The images are flexible and their force depends on context, so they should not be reduced to a single fixed meaning.

The elemental imagery draws on [the four elements](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB5EC5C96FE46669738E999DA08B541) and describes movement toward [nearness to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB0490F3653EEB9948DF8F6B543DC7C). Fire as spiritual ardor relates to [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) and [love as fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D), while [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) is associated with illumination. The images also relate to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), where purification and renewal are central.
