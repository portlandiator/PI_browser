# law of Huququ'llah

Ḥuqúqu’lláh, the Right of God, is a religious offering calculated on qualifying wealth under defined rules. The writings describe a threshold and a one-time payment on applicable assets, with household necessities and a residence exempted; in some guidance, annual expenses are deducted from income before calculating the amount due. Payment is an act of worship and spiritual purification, and funds support the needs of the Faith and charitable purposes. The offering must be voluntary and may not be solicited.
