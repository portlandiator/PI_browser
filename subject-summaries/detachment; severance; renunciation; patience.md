# detachment; severance; renunciation; patience

Detachment, severance, renunciation, and patience free the heart from worldly attachments that obstruct the path to God. The counsel is vivid: leave created things behind, turn the heart toward God, and remain joyful in divine remembrance rather than grieving over passing conditions. Detachment is not mere withdrawal; it is surrender of competing desires so that spiritual service, trust, and steadfastness can flourish.

Renunciation faces the [transience of the physical world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C) and the movement from [evanescence to eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70850E6FB45E400FA41773E2557B5CF3); it requires [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [submission to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA), and inner [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). It can bring [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) and [joy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), support [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and open the way to the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417).
