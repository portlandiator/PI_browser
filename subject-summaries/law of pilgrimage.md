# law of pilgrimage

Pilgrimage is an obligation for believers who are able to undertake it, with prescribed destinations and rites. The Kitáb-i-Aqdas identifies the sacred Houses in Baghdad and Shiraz, and later guidance designates Bahjí’s Most Holy Shrine as a place of pilgrimage. Ability and circumstance matter: the duty applies when a person can afford and manage the journey without insurmountable obstacles. Pilgrimage is more than travel; it is a devotional approach to sacred places that carries longing, sacrifice, and remembrance.

Pilgrimage is naturally linked with [shrines and holy places](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=69863262A551E3763501D5846A98329B) and with [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) for those who undertake the journey.
