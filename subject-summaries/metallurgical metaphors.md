# metallurgical metaphors

Metallurgical metaphors describe spiritual change through processes such as refining, testing, and transforming base material into something purer or more valuable. The imagery suggests that difficulty and discipline can disclose latent qualities and strengthen character. It presents transformation as a process requiring effort and heat rather than an instant change.

This imagery connects to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), where hardship and effort are associated with the development of character and capacity.
