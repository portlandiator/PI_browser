# idle fancies; lust and passion

Lust, passion, and idle fancies can draw a person away from reasoned choice and spiritual purpose. Allowing desire to govern the will, while recognizing that human impulses are not themselves a final measure of character: they can be disciplined and redirected. The concern is enslavement to appetite, not the denial of human feeling or affection.
