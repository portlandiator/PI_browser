# sacred remains; burial of the Bab

After the Báb’s execution, his remains were secretly moved and protected for decades before being placed in the Shrine on Mount Carmel. Their long journey reflects the dangers faced by the Bábí community and the devotion of those who safeguarded them. The shrine became a sacred place of pilgrimage and remembrance, joining a history of persecution with a lasting public site of reverence.

[Shrines and Holy places](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=69863262A551E3763501D5846A98329B) gives the broader context for the Shrine of the Báb as a sacred site. The connection links the remains’ difficult history and eventual burial with the devotional and commemorative role of Bahá’í holy places.
