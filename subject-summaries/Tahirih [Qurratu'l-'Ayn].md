# Tahirih [Qurratu'l-'Ayn]

Tahirih, also known as Qurratu’l-‘Ayn, was a prominent Bábí poet, scholar, and advocate whose courage and learning made her one of the most notable women of nineteenth-century Persia. She joined the Bábí movement, took part in the Conference of Badasht, and publicly challenged restrictions on women’s roles. Her life combines religious conviction, intellectual achievement, and personal sacrifice.

[Quddus](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BF08BD4956E5654A7316968B4444DD) was another prominent Bábí leader associated with the Conference of Badasht and the early movement. The connection places Tahirih within the circle of figures who helped articulate the Bábí community’s distinct identity, while her own role as a woman scholar and poet remains singular.
