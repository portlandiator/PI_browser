# youth and pre-ministry of Abdu'l-Baha

‘Abdu’l‑Bahá’s youth unfolded alongside Bahá’u’lláh’s imprisonment and exile. From childhood He shared the family’s displacements and hardships, and He later recalled formative encounters that shaped His understanding of service and devotion. This early period precedes His public ministry, but it already reflects the close bond with Bahá’u’lláh that remained central throughout His life.

The autobiographical narratives of ‘Abdu’l‑Bahá provide first-person recollections that frame these early years; stories and poems preserve particular memories and expressions associated with them. Bahá’u’lláh’s life supplies the larger setting, including the family’s persecution and forced movements. These connections keep the focus on formation and lived experience rather than treating youth as an isolated biographical stage.
