# Buddhism and Hinduism; religions of the East

Buddha and Krishna are revered in Bahá’í interpretation as divine Teachers whose original teachings affirmed one God, love, and spiritual life. Later religious practices are distinguished from those teachings; some excerpts criticize image worship, while Buddhist prophecies of a future Manifestation are read symbolically. Hindu ideas such as Nirvana are used to discuss spiritual perfection, but these interpretations do not encompass every school or community.

[Oneness; unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD) connects these traditions with the idea that religions share a spiritual foundation. [Corruption and renewal; abandoning religious dogmas](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B6DA84931BE1A9E2A91755E047538C) concerns the renewal of religion over time, and [Progressive revelation; renewal of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A868B8CCFDF44B2890211A0FAFE9F2B9) frames successive teachings as suited to changing human capacities. Together these links support comparison across religions while preserving the distinct histories and forms of Buddhism and Hinduism.
