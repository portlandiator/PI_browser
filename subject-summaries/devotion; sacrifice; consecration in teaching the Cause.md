# devotion; sacrifice; consecration in teaching the Cause

Teaching calls for loyalty, devotion, and willingness to give time and comfort in service. Knowledge matters, but commitment is treated as primary: a teacher should dedicate effort wholeheartedly, endure hardship, and place the work above personal ambition. Sacrifice here means consecrating one’s capacities and choices to the Cause; it is not a demand for theatrical gestures. The goal is sustained service, whether at home or through travel, supported by faithfulness and spiritual purpose.

Devotion finds its practical expression in the [call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), where self-giving service carries the message forward.
