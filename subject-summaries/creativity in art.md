# creativity in art

Creativity in art gives form to perception, imagination, and skill. Artistic practice can reveal beauty, communicate ideas, and offer new ways of seeing familiar things. It is a discipline that benefits from both inspiration and study; talent can be cultivated through sustained work. Creative expression contributes to individual development and to the shared cultural life of a community.

[Literature; drama; humanities; the arts](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B596151C89A441D493D3B839D8579C43) places artistic creativity within broader forms of cultural expression, including writing and performance. Its cited example encourages artistic study while recognizing its potential value. The connection shows creativity as both a personal practice and part of a wider humanistic life.
