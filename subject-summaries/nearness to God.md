# nearness to God

Nearness to God is spiritual intimacy, not physical distance: the soul draws nearer through love, knowledge, purity, and turning toward divine guidance. It is called a source of lasting happiness and honor, yet it is not easy or automatic; trials may accompany the effort, and clarity of heart shapes a person’s capacity to receive. The goal is continual progress toward the divine threshold, where devotion becomes a way of life rather than a passing feeling.
