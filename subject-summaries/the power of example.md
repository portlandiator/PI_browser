# the power of example

A life shaped by Bahá’í principles can teach more powerfully than speech. Kindness, hospitality, integrity, and steadfast conduct let others see the Faith embodied, while hypocrisy weakens any spoken appeal. ‘Abdu’l‑Bahá and Bahá’u’lláh serve as examples to follow, and each believer’s behavior can illuminate the teachings for those nearby. Example is therefore both a personal discipline and a quiet form of service that can awaken interest without pressure.

The power of example belongs to the [virtuous spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) and gives visible form to the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10).
