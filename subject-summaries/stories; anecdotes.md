# stories; anecdotes

Stories and anecdotes make spiritual qualities concrete through memorable people and incidents. Episodes about Jonah in the whale, a generous ancestor of Muhammad, and a loyal Black servant illuminate endurance, hospitality, and character; Joseph’s reunion with Jacob becomes an image of spiritual discovery. Other accounts use humor, historical figures, and encounters across religious communities to show how conduct and insight can be taught through narrative.

Narrative joins sacred history and lived example: [Adam and Eve](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6B2378895BCC48AEB5D0E7EA6B70D626), [John the Baptist](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F0EB2ED42499EBA2278AA145E8D4C11A), [Judaism and the Torah](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=93585856EDADED995488E210D42FFF2E), [Qur’an and Hadith interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=19377C1C79EB4023BAD88C9FEA93B741), [the apostles of Christ](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A0576BEEDEFBE1D30BDBD486E037E8EB), [early Islamic history](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7224C128FE3EED201628A9AF51BCF757), and [Muhammad’s family and early figures in Islam](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D847C787D6FEA9EBEB20CB63CD11BDC) supply religious settings and characters. Recollection and testimony appear in [eulogies and reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137), [Abdu’l-Baha’s autobiographical narratives](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710), and [his youth and pre-ministry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B09DF372555FE0EF51DAE5C0119AD51E). Stories also carry moral force through [miracles and their interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E78A8B4557E4886A5D3BC8F84BFB09E), [honesty and trustworthiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), [dreams and visions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B57EEE27265EC0B9258A2C095E271E7), [contentment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8143807DA046EA3DD9AFADD97E824E93), [moderating wealth inequality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A8B7BAB865C1EDFF1E086B0B1C1C8B38), and [kindness to animals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=484B9ADCF51B49ABAC2901C126EE1E4E). Episodes of exile, hardship, and sacrifice connect with [‘Akka](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4FAFE46E3FC7417BAC3D18A3C7D260C2), [tests and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), [Adrianople](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6580A1098A7242F9B29D4916F5B4F414), [Baghdad](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD6AD7D972DCE3881CF23778EE78C347), and [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977); other narratives illuminate [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), [poetry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91840AE122AEC232D80E33023EC88DF), [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), and [the world’s transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C). Their ethical and communal implications meet [personal guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2), [growth of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22EED87D5EEC4CA68E16AB238733762E), [hypocrisy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=51967056B127E9B5141EB6C5BDB5ECAF), [gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), [persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), [biblical interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1EBA961BF35048008A08B489196F1A6E), and [work as worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D), while [humor](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8D9F026E4760E4C06FB3125FB37BD27E) gives some episodes a lighter register.
