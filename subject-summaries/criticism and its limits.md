# criticism and its limits

Criticism can expose errors and improve understanding, but it can also become destructive when it turns into personal attack, gossip, or division. Constructive criticism addresses ideas and conduct with fairness and a desire to help. Its limits matter because relationships and trust are part of the conditions for communal life. The aim is truthful discussion that contributes to learning and improvement while preserving respect for others.
