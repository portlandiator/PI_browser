# evolution; human evolution

Evolution encompasses gradual development through changing forms and capacities. In Bahá’í thought, humanity retains a distinctive spiritual potential through the stages of its physical development. The passage of matter through mineral, plant, and animal kingdoms offers a related account of progression in nature. Spiritual evolution concerns the growth of understanding and virtues; these connected meanings of development require care when relating religious explanations to scientific theories.

[Two aspects of the human soul; the higher and lower natures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5410D4DAE5454E8D81B13EC3E9DA2F23) concerns inner moral orientation, adding a spiritual dimension to human development. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) connects development with efforts toward the betterment of others. [Teleology; goal-directed motion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B0B2077A5A44699B8F756D6A1F5CC7D) frames physical evolution as movement through degrees, while [man is the sum; pinnacle; fruit of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBA5068DF2C24AE6A61E7337FF0B529D) considers humanity’s place in creation. [Divine philosophy and natural philosophy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=43F0402F0B1F4706BC577B7A7A79BFFD) distinguishes spiritual and material civilization, and [Passage of elementary matter through degrees of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=34D3884EDFE04E158B8879D2E51BA67A) follows material forms across kingdoms. Together these links show the breadth of evolution as a topic: bodily development, material progression, and moral or spiritual growth remain connected yet distinct.
