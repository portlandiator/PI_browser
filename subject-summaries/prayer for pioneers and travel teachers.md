# prayer for pioneers and travel teachers

Pioneers and traveling teachers leave familiar homes to share the Bahá’í teachings in distant places. Their journeys may bring loneliness, discomfort, and separation from family, so prayers ask for courage, protection, companionship, and confirmation in service. The work is not framed as travel for its own sake: the aim is to communicate the teachings through clear explanation and loving conduct. Those who go and those who support them both participate in this service.

These petitions draw on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), seeking aid for people who carry the teachings into unfamiliar communities and conditions.
