# universality of the Cause

The Bahá’í Cause is universal in reach and purpose: it seeks to renew, reconcile, and unite the world’s religious traditions while serving humanity as a whole. This universality does not erase the distinct voices of earlier Prophets; it recognizes them as contributors to an unfolding divine revelation. Its practical horizon is a unified human society, built through love, teaching, and social progress. The message is intended for every people and age, not one nation or religious group alone.

Universality rests on the [oneness of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), while the Manifestation possesses [special knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E500F3DB980EEA00468CEB52774C33B7). It calls for [service to human progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed in a related entry on [human betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80), and advances [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700) through the [work of teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF).
