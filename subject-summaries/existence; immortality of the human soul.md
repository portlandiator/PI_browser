# existence; immortality of the human soul

The human soul is a spiritual reality that continues beyond bodily death. Its immortality is linked to its nonmaterial nature and to the persistence of consciousness, love, and spiritual capacities beyond physical life. The body changes and perishes, while the soul’s existence belongs to a different order.

The soul’s afterlife is treated in [the soul and spirit after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), while [the transience of the physical world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C) contrasts bodily life with enduring spiritual reality. [Martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977) relates suffering and sacrifice to the purification and continuing life of the soul.
