# Manifestation of God as mediator

The Manifestation of God mediates between the unknowable divine Essence and the created world. As a channel of revelation, the Manifestation makes divine attributes and guidance accessible without implying that God descends into or becomes creation. The mediator image thus preserves both divine transcendence and the possibility of knowing God through revelation.

This mediating role connects [the Primal Will, Word, and Remembrance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA) with [the relationship of the Manifestation to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB). The metaphor of [the Manifestation as mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D941821F406B4A20B1E60AD343B49717) conveys reflection without identity, while [the Manifestation as pen](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=72E10246A92341B09224346EC3B1C47C) presents revealed expression as an instrument of divine purpose. Reception of revelation is treated in [the Manifestation and the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=108A205E354349B7B235F2EDB2FD6A52), and divine agency in [the Manifestation’s acts as reflection of God’s will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6D7204B00C3E4F1B99909ABBFDE53798). The route from mediation to knowledge is explicit in [the Manifestation as the only route to knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3855920D804CC38601F6D85145019F), and in [the presence of the Manifestation as the presence of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82D2161C8BB4E087647B84F29B41D6D3).
