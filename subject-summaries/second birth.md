# second birth

Second birth is spiritual renewal: a person awakens to divine teachings and gains a new capacity to perceive and live. It is linked with Christ’s advent, humanity’s equality beyond race or borders, and setting aside earthly things so the spirit can grow. This birth is not physical; it marks an inner transformation through which spiritual life begins to animate human capacities.

Second birth involves [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), the relationship between [material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), and [spiritual baptism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B3EA44B110164A69A3BD4B928633F1E3). It opens toward [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), concerns the [life of the soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), and depends on [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853).
