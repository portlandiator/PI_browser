# beauty; grace; aesthetics

Beauty and grace arise from faith and spiritual qualities, not merely outward appearance. Inner beauty outweighs physical attraction, and the divine Beautiful shines as an enduring effulgence that draws hearts. Aesthetic harmony can please the spirit, but its lasting form lies in a radiant character whose loveliness does not fade when circumstances change.

Beauty is associated with [cleanliness and refinement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=930E4F8D8225422881E16A841A80DE02), where harmony and symmetry also give pleasure to heart and spirit.
