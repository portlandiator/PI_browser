# religion as source; stimulus of knowledge and science

Religion can stimulate knowledge by encouraging inquiry into nature, truth, and human possibility. Spiritual insight and scientific learning complement one another: intellectual progress without spiritual development is incomplete, while authentic religion supports education rather than opposing it. Islamic societies offer historical examples of flourishing scholarship and influence beyond their borders. Religious teachings can awaken learning and help civilizational knowledge take root.

The historical relationship between faith and learning is connected to [Muhammad and Islam](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CCF2AE9DD9D04057AF9F40C129F86467), whose intellectual and educational legacy is highlighted.
