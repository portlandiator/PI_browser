# special knowledge of the Manifestation of God

The Manifestations possess knowledge that does not depend on schooling or ordinary learning. Their divinely bestowed insight encompasses spiritual realities and the conditions and needs of humanity. It enables them to answer difficult questions, disclose hidden meanings, and foresee events. This knowledge belongs to their revelatory station and differs in its source from the understanding people gain through study and experience.

The sources tie this knowledge to the [universality of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25D5D8F955CC436AA7A1B20E51D912F3): insight into human and spiritual realities enables a revelation addressed to changing conditions across peoples and eras. The [Perfect Man (insan-i-kamil)](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=77E5DDB689E847A198C9D181A95A2B27) is another connected theme, reflecting the comprehensive perfections attributed to the Manifestation. Knowledge is also linked with [infallibility and sinlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=46FD5CCF1C43454DB91FC516E5D3B7F7), since the authority of revealed utterance is treated as more than acquired opinion. Finally, the connection to the [Manifestation of God as recipient of divine revelation; Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=108A205E354349B7B235F2EDB2FD6A52) points to the source and mode of this knowledge: some accounts describe it as bestowed or inspired, rather than learned through human instruction.
