# emanation vs. manifestation

Emanation and manifestation describe different relations between a source and what proceeds from it. Emanation is likened to speech or rays proceeding from a speaker or sun; manifestation is a reality appearing in another form. This distinction explains how creation may depend on God without containing or becoming the divine Essence, and how the Manifestations disclose divine qualities while remaining distinct from God. Technical terminology varies across translations.

The distinction’s implications for created things are explored in [divine attributes within all things](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B996EA76F6EC401A9148E1F0507C179A) and [divine emanation as all-pervading bounty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9), with [ceaseless divine emanation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=68137F318C6144AAAF17C8290F33061E) emphasizing its continuity. [The station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) concerns the particular revelatory disclosure of divine guidance, while [the holy and most holy outpourings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5A1748B3FB14D8EA1B9992E6E0409BF) describes technical stages of emanation. The relationship between creative agency and its universal intermediary appears in [the Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D9002D8700F4A2D893B0869236D586F). Together these subjects clarify the distinction between divine source, the continuous outflow of bounty, and its perceptible forms.
