# pilgrimage [Hajj] in Islam

The Hajj is the pilgrimage to Mecca required of Muslims who are physically and financially able to undertake it. Its rites connect individual devotion with the global community of believers, bringing pilgrims together in worship and remembrance. The pilgrimage recalls sacred history and expresses equality before God through shared practices. It is one of the central obligations of Islam and a powerful form of communal devotion.
