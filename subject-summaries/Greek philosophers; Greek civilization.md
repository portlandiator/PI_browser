# Greek philosophers; Greek civilization

Greek philosophy raises questions about the relationship between reason, revelation, and the transmission of wisdom across cultures. In Bahá’í accounts of intellectual history, Greek thinkers are associated with Hebrew prophetic traditions, particularly belief in God's oneness and the soul's immortality. The accompanying questions about travel, chronology, and the meaning of translated terms require historical and linguistic care. The central concern is the relationship between spiritual education and philosophical discovery, rather than an isolated history of ideas.
