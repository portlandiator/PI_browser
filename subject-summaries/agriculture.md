# agriculture

Agriculture is a vital foundation of prosperity and a noble science whose development can provide for many people. Bahá’í guidance urges attention to farming in Persia, where it was neglected compared with foreign countries, and treats agricultural skill as a form of worship. Advice also addresses cultivating land directly, horticulture, and postponing irrigation when work is forbidden, joining economic development to daily practice.

[Promotion of commerce and industry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B8AD755DDAEBE3A45D40D6E7D974637B) places agriculture alongside other foundations of economic development. [Work as worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D) relates productive labor to spiritual meaning. Together these links connect farming with broader prosperity and the dignity of work.
