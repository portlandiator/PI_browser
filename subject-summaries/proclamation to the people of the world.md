# proclamation to the people of the world

Proclamation to the peoples of the world announces the appearance of God’s Cause and calls humanity to hear, turn, and respond. “Peoples of the earth,” “dwellers” and “concourse” are summoned; the Most Great Gate has opened, and the Most Great Name brings glad tidings. The address is universal in reach, joining announcement with warnings against heedlessness and appeals for fairness and spiritual attention.

The global call asks for [detachment from worldly attachments](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and echoes [prophecy fulfilled](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797). Its direct summons joins [calls to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE); warnings about refusal relate to [chastisement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118). The proclamation is carried by [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), whose [greatness and influence in this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7) frame its universal claim.
