# prayer for teaching

Teaching the Faith means communicating its message with wisdom, clarity, and loving concern so that others may encounter its teachings for themselves. Prayers for teaching ask for courage, knowledge, eloquence, and divine confirmation; they also recognize the hardships of travel, rejection, and sustained service. The aim is not argument for its own sake, but to awaken hearts, guide willing listeners, and help believers translate their own recognition into generous, responsible action.

These petitions rely on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) and directly support the work of [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), asking for ability and confirmation in that service.
