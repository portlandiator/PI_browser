# Manifestation of God speaking in the voice of Divinity

When a Manifestation of God speaks in the voice of Divinity, the words express divine authority through a human speaker. The language does not mean that the unknowable Essence has become a human body; it describes the Manifestation’s revelatory station and the first-person voice of scripture. This distinction allows divine speech to address humanity directly while maintaining the transcendence of God.

This voice belongs to the revelatory station described in [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), and its relation to the Manifestation’s own identity is clarified by [the relationship of the Manifestation to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB) and [the twofold or threefold station](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=568978583E424499943612635F17DB7F). The authority and efficacy of this speech connect with [the power of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0E46A9D425ED4F89AB54563B472E6EF6) and [the Manifestation as recipient of revelation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=108A205E354349B7B235F2EDB2FD6A52). Related images and experiences appear in [the tree of life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7CC32A1E2224C06A744204F2B89557C), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and [self-concealment of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8BF9EC5D142EF326540C7ED4200001D). The [station of the Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) remains distinct from the outward conditions of [suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A), while inspiring [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668); the language also appears in contexts of [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), [acknowledgment of gifts](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78), and [divine sovereignty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E).
