# dissimulation [taqiyyih]; concealment or denial of Faith

Taqiyyih, or prudent concealment of belief, appears in historical and religious settings shaped by danger and the need for wisdom. Earlier counsel includes concealment of faith, while a distinct Bahá’í administrative principle holds that a believer may not deny being Bahá’í merely to evade a law and then return without consequence. Concealment in a period of persecution and dissimulation to break a known obligation are not interchangeable.
