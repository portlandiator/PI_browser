# Manifestation of God as educator

The Manifestation of God appears as humanity’s universal educator, guiding people toward spiritual maturity while renewing the conditions for collective life. The passages describe education as more than instruction: it cultivates latent capacities, refines character, advances knowledge, and helps people reflect spiritual qualities in action. They also connect this work with material and social ordering, so education embraces the person and the wider human world.

The cited passages place this educational mission within the larger purpose of revelation: the [mission of the Manifestation of God in the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25E17ABCAE6B4195844602A0178DB4E5) is to deliver divine guidance and turn people toward spiritual transformation. That reach explains the adjacent theme of [universal education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=94AE855C19A34B0D9F7D09FA46079FB3), while the image of latent human capacities brought to light connects with the [human soul as mirror; divine light, attributes within](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F). The aim also has a social dimension: the [purpose of religion in the world (personal and social)](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=29AF567C76864D208EA3BD39BC5CC7CF) concerns both inward change and human fellowship. The texts reason that such development requires a source of guidance, linking this topic to the [need for an educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A9FB0DFA63874B4E9177CA70ED30CFEA), and they distinguish that universal role through the [essential distinction of Manifestation of God from others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4657EFC24412422099AA26E26F483158). Examples of prophetic teaching include [Moses](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=39795FE0FF8AE70BF2A0D5EF2720675C), whose education is described in terms of knowledge and collective advancement. Finally, education leads into recognition and response: the [obedience to; authority of the Manifestations of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10C0903A3A7048C193248AAA8629D4D4) concerns the authority attributed to these divine teachers. These connections are supplied as thematic links; several are proposals rather than reviewed relationships.
