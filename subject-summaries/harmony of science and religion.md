# harmony of science and religion

Science and religion are understood as complementary ways of investigating reality, both accountable to truth and reason. Scientific inquiry studies the material world, while religion addresses moral purpose and spiritual realities; conflict arises when either is misunderstood or used to deny evidence. Their harmony supports knowledge, civilization, and service to humanity.

This principle depends on [religion as reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3C0575BFA942CA95AB98EA453F1294) and connects with [Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) and [the progress of material civilization](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=15E8B3AB36AF495684116E6C5150950A). It also bears on [prejudice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57), [the oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), and the [goal of universal peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675). Religion’s moral and social role appears in [religion as a basis of morality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4F32835BDB1F4C02956E92DB1AE38C5F), while [different kinds of knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A11545F6FCC460995486A8C10B9506C) helps distinguish their domains. The practical ends of learning include [serving humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and [the mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C), as well as [serving humanity and working for progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80).
