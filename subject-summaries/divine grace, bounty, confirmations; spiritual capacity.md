# divine grace, bounty, confirmations; spiritual capacity

Divine grace is God’s unearned bounty; confirmations are the spiritual assistance that enables people to act beyond their unaided capacity. These gifts are abundant, yet receptivity matters: effort, discernment, and readiness help latent capacities emerge, but cannot claim to produce grace. Such aid may strengthen teaching, service, and perseverance or bring insight and transformation. Spiritual capacity therefore joins divine initiative with the person’s response, without implying that every outcome is guaranteed.

Grace meets human limitation through the difference between [innate capacities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790), the [readiness developed to receive them](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8867A9B4875C48BB846B800856E218D7), and the reminder that ability is not solely individual achievement (see [disregarding personal capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DF5AB62FC672E0C0F4E413C12B784E5E)). It strengthens [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and [Covenant firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633), sustaining the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). The [Manifestation as the Sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) illuminates what grace makes possible; this can prompt a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), support [devotional gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), and affirm the [station of believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9A8742BB523FE7870795B9A71B370B0A). The [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and the act of [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) convey these gifts, while [prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), [faith and spiritual power](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91D7DB188F74200962A9A9F8446281D), and the [ethical prerequisites of teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) shape their reception. [Unity and fellowship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A), the aid of [angels](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2A6D62207284EAED0144C1FDBFABE5CD), [trust in God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D1CA07BA971AE54EB4BCE0ABB94841E7), and the [Holy Spirit’s use of a teacher](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FEC1A5C44287454E9CA7BCBB4E5EC15C) are also linked with confirmation. Grace calls for [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), invites [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and effects [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) in a spirit of [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), contributing to the [growth of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22EED87D5EEC4CA68E16AB238733762E). Even [search](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA) and teaching’s [confirmations and rewards](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7CD2FD413A26449884C53C73A2482877), as well as the [enkindlement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=023F2C5312FE4C9BA59624CDFA1998E1) needed to teach, are associated with spiritual aid. It may bring [comfort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), deepen [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and flow like [living water](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992), while revealing the [greatness of this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7). The range of [general prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=738F8F7E4890E6B818B5ACE3C3036604) sits beside recognition that grace may counter [weakness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DF5AB62FC672E0C0F4E413C12B784E5E), deepen [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218), and open the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) through [spiritual recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8). It can reorient the believer toward [self-surrender and eternal life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70850E6FB45E400FA41773E2557B5CF3), foster [others’ spiritual progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3), and sustain those facing [persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E). The response is [gratitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD4052A3136042C4B86176AB2F552C55), including prayer for [individuals and groups](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343), and trust in the [Concourse on High](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C2659CEFAA5E6114DDA8A2D71F13AF2). Grace also enables [education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) and [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), expressed in a related topic on [human betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80), alongside [praise and thanksgiving](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A507E7D3A781E22F35AB894700017D84). Its significance intersects the [Covenant’s power](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA), [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), [mystical insight](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623), and accounts of ‘Abdu’l-Bahá’s [Western travels](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6E786EE894E9EA130794C22592689E71). [Symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450) gives language to these gifts, while [purity and sincerity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098), [counsel](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B), and the [power of speech](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A) can direct them toward [teaching’s influence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0932600F9AFF44C2B17210548E889F44). Ultimately, confirmations remain gifts of divine [mercy and bounty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5CF47B6B008F461986A90EF7E6BF176E).
