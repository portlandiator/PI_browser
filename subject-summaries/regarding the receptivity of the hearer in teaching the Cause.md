# regarding the receptivity of the hearer in teaching the Cause

Good teaching meets the hearer where they are. A teacher should consider a seeker’s readiness, knowledge, and immediate concerns, choosing words and proofs the person can receive without overwhelm. This calls for scholarship as well as tact: one must understand the teachings deeply enough to explain them in relevant ways, and reveal difficult truths gradually when needed. Timing is part of wisdom; offering insight before someone is prepared can close the very door that careful conversation might open.

Sensitivity to the listener forms part of the [spiritual prerequisites of teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), joining knowledge and good character with patience and discernment.
