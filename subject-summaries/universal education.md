# universal education

Education should be available to everyone, because learning develops human capacity and supports the well-being of society. Universal education includes intellectual knowledge, practical skills, and moral and spiritual formation. It helps people participate more fully in their communities and equips humanity to address shared problems. Access matters: a society that leaves people without education limits both individual potential and collective progress.

[Manifestation of God as educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=54135CB53EEE434D8B77F1C91F81A26C) describes the spiritual purpose of educating humanity. [Essential distinction of Manifestation of God from others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4657EFC24412422099AA26E26F483158) clarifies the station from which that education is understood. [Need for an educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A9FB0DFA63874B4E9177CA70ED30CFEA) addresses why guidance is necessary, while [Call to acquire knowledge; sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=157B1606343947178806DFE21D30B32B) emphasizes learning as a duty. [Educational curricula](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DA23248649CEE53DD015F93A520C3B49) concerns how education is organized, and [Education of children; moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) focuses on young learners. [Lists; enumerations of Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) places universal education among wider social teachings. Together these links connect broad access with purpose, guidance, content, and implementation.
