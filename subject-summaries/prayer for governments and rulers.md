# prayer for governments and rulers

Rulers and governments exercise power that can protect a population or expose it to injustice. Prayer for them asks that leaders gain wisdom, act with fairness, preserve public welfare, and safeguard the vulnerable. The petitions range across historical monarchs and contemporary offices, so their enduring concern is ethical responsibility in governance rather than endorsement of any one political arrangement. Some ask that leaders turn toward spiritual guidance as well as fulfill their public duties.

These petitions make [prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) a means of seeking ethical conduct from those who govern. Their recurring appeal to justice connects them to the broader account of [justice and fairness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34), which treats equity as a condition for the welfare of society.
