# infallibility; sinlessness ['ismat]

‘Ismat is the infallibility or sinlessness associated with the Manifestation of God. It is distinguished from forms of conferred infallibility attributed to other institutions or persons and from ordinary freedom from error. This station concerns the authority and reliability of revelation, while allowing different roles to have distinct scopes, knowledge, and responsibilities.

This distinction is clarified by [the House of Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A801789EBB804ABB86C36BB8CAB50FED), whose infallibility is described as conferred and not omniscient, and by [the authority of ‘Abdu’l-Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315). [Obedience to the Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10C0903A3A7048C193248AAA8629D4D4) follows from their unique revelatory station, while [the Perfect Man](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=77E5DDB689E847A198C9D181A95A2B27) offers a related but broader language of spiritual completeness. The source and limits of special revelation are further treated in [special knowledge of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E500F3DB980EEA00468CEB52774C33B7). Together these subjects distinguish the Manifestation’s innate infallibility from other forms of authority and human perfection.
