# day of Resurrection; Judgement

Resurrection and judgment need not refer only to a physical event at the end of time. In Bahá’í interpretation, resurrection can mean the renewal of religion and the awakening of people to a new Revelation; judgment can mark their response to that truth. These spiritual meanings retain the urgency of the imagery without reducing it to bodily events.

[Prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) connects resurrection and judgment imagery with the interpretation of religious expectation. The link highlights how prophetic language may point to spiritual renewal and recognition rather than only to literal future events.
