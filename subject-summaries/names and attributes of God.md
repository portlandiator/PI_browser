# names and attributes of God

God's names—such as the All-Knowing, the Merciful, and the Almighty—open paths to recognizing His perfections. They guide prayer and illuminate the qualities revealed throughout creation, most fully in His Manifestations. Like mirrors reflecting sunlight, these names disclose divine glory without containing God's Essence. They also call for a response in human life: mercy, generosity, and care for others give practical expression to the qualities we praise.

[Transcendence and unknowability](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) mark the limit of every divine name, while [knowledge of attributes as knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A7BDCD83AE14B19B4D78332D456B1D3) explains their value as paths of recognition. [Essential and actional attributes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B8ED66ABF42C4C94B1950954F56B46F0) distinguish the inaccessible divine reality from its activity and effects in creation. [God's oneness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6B2E2534F05E41069B3E1A18F5B552D4) asks how many names can point toward one undivided source without introducing divisions into God. The subject of [attributes requiring objects](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E64D5F25E34797A55642F2919C4317) explores the relationships implied by names such as Creator and Provider. [Mercy, forgiveness, and bounty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5CF47B6B008F461986A90EF7E6BF176E) give these names a devotional and ethical meaning; [power and sovereignty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E) express their creative authority. Together, the seven connections move from the language of praise to its meaning, its limits, and its expression in the world.
