# meaning; importance of dreams and visions

Dreams range from ordinary products of memory and imagination to experiences of spiritual insight. A vivid dream is not necessarily a prophecy, and a true vision need not occur during sleep. Discernment requires attention to an experience's origin, meaning, and effects, without treating intensity alone as proof of truth. Inner experience can enlarge understanding while still calling for humility about what it reveals.

The evidentiary role of visions relates to [proofs for the soul’s immortality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9776E3373AB6EADEF00584AE6CE4505D), while [capacity and readiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8867A9B4875C48BB846B800856E218D7) concerns the spiritual conditions associated with perception. These links frame dreams as experiences requiring discernment rather than automatic proof.
