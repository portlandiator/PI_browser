# the soul as soil; cultivation of the soul

The soul as soil is an image of human receptivity: divine teaching and mercy can cultivate the heart until it yields knowledge, unity, and good deeds. Spiritual educators are gardeners, and the heart is fertile ground that must be tilled. Soil left uncultivated grows weeds; readiness, guidance, and effort prepare it to bear fruit and sustain life.

The image joins the Manifestation as [gardener](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DD46AC953147458BAB7F06770FD865D5) with [fruitful results](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48DEE65D2ED14EB091B457DDD6377B17) and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). The [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) cultivate the heart; [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8) further ready the soil.
