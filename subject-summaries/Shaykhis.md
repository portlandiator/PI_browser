# Shaykhis

The Shaykhi movement, founded around the teachings of Shaykh Ahmad al-Ahsa’i and Siyyid Kazim Rashti, anticipated the advent of a promised religious figure. Its followers studied Islamic traditions and developed distinctive interpretations of messianic expectation. The Báb emerged from this milieu, and some Shaykhis became among his earliest followers. The movement is best understood through its specific teachers and doctrines rather than as a single undifferentiated sect.
