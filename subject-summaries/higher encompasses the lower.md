# higher encompasses the lower

A higher reality may include and sustain what lies below it without being limited to the lower level. This principle is used to explain why a greater order can contain the conditions of a lesser one, while the lesser cannot account for the greater in full. The relation is one of encompassing and dependence, rather than simple physical containment. It offers a way to describe different levels of reality and their unequal powers of comprehension.

[The mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C) brings the principle into human inquiry: reason can discover properties of the natural world, though its range has limits. The relation between encompassing and being encompassed helps clarify how knowledge can be real without becoming exhaustive. The connection keeps the focus on degrees of understanding and the difference between knowing effects and comprehending a whole reality.
