# prayer for martyrs

Prayers for martyrs honor those who died for their faith and ask that they receive mercy, forgiveness, and nearness to God. They also address the living: families are remembered in grief, and communities seek courage and constancy from the example of sacrifice. Grave visitation and remembrance give these prayers a place in communal memory. The language can celebrate steadfast witness while still mourning the violence and loss surrounding a martyr’s death.

These petitions use the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) to remember the deceased and console the living, joining devotional remembrance to hope for divine mercy.
