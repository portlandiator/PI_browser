# wisdom [hikmat]

Wisdom, or hikmat, applies spiritual understanding with tact, discernment, and attention to consequences. It can mean choosing the right moment or manner for action, serving the Cause thoughtfully, and speaking in a way that reaches hearts. Wisdom therefore joins knowledge to character: good judgment protects unity, helps teaching succeed, and prevents zeal from becoming careless or counterproductive.

Wisdom prepares [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), gives substance to [exhortations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B), and can animate the [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41). It is needed amid [opposition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) and expressed through [courtesy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF1F45263A44466C9655EE1714DE1B33) and [teaching methods](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=369DAA81C51F4587B8F070614833A216). Wise counsel supports [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), [unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), and the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), while requiring [kindness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=210D276A06CCE27ECA75FDC0B8F5205C), [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775).
