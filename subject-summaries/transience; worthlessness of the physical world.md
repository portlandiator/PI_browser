# transience; worthlessness of the physical world

The physical world is transient, so attachment to it cannot provide a lasting home for the heart. Numbered breaths and shifting desires stand against the soul’s origin in holiness and longing for reunion, warning that worldly delusion ends in loss. Awareness of mortality is meant to redirect life toward God and eternal realities, not to deny the value of action in this world.

Awareness of transience leads toward [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), trust in the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), and hope for the [life of the soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313). It exposes [greed and attachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7D483E56354A41E7BBF405202EC78AAE), [heedlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9EC159259B874FABB63915C45183582D), and [worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85), while placing earthly reward and loss in the light of [requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118). This perspective can prompt [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0), and [tests and trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), and comfort those facing [suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) or [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977). It relates to [eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B73760379052E56FB65E6087189A0312), the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), [immortality of the soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C691C4FF81064ADC8EE804AF197F35A9), and [prayer for the departed](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6F48F0918CDBEBB76DD545292C255B68). [Stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8), [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450), and [wine as transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102) give images for the contrast; [the Word’s own standard](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=476749D229DA473E89464763E1955D51), [trust in God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D1CA07BA971AE54EB4BCE0ABB94841E7), and [submission](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA) guide response. [Joy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) show that detachment need not become despair. The temporal world also carries [historical lessons](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EFCE6C7ADF264310AFD8C5A360881252), [wealth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C265864F8406449F9A2770021D2BBB3C), [persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), and [tests](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), all interpreted in relation to the [eternal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B73760379052E56FB65E6087189A0312). This reflection also invites [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
