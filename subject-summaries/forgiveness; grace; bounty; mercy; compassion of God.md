# forgiveness; grace; bounty; mercy; compassion of God

God's mercy sustains creation and opens the way to forgiveness and renewal. His bounty reaches beyond human deserving, providing life, guidance, and help before they are requested. Revelation is itself a gift, drawing people toward God and teaching them what benefits their souls. No one should despair because of past wrongdoing: sincere, enduring repentance turns the heart toward pardon. Receiving such generosity calls for gratitude, changed conduct, and compassion toward others, making divine forgiveness a source of hope and moral renewal.

Mercy connects the [names and attributes of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9AB88215A3AC410692D350AE3BEB3A54) with the experience of being sustained, forgiven, and guided. [The divine emanation; the all-pervading bounty of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9) extends this theme across creation, while [divine grace, bounty, confirmations; spiritual capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) explores the assistance that strengthens human response. [Living waters; water of life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992) gives that renewal an image: revelation refreshes and quickens the soul. [Prayer for forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0137285CF3DEE3ABF3193E5E857AE7DB) expresses dependence on mercy for oneself and others; [attaining good pleasure of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=034E6E7433614B24B4A7683E29D0C241) directs that prayer toward a life acceptable to Him. [Consolation and comfort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) and [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) bring assurance that weakness, sorrow, and past failure need not extinguish hope. The response becomes practical through [thankfulness; gratitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD4052A3136042C4B86176AB2F552C55), expressed in loving action, and [forgiveness; a sin-covering eye; patience and forbearance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), which carries the example of divine compassion into relationships. Together these connections join receiving mercy with extending kindness and making a fresh beginning.
