# photographs and recordings

Photographs can preserve remembrance, but they must not become objects of worship. Reverence for a spiritual figure differs from extraordinary homage to an image. ‘Abdu’l-Bahá's reluctance to be photographed or to have His portrait widely reproduced reflects this distinction, alongside concern about unsuitable publicity and misleading representations. Care with sacred images therefore involves both respectful use and accurate identification, so that a picture supports remembrance without taking the place of spiritual recognition.
