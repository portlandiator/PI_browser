# wealth inequality and its moderation

Extreme inequality undermines social well-being when a few possess great wealth while many lack the means to live. Bahá’í teachings call for economic arrangements that reduce extremes and encourage generosity, cooperation, and justice. The aim is not simply to redistribute by force, but to create conditions where resources and opportunities support the whole community. Moderation in wealth is therefore a social and moral concern, not only a private virtue.

[Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34) frames economic arrangements through fairness and the welfare of all. [The economic problem and its solution; voluntary giving](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FC3291E97A2B4A1FB2A2B664AB345C85) distinguishes freely chosen sharing from coercive equalization. [Assistance to the poor and lowly](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B118E279E521421DA6DE76C3D326B7A6) emphasizes concrete care, while [Critique of Western values; culture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC28DBB6808BE947DE1EE25ECB14F544) questions social systems that prize exclusiveness. [Stories; anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) offers narrative examples, and [Lists; enumerations of Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) places economic teachings among wider principles. [Education of children; moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) connects inequality with the conditions shaping young lives. [Labor and management; labor relations; profit sharing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7494D3CC7B7FE1DCACD73621D5B5AE75) addresses fairer economic relations. Together these links connect wealth moderation with justice, voluntary action, assistance, education, and economic structure.
