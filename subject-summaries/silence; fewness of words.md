# silence; fewness of words

Silence makes room for thought, contemplation, and steadiness. A measured tongue can protect a person from careless speech, preserve inward attention, and keep a message concise and beneficial. Yet silence is not an absolute ideal: remembrance and proclamation have their place, and the believer may be called to speak. The discipline lies in knowing when quietude guards understanding and when words serve truth; restraint can become a fortress against distraction and error.
