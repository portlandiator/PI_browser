# the truth of past religions and sacred scriptures

Earlier religions and scriptures carry genuine spiritual truth and have educated humanity across successive ages. Their Prophets belong to a connected history of divine guidance; later revelation can renew and expand understanding without requiring contempt for what came before. Shared teachings such as love and unity persist, while social laws and forms may change with human needs. Honoring past scripture thus means recognizing its spiritual value and reading it within a continuing story of revelation.

The truth of earlier faiths is exemplified through [Christ and Christianity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FA444A710AD4FADBE7A093449A77252), alongside the [enduring and changing aspects of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B4B220998C46ED868D97D28B10F856). Renewal can correct [corrupted dogma](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B6DA84931BE1A9E2A91755E047538C) and forms part of [progressive revelation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A868B8CCFDF44B2890211A0FAFE9F2B9), in which [prophecy finds fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797). It also challenges [blind imitation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E0F43FD317AEC54131AD435E2317B05) while affirming the [unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), shaping the [qualities of Bahá’ís](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7) and religion’s role in fostering [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FFC8B795919D48FD82F6DD702B6A4A3E).
