# spiritual communication; connections of the heart

Love can sustain intimacy across distance, bodily separation, and even death. Remembrance, prayer, and written words carry that bond, while spiritual nearness is understood to extend beyond ordinary speech. The idea does not authorize every unusual experience: spiritual connection is distinguished from occult practices. Its emphasis is enduring affection and presence of heart, rather than a supernatural technique.

This kind of bond is part of the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and can bridge [East and West](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDED88E95778E61DFBBBA823FAF0AE38), reflecting interaction between the [material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61). It extends to the [soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), often through prayer and [eulogies and reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137), and is felt in [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B). Its animating force is [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), while the image of the soul as a [mirror reflecting divine light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) and the distinction between [material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B) offer related ways to understand inner life. The collection also places clear boundaries around [occult and psychic claims](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=666ED6B326A8EDED8D4E3FF383F97184). Bonds of remembrance and devotion can find communal expression in [spiritual gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483).
