# characteristics and conduct of true believers

True believers are recognized by the virtues they practice, not by affiliation alone. They are urged to shine like the sun, cultivate heavenly character, and let the luminosity of faith become visible in conduct. Their example matters in community life and in relations with neighbors, strangers, and opponents; spiritual conviction appears in the quality of everyday dealings and deeds.

Believerly conduct serves [cooperation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5CA8DC45DCC48AABFC267FC9214C6E2), [the golden rule](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9E2097943E7F4A8AB3F8A1B03D1E644D), [kindness to all](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80), [hospitality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02AB9A3E1C13E61D5B0AE0F70778680D), and [fairmindedness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82455B2C114D470186777AA5E2D9F29B). It includes [heartfelt communication](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E), [love of enemies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D614A134412E9DA43B77542E47091BD), [forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), [compassion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=210D276A06CCE27ECA75FDC0B8F5205C), and [generosity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=531C2217F0C1EC4A8750BEC3A0B284AA); it is sustained by the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946), and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). The community’s [fellowship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A) and [marriage](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6615BDF38C0B46B9960CFA282715258D) provide settings for virtue, while [animal kindness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=484B9ADCF51B49ABAC2901C126EE1E4E) extends care beyond human relations. Inner virtues include [love’s pathways](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=552E8D5180B04AAB91BE86621F37CD9B), [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), [contentment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8143807DA046EA3DD9AFADD97E824E93), [honesty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703). The believer serves [humanity’s progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and practices [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7), not mere identity; deeds should outweigh [words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A), and conduct becomes an [example](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DB53ABF4937F457EBF7432583E55A26C). Virtue also shines through a [radiant countenance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F501A1D6AA54C2E8306ED06B66657A5), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and [reminiscence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137), is nurtured by [children’s moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4), and may be encouraged through [exhortation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B) and speech’s [the power of words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A). [Worldly detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85) helps sustain this life amid trials and [betterment of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80).
