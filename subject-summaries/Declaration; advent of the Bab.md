# Declaration; advent of the Bab

The Declaration of the Báb in 1844 marks the beginning of his public mission as the Gate of God and herald of a new religious era. The event is remembered as a moment of spiritual recognition and the start of a movement that would spread rapidly despite persecution. Its meaning lies not only in a date but in the claim that a new revelation had appeared and called people to prepare for what followed.
