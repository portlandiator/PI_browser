# prayer for gatherings; meetings; assemblies

A devotional gathering becomes more than a meeting when people assemble in humility, harmony, and a shared purpose of worship or service. Prayer for such occasions asks that the group’s deliberations be illumined, its members strengthened, and its fellowship made a source of unity and benefit. The images of a garden, lamp, and gathering of lights express how collective devotion can nurture individuals while radiating beyond the room into the wider community.

These gatherings draw on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), with participants seeking divine guidance together and hoping that their fellowship will strengthen both devotion and service.
