# limits of language

Language has limits because words cannot fully contain the realities they name, especially spiritual and divine realities. Human speech can point toward truth and communicate its signs, but it does not encompass the essence of what is described. This limitation calls for humility in theological language and attention to what words can and cannot establish.

The limits of speech follow from [the transcendence and unknowability of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4), whose Essence exceeds every human description. Language may indicate divine attributes without enclosing the reality to which it points.
