# definitions of a Baha'i; qualities of a Baha'i

A Bahá’í identity is expressed through love for humanity, service, and commitment to the teachings, not merely through a label. Belief is personal and should not be coerced, while community life requires shared understanding of essential principles. People may enter the Faith with differing levels of knowledge and grow through study and practice; others may benefit from Bahá’í wisdom without becoming members. The central measure is a life of action, unity, and respect for human diversity.

Bahá’í identity is situated among [Christianity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FA444A710AD4FADBE7A093449A77252) and the [unity of religions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), expressed through [kindness across communities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80) and the [conduct of believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10). The community seeks [unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) and renewal beyond [corrupt dogma](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B6DA84931BE1A9E2A91755E047538C), while honoring the [truth of past scriptures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=193E825FA96F443BBA9ABC0E163E0F11). Identity requires [deeds with words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A) and a [virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), leading to [good actions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946) and resistance to [prejudice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57). It also relates to [religion as reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3C0575BFA942CA95AB98EA453F1294), [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and the articulation of [Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7).
