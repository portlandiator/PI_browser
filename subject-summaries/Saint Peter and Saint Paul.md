# Saint Peter and Saint Paul

Peter and Paul were influential figures in the early Christian community, remembered for distinct roles in the spread and interpretation of Jesus’ teachings. Peter’s denial and subsequent return, and Paul’s transformation and missionary work, illustrate different paths of discipleship. Their histories helped shape Christian tradition and invite reflection on failure, forgiveness, and service after a change of heart.

[Judas](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DC052035304CEABF417ECFC688C5A234) offers a contrasting account of a disciple’s response to Christ. [Material and spiritual existence; two books](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B) connects the apostolic mission with the wider spiritual and material dimensions of religious life. [Teaching the Cause; call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) relates the apostles’ missionary work to the broader vocation of sharing religious teachings. Together these links connect discipleship with choice, spiritual purpose, and teaching.
