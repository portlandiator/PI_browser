# Shoghi Effendi and the Guardianship

Shoghi Effendi, the Guardian of the Bahá’í Faith, guided the community after the passing of ‘Abdu’l-Bahá and helped establish its administrative institutions. The Guardianship provided authoritative interpretation and direction within the Covenant. Its role is distinct from the legislative responsibilities of the Universal House of Justice. Understanding the relationship between these institutions helps explain the continuity and structure of Bahá’í administration.

[House of Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A801789EBB804ABB86C36BB8CAB50FED) is the complementary institution whose legislative role is distinct from the Guardian’s interpretive and guiding function. Their relationship forms part of the administrative order established by the Covenant, with each institution having a specific authority and responsibility.
