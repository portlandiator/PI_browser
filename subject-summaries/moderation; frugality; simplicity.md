# moderation; frugality; simplicity

Moderation, frugality, and simplicity favor balance over excess in material life. Moderated freedom protects human welfare, checks forces that fragment society, and calls for unity in the face of destructive conflict. Simplicity is not deprivation for its own sake: it helps preserve health, maintain proportion, and loosen attachment so that resources and energy can serve lasting purposes.

Moderation is grounded in the [principle of balance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CD9C3EBD7DB44EFF9DDFF8598C7369FF), joined to [health and healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55), and supported by [detachment from worldly attachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C).
