# spiritual assemblies; administrative matters

Spiritual Assemblies administer Bahá’í community affairs through consultation, service, and care for the community’s members. Local assemblies form a basic unit of administration, while national and international institutions coordinate wider responsibilities. Their authority is exercised within the Covenant and the teachings of the Faith. Administrative work is meant to support unity, participation, and the spiritual and practical needs of the community.

[Hands of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A2D5016F2A87467E87D0D1509478C894) represents a related but distinct institution focused on protection and propagation. [Elections](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8E30B938D8A9455F8C4FABC5769EBBF4) concerns the process by which assemblies are formed, and [Praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) reflects the supportive character expected of their service. [Teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) connects administration with the community’s outward work. Together these links show assemblies in relation to election, institutional service, and the life of the community.
