# inmost heart [fu'ad]

The inmost heart, fu’ad, is a person’s deepest spiritual reality and the place where divine revelation may be reflected. The heart is a high station within created existence and a tablet on which words of light can be inscribed. Understanding the heart therefore involves more than emotion: it concerns the inner capacity to mirror divine meaning and perceive the significance of reality.

The inmost heart is linked to [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) and to [degrees of faith and certitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C306A880A1774D0FBF02956CCEAF45FF).
