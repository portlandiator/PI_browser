# prayer for parents

Parents are remembered with gratitude, affection, and a sense of duty that can continue across generations. Prayers ask for their health, spiritual well-being, forgiveness, and nearness to God; they also ask children to serve their parents with kindness and become a source of joy to them. Some petitions remember a deceased mother or father, while others concern living family life, bringing filial care and spiritual remembrance together.

These appeals are expressions of the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), extending devotional concern to parents’ welfare and to the bonds of care between generations.
