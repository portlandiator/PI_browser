# progressive revelation; renewal of religion

Progressive revelation describes religion as a continuing process: successive Manifestations renew spiritual teachings for humanity’s changing needs. The central foundation remains one, while laws, customs, and social forms may change or become obsolete. Earlier religions are not simply denied; their truths are honored within a larger unfolding sequence. This view distinguishes enduring spiritual principles from time-bound provisions and gives renewal a purpose: guiding an evolving world without confusing distinct missions or eras.

Progressive revelation relates to [Eastern religions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=81056F52133146DC9ED2FCA3A4D36D9E) and the [oneness of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), including the continuity between [Christ and Christianity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FA444A710AD4FADBE7A093449A77252) and later faiths. It helps distinguish [changing and enduring elements](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B4B220998C46ED868D97D28B10F856) and explains the need for [renewal beyond obsolete dogma](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B6DA84931BE1A9E2A91755E047538C), while recognizing the [truth of earlier religions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=193E825FA96F443BBA9ABC0E163E0F11). Communication across [East and West](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDED88E95778E61DFBBBA823FAF0AE38) and differing [human capacities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790) affect how teachings are received. The sequence also preserves the [distinct stations of the Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=797723473B3647F38FDE65C86DBE2669).
