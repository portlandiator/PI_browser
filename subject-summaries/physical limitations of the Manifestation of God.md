# physical limitations of the Manifestation of God

A Manifestation of God shares the bodily conditions of human life, including hunger, fatigue, illness, and suffering. Physical vulnerability does not diminish His spiritual station or the authority of His revelation. Distinguishing bodily life from rational and divine capacities helps explain how a bearer of transcendent guidance can endure ordinary human constraints. Recognition therefore requires looking beyond outward weakness to the wisdom, character, and transforming power revealed through that human life.

One adjacent theme is [differences in human capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790): the sources recognize varying capacities among people, while also distinguishing the Manifestation’s bodily condition from His spiritual station. Another is the [inability of the Manifestation; Prophet to comprehend God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B492C6CABFF847E5814269886AE0C4E9). The Manifestation’s physical limits do not mean that the divine Essence is comprehensible; the passages instead separate human bodily experience, spiritual authority, and knowledge of God’s ultimate reality. Together these connections clarify that limitation in one register does not erase distinction in another.
