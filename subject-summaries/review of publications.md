# review of publications

Review of publications can help ensure that material issued in the name of the Bahá’í community is accurate, responsible, and consistent with its purpose. Such review carries a tension: guidance may protect the community and readers, while excessive restriction can hinder learning and expression. The process therefore requires wisdom and care, with attention to both the integrity of published work and the freedom needed for ideas to develop.

[Covenant-breaking and Covenant-breakers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42) concerns protecting the unity of the Faith, a distinct but related institutional responsibility. [Defending the Faith; protecting the Cause; apologetics](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AE513863B45CE049600364AD783BC0EF) addresses responses to criticism through words and writing. [Newspapers and the media; publications](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2D71B43F6FD6403083AC1BCB025C195E) places review within the wider field of public communication. [Personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2) reflects case-specific guidance, while [Wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B) names the judgment needed when standards meet particular circumstances. Together these links connect publication review with protection, communication, and prudent application.
