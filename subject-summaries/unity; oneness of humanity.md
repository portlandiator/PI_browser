# unity; oneness of humanity

Humanity is one family, sharing a common origin and a common destiny. This oneness is the central principle of Bahá’u’lláh's teachings and calls for a transformation of relationships among peoples, nations, and institutions. Its realization requires the abolition of prejudice, the practice of justice, and cooperation across inherited boundaries. Love must become visible in conduct and social organization, carrying human solidarity from the family and community to the whole world.

The [maturity of mankind](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FA54B06FDFAEE43C8FE0DBD8E1679105) gives world unity its historical horizon, with [universal peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675), the [spiritualization of humanity in the future](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7FD4EDEC44AD46EFB11981D4CAC32114), and [predictions and prophecies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E) pointing toward its fulfillment. It addresses the practical stakes of [war and peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50D596B8CDE8EE327D4667F516197AF2), [nationalism and love of country](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E5EFFF9275424573B014771437ACCE2C), and relationships between [East and West](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDED88E95778E61DFBBBA823FAF0AE38), while [unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) safeguards humanity's richness. Overcoming [prejudice and class distinction](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57), advancing [race unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=97E01B3F4DF6EC02175D948C7567769E), establishing the [equality of men and women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55D2FC30AFA84B35ACE7F2ADC2E8DD0E), and practicing [social and divine justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34) make oneness concrete. These purposes connect the [mission of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25E17ABCAE6B4195844602A0178DB4E5), the [purpose of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=29AF567C76864D208EA3BD39BC5CC7CF), the [unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), and [religion as a basis of morality and civilization](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4F32835BDB1F4C02956E92DB1AE38C5F). The [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), its [interpretation in scripture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=58B72013D935E92718D07A6AB1DCFB78), and the [centrality of the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA) supply foundations for shared understanding; [independent investigation of truth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30A42A15DD044876BA9A0BA8D1D60330) and the [harmony of science and religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=99656E1B1D7344D8930ACA530FDE210B) guide inquiry. [Enumerations of Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) place these relationships together within the wider [influence of the Cause in this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7), while [interconnectedness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2FC11A99D6A34DFD93993D59F31450E8) offers an analogy for mutual dependence and [integration and disintegration](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994) illuminate opposing movements in society. At the personal level, [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [walking the spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B), and [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) turn conviction into the [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7). [Love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700), [fellowship among the friends](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A), [forgiveness and forbearance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), and [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) sustain the relationships necessary for [community building](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D2A37FC072734EADAA2D0F4A2BC2B5B0). [Spiritual gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), [moral education of children](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4), [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and [prayer for individuals and groups](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343) nurture this shared life. The [call to unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FE8395896269EDE56E9A05BA5E15262E) therefore becomes a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), expressed in [service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [serving humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), and [working for the betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80). [Teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), its [spiritual prerequisites](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), and [proclamation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3CD1B6C1EA79E50DBEE4211DC6F9A186) invite wider participation; [being a source of light and guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1) and the [growth of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22EED87D5EEC4CA68E16AB238733762E) depend on communities demonstrating the unity they proclaim.
