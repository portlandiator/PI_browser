# prayer for unity

Unity joins people in love, harmony, and a shared purpose, repairing divisions caused by distance, hostility, or dispersion. The prayers ask that scattered believers be gathered, hearts reconciled, and communities strengthened in fellowship. Their vision also widens beyond a religious group to harmony among peoples and nations. Unity is therefore both an inward bond and a visible social condition, made real through mutual affection, cooperation, and the overcoming of estrangement.

Prayer for unity draws on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), asking that fellowship and concord replace separation and discord.
