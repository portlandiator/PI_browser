# family of Muhammad; early figures in Islam

Muhammad’s family and the early figures of Islam shaped the formation of the Muslim community and its historical memory. Accounts recall kinship, leadership, devotion, and sacrifice, and Bahá’í discussions place these lives within the wider history of Islam. Particular stories may carry spiritual lessons as well as historical significance, and their importance extends across later Muslim traditions.

[Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) connects the lives of early figures with ethical change and service. [Stories; anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) reflects the narrative traditions through which their memories are transmitted. Together these links connect historical figures with both the lessons drawn from their conduct and the stories that preserve them.
