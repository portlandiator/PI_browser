# alcohol and drugs; tobacco; opium

The Bahá’í writings prohibit opium and other intoxicating or mind-altering substances that impair reason and harm the body; guidance also calls for abstinence from alcohol and habit-forming drugs. Hashish is explicitly condemned in later counsel. Tobacco appears in related discussions but is not treated with the same uniform legal wording, so these categories should not be collapsed. The underlying concern is preserving human faculties and dignity, while addressing addiction with care rather than contempt for those who struggle.

The prohibition shares legal context with [gambling and games of chance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF0ECD50DBFC42C3A855CE24E0DCAABD), which are also forbidden in the Kitáb-i-Aqdas.
