# Imam Husayn

Imam Husayn is remembered for his martyrdom at Karbala, a defining event in Shi’i Muslim history and a lasting symbol of resistance to injustice. His stand is associated with sacrifice, steadfastness, and fidelity to principle despite overwhelming danger. The memory of Karbala continues to shape religious devotion and communal identity. Its meaning includes grief for a historical loss and inspiration drawn from Husayn’s moral example.

[Martyrs and martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977) places Husayn among figures whose deaths are remembered as witness to faith. [Consolation and comfort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) connects mourning with spiritual solace. [Episodes from the early history of Islam](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7224C128FE3EED201628A9AF51BCF757) gives the Karbala story its historical setting, while [Eulogies; reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137) and [Expressions of grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14) reflect how the event is remembered and mourned. Together these links connect history, sacrifice, memory, and grief.
