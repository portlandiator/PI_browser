# importance of authoritative interpretation

Authoritative interpretation provides guidance on the meaning and application of religious teachings when questions arise. It differs from personal study, which remains important, by offering an authorized reference for resolving ambiguity within the community. This role supports coherence while leaving room for continued learning and consultation.

The authority to interpret is connected to [the House of Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A801789EBB804ABB86C36BB8CAB50FED), whose institutional role provides guidance on questions affecting the community.
