# acting according to the exigencies of the day; pragmatism

Practical decisions must respond to the needs and conditions of their time, but expediency alone cannot sustain lasting solutions. Bahá’í writings argue that spiritual principles—justice, unity, and concern for human dignity—give practical action its purpose and direction. The task is to apply enduring values wisely to changing circumstances, avoiding both rigid formulas and short-term measures detached from moral aims.
