# the Bab; the Primal Point; station and claims of the Bab

The Báb, known as the Primal Point, founded a new religious dispensation in nineteenth-century Persia and announced the coming of a greater Manifestation. His writings and claims place him both as an independent Manifestation of God and as the herald of Bahá’u’lláh. The distinction between these stations is central: the Báb’s revelation has its own authority, while also preparing the way for a succeeding revelation.

[The Word of God; influence and centrality of](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) relates the Báb’s station to the divine Word as a source of spiritual illumination. [Arcs of ascent and descent](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8CDBF5DFF3E24FB1AAF00AA7099BA2CE) connects prophetic succession with the movement of spiritual history. [Martyrs and martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977) recalls the cost of the Báb’s mission, while [Prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) situates his advent within religious expectation. [Twin Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F115687B598B470AB058ADEDD3C97F66) clarifies the relation between his revelation and Bahá’u’lláh’s, and [Primal Will and the Word; Remembrance; Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA) addresses the theological language surrounding his station. Together these links connect his claims with revelation, succession, sacrifice, and fulfillment.
