# tests and trials; sacrifice and suffering

Tests, trials, sacrifice, and suffering are difficulties encountered in the pursuit of material and spiritual goals. Hardship in the pursuit of worldly success differs from the greater trials faced by those seeking divine guidance; suffering may also become a means to spiritual distinction. Endurance is not sought for its own sake; it is patience in the path of a meaningful aim.

Trials raise questions about [divine will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B71A5AD3EFDF459695EBD1C1B11C18F6) and [theodicy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9DEC6BF8F69A406FADF3FCA1C4BFAE33), and may accompany [Bahá’u’lláh’s Ascension](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AA92E315F90E73014DE6159A4A9C633), [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), and [Covenant-breaking](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42). Believers seek [protection](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B0211B4DCD4EA86BB1FC4BB6ADCD7C3), remember [martyrs](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), and endure [imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) through [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Practical responses include [pilgrimage](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=71CA0BAD93CAE678CC1BA773DF8A5EC8), [contentment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8143807DA046EA3DD9AFADD97E824E93), [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), and receiving [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) along the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Personal narratives of [Abdu’l-Baha](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710), [‘Akka](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4FAFE46E3FC7417BAC3D18A3C7D260C2), [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8), and [Adrianople](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6580A1098A7242F9B29D4916F5B4F414) preserve particular experiences; the response may include [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), reflection on [material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), [composure](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7E47D15B29E2E0A0D13CF981131C65BF), and awareness of [transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C). [Opposition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) tests [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), while [forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) shape conduct. Hardship also bears on [wealth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C265864F8406449F9A2770021D2BBB3C), [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450), [growth of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22EED87D5EEC4CA68E16AB238733762E), [prayer for individuals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343), [joy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [humor](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8D9F026E4760E4C06FB3125FB37BD27E), and [self-knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3ED3EB2EFEBF43C9A36134E26FDB38EE). [Trust](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D1CA07BA971AE54EB4BCE0ABB94841E7), [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and the [pain of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A230B24E88D943A79A527E42B4172FE0) help bear it; [poetry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91840AE122AEC232D80E33023EC88DF), [gratitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD4052A3136042C4B86176AB2F552C55), [prayer for steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A2DA22B0196E4813CDC85DB1C970313), and [the people of the Bayan](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E24FE7CCD7B1E27FD3CD60548C20CE2D) preserve its meaning. The [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), [sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0), and [‘Abdu’l-Baha’s station](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315) illuminate suffering, while [wine imagery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102) and [divine justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9170438CF5C0E2D84968B1DE41C36F0F) frame hope and recompense.
