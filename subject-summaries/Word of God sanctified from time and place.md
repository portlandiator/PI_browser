# Word of God sanctified from time and place

The Word of God is sanctified from time and place because its spiritual reality is not confined to a particular location or historical moment. Its manifestation occurs in time through revelation, but its divine origin and spiritual station transcend physical measures. This distinction separates the Word’s eternal significance from the temporal circumstances of its appearance.

The Word’s relation to divine transcendence connects with [God as immanent and transcendent](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6), while [the preexistence of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAC65ADBDE024CAFA9F155EDDCCA0C1F) considers how a spiritual station relates to its historical embodiment. Together these subjects distinguish the timeless source of revelation from its appearance in human history.
