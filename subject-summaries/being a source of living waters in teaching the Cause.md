# being a source of living waters in teaching the Cause

A teacher becomes a source of living waters by drawing wisdom from divine guidance and sharing it freely with others. The image of a spring or cup suggests nourishment that flows outward: knowledge is not hoarded, but offered to revive understanding and help souls grow. Detachment, purity of heart, and dedication clear the channel, while the Holy Spirit is understood to quicken the hearer. The teacher serves as a conduit, not the origin, of this life-giving influence.
