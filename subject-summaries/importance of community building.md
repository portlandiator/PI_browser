# importance of community building

Community building develops relationships, capacities, and institutions that let people work together for a better society. It depends on more than increasing numbers: trust, service, learning, and participation must grow as well. Communities become stronger when members support one another and build ties with the wider society. The process links spiritual ideals with practical efforts that address shared needs.

[East and West; communication between East and West](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDED88E95778E61DFBBBA823FAF0AE38) connects community building with relationships across cultures. [Teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) concerns inviting people into shared purpose, while [Unity; oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) supplies a broad social aim. [Love, unity, and fellowship among the friends](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A) describes the bonds that sustain local efforts, and [Spiritual meetings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) provides settings for those bonds to grow. Together these links show community building as relational, spiritual, and outward-facing.
