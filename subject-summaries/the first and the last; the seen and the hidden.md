# the first and the last; the seen and the hidden

“The First and the Last” and “the Seen and the Hidden” are divine names that express encompassing presence across beginnings and endings, appearance and concealment. These paired terms do not imply temporal change in God; they describe how divine attributes may be recognized in the created order while the divine Essence remains beyond sight.

These paired dimensions relate to [the microcosm and macrocosm](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EA626E1123AE4CA9BDB62193BD55DE62), where patterns in the small and large scales of existence are understood as corresponding aspects of one ordered reality.
