# oneness; unity of religion

The divine religions share a spiritual foundation and call humanity toward fellowship rather than rivalry. Their teachings converge on love, moral action, and the unity of humankind, even as their historical forms and the missions of their Manifestations remain distinct. Recognizing common truth does not require erasing differences; it invites people to honor other traditions, reject sectarian strife, and see religion as a source of concord. Unity is both a spiritual principle and a social responsibility.
