# unity in diversity

Humanity's diversity enriches its unity. Like flowers in a garden or organs in a body, different peoples and capacities contribute to a shared life without losing their distinctive qualities. Cultural identities can flourish within allegiance to the well-being of the whole human family. The differences to overcome are those that generate prejudice, oppression, and hostility; differences that bring beauty, insight, and complementary strengths belong within unity itself.

The [oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives diversity a common foundation, while [diversity as a requirement of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D311B27723C741DCB7348644B473637F) and [differences in human capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790) explain why unity need not mean sameness. This vision challenges [prejudice and class distinction](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57) and requires deliberate work for [race unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=97E01B3F4DF6EC02175D948C7567769E). [Love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700) become visible through [kindness and fellowship with all](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80), expressing the [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7). The [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) supplies a spiritual center around which differences can become complementary. [Spiritual and devotional gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) and the [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41) offer settings for that shared devotion; [marriage as a means of spiritual progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6615BDF38C0B46B9960CFA282715258D) brings the same principle into the intimate cooperation of two distinct persons.
