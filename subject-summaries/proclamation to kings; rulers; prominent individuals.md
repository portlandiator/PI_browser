# proclamation to kings; rulers; prominent individuals

Proclamation to kings and rulers addresses political power with a direct summons to hear, judge fairly, and follow the straight path. Messages conveyed from the prison call sovereigns to consider a spiritual appeal alongside their worldly authority. Their response to revelation can shape the condition of their peoples.

The summons arises amid [suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) and interprets earlier promises through [prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797).
