# martyrs and martyrdom

Martyrdom refers to sacrifice or death suffered in fidelity to religious belief and principle. Its spiritual meaning lies not in violence or death itself but in steadfastness, witness, and the willingness to serve truth despite danger. Martyrdom also brings grief to communities and leaves examples for later generations.

Martyrdom is situated in [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) and in the memory of [Imam Husayn](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B08DD762C73AED1C83EAE3215CED1D53), amid [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14) and [tests and sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4). Accounts of [eulogies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137) and [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) preserve remembrance, while [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8), and [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) show how sacrifice can inspire others.
