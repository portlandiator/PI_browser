# generosity [kirama]

Generosity gives freely from what one has, whether wealth, knowledge, time, or a modest gift. Its worth lies in a willing and joyful spirit, not in the size of the offering; gratitude remains possible even in scarcity. Munificence can relieve the poor, support learning, and extend mercy without bias. Spiritual generosity also gives of oneself in service, and material bounty should benefit all, not a privileged few.

Generosity is a mark of the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), and can be cultivated through [children’s moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4).
