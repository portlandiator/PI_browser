# power of faith; power of the spirit

Faith and spirit can confer capacities far beyond a person’s unaided strength. Divine confirmations can turn a drop into a sea or a gnat into an eagle, transforming what seems limited or weak. This power comes through grace and steadfast reliance, enabling action that would otherwise appear impossible and giving the believer courage to serve.

Spiritual power depends on [divine confirmations and capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and grows through [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668).
