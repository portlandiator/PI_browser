# limits of the intellect

The intellect can investigate created realities and reason about divine signs, but it cannot encompass the essence of God. Its limits are not a rejection of reason; they distinguish what the mind can know through attributes from what remains beyond finite comprehension. Human intellectual power is great within its proper domain and bounded before the divine Essence.

These limits are clarified by [the Word of God and human limitations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A867E142C1FC454488A95DEED1B39E95), while [human distinction from animals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB) concerns the mind’s remarkable capacity within creation. [Rational arguments](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12FD56FFF2C74384AF52D04E7CEE255F) show how reason can support belief, and [the impossibility of true worship of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=173632E7BF104AB486EFBB383AF8DEAD) addresses the gulf between finite understanding and divine reality. The Prophet’s own limitation before God is considered in [the Manifestation’s inability to comprehend God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B492C6CABFF847E5814269886AE0C4E9).
