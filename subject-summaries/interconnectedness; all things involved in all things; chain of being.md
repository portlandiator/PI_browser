# interconnectedness; all things involved in all things; chain of being

Created things exist through relations with other things: causes produce effects, parts contribute to wholes, and changes in one place can affect what is connected to it. The image of a chain of being expresses this mutual dependence across many degrees of existence. Interconnectedness does not mean that all things are identical or that every association is equally direct. It means that isolation is limited and that a world of relationships must be considered as an ordered whole.

[Law of cause and effect](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DF7D0EA999F44DBCA4F5C695B3851674) focuses on one form of relation, where effects depend on causes. [Interconnectedness of the teachings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DFD7CEB3B4E44FA3B8BE8B17E353D434) applies a similar principle to interpretation, warning that one statement alone may not yield a complete picture. [Astrology and numerology](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80A27A174ADBEBDDDCB2D427A7812EEC) marks a boundary: belief in relations among things does not validate every system claiming hidden correspondences. [Cycles in the physical and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E10CB3367945B283E91F7CC23BFD22) shows connection across recurring patterns. [Microcosm and macrocosm](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EA626E1123AE4CA9BDB62193BD55DE62) compares small and large scales; [Unity of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0858B3C3177BE8300A0B98D4DF4EBB10) considers philosophical claims about oneness; and [Unity; oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives interconnectedness a social expression. [Divine attributes are within all things; every atom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B996EA76F6EC401A9148E1F0507C179A) adds a theological account of signs throughout creation. These links show several meanings of relation while preserving their differences.
