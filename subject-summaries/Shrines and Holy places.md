# Shrines and Holy places

Shrines and holy places preserve the memory of central figures and events in Bahá’í history and provide places for prayer and pilgrimage. They are treated with reverence because of their spiritual associations, and their care connects devotion with stewardship and community service. Sacred places include shrines, burial sites, and locations linked to the lives of the central figures; these forms are related but not interchangeable.

[Holy Days and the Bahá’í calendar](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1FED56E998B24C76A6E68CC456452598) places visits and commemorations within sacred time. [Sacred remains; burial of the Báb](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=51F55B9A3EECE5C74C07866CC5F2D02D) concerns a specific shrine and its history, while [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41) distinguishes places for communal worship from shrines. [Business; financial; property matters](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8C309A3E20044543A9A72F36981F7D2A) touches on stewardship, and [Prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) relates sacred places to religious history. [Law of pilgrimage](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DEE255C5CCCC4F548E258C3E5E1B450C) and [Permission for visit; pilgrimage](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=71CA0BAD93CAE678CC1BA773DF8A5EC8) address visitation. [Praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), [Expressions of grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), and [Acknowledgment of gift](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78) connect devotion, memory, and support. [Suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) recalls historical sacrifice, while [Spiritual meetings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) and [Service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) connect sacred places with community life. [Rejection, opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) and [Future of Haifa; of the Holy Land](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5AC79BF370C1E403616347F043187025) place these sites within both historical hardship and future development. Together these links distinguish shrines from Houses of Worship while connecting sacred geography with pilgrimage, memory, and service.
