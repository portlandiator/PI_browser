# identity; relationship of Manifestation of God to God

The relationship between God and the Manifestation is described through language of identity that signifies perfect reflection and representation, not the absorption of the Manifestation into the unknowable divine Essence. The Manifestation speaks with divine authority and makes God’s attributes present to human beings, yet does not claim independent control over God’s will or purpose. This distinction preserves divine transcendence alongside the immediacy of revelation.

The question overlaps with [God as personal or impersonal reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0905F934B5D4402E84FCD0F0E325E0A7) and [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5), which situates this relationship in the Manifestation’s revelatory role. The mirror image in [Manifestation as mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D941821F406B4A20B1E60AD343B49717) expresses perfect reflection while preserving distinction, and [speaking in the voice of Divinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25) explains the authority of revealed speech. Limits on the Manifestation’s knowledge are treated in [the Prophet’s inability to comprehend God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B492C6CABFF847E5814269886AE0C4E9), while [the Manifestation as mediator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=83C2E82C31DB4115A3CDF5884D0CB19E) describes the route by which divine guidance reaches creation. The meaning of divine presence is explored in [presence of the Manifestation as presence of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82D2161C8BB4E087647B84F29B41D6D3); [Manifestation as sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) and [the twofold or threefold station](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=568978583E424499943612635F17DB7F) offer related metaphors and distinctions.
