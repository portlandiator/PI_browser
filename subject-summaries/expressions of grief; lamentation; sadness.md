# expressions of grief; lamentation; sadness

Grief and lamentation are human responses to loss, suffering, and separation. Religious consolation can accompany mourning without silencing it, while prayer, remembrance, and hope offer ways to endure sorrow. Mourning can preserve emotional honesty while drawing on spiritual resilience and compassion for those who grieve.
