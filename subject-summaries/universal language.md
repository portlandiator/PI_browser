# universal language

A universal auxiliary language would let people communicate across linguistic boundaries while preserving the value of their mother tongues. A shared language could reduce the time and effort spent learning many languages and help people understand one another directly. Its selection and adoption are matters for international agreement, not for one community to impose. The aim is practical mutual understanding as a support for unity and cooperation.

[Lists; enumerations of Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) places a shared language among a broader set of teachings for social harmony. [Predictions and prophecies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E) connects the idea to expectations about future change, while [Means of establishment of future civilization](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22163AE55B8C4896A94CFA8EC0CB10C3) situates it within the institutions and practices needed for a more unified world. Together these neighbors frame a common language as both a practical instrument and part of a larger vision for civilization.
