# Manifestation of God as divine physician

The Manifestation of God as divine physician describes revelation as a remedy for spiritual and social illness. Divine teachings diagnose harmful conditions and offer guidance that restores human well-being and moral health. The metaphor concerns spiritual healing while coexisting with the practical role of competent physicians in treating bodily disease.

The distinction between spiritual and bodily care is developed in [health and healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55), while the transformative effects of guidance connect with [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Together these subjects frame the physician image as a metaphor for the healing influence of revelation, without displacing material medicine.
