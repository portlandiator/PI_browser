# Imam 'Ali

‘Ali ibn Abi Talib is revered in Islam as Muhammad’s cousin and son-in-law, an early Muslim, and the fourth caliph; Shi’i Muslims also regard him as the first Imam. His life is associated with learning, justice, courage, and leadership during a formative period in Islamic history. Sunni and Shi’i traditions differ in how they frame his succession and authority, while both honor his importance.
