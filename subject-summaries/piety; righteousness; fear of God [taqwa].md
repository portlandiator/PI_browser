# piety; righteousness; fear of God [taqwa]

Piety, righteousness, and fear of God describe an ethical orientation that shapes what a person says, values, and does. Truthfulness, trustworthiness, courtesy, and fidelity adorn human character; righteousness brings a station of influence. Piety is not simply fear of punishment; it is reverent awareness that guides conduct and safeguards justice and compassion.

Piety begins with [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), is walked out along the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and is cultivated through [exhortation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B). It is reflected in [purity of heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098).
