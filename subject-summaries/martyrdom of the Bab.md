# martyrdom of the Bab

The Báb’s execution in 1850 at Tabriz became a defining moment in the history of the Bábí movement. His public martyrdom, carried out after a period of imprisonment and trial, intensified the resolve of his followers and drew wider attention to his cause. Bahá’í accounts remember the event as both a historical tragedy and a spiritual witness, while preserving the circumstances of the execution.
