# symbolism of color and light

Color and light carry symbolic meanings in religious language, where brightness may suggest knowledge, guidance, or spiritual life and darkness may suggest obscurity. Particular colors can also represent different human or spiritual capacities. These associations function as interpretive images rather than fixed physical explanations.

The symbolic reading of color connects to [literal interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=87CAE620F6194480B20F2C8B053A5841), [the human soul as a mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F), and [the Manifestation as the sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35), where light represents spiritual illumination.
