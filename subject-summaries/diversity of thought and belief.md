# diversity of thought and belief

Diverse minds reach differing judgments, yet their variety can contribute to harmony. Distinct perspectives and degrees of understanding can each enrich a shared life, like different flowers in one garden. A wider spiritual perception offers a meeting point for divergent views. Unity grows as people recognize both their common purpose and the particular contribution each can make.
