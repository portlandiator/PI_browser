# education of children; moral education

Children’s education joins intellectual learning with moral and spiritual development. Young people need knowledge, good character, practical skills, and opportunities to serve; families, schools, and communities all share responsibility for nurturing these qualities. Early education can shape habits and conduct that are difficult to change later. The aim is not only academic attainment but the development of people able to contribute to the well-being of others.

[Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) links education with becoming more concerned for the lives of others. [Manifestation of God as gardener; cultivator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DD46AC953147458BAB7F06770FD865D5) uses cultivation as an image for guiding young minds and hearts. [Teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), [Assistance to the poor and lowly](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B118E279E521421DA6DE76C3D326B7A6), [Service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), and [Call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) connect education with practical service and responsibility. [Praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) supports the young, while [The economic problem and its solution; voluntary giving](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FC3291E97A2B4A1FB2A2B664AB345C85), [Divine grace and spiritual capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), and [Generosity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=531C2217F0C1EC4A8750BEC3A0B284AA) connect moral education with compassion and spiritual growth. [Role and station of women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=95E0DCD23ED9E61EE2BC99ECDBC8D8C0) and [Characteristics and conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) bring family and example into view. [Prayer for children and youth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6193A5AE5579E4039E4D0589AE21DACA), [High station of learning](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25BCE94ADDAF4B1B89551A0B5A85DE9C), and [High station of the arts and sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8D8CB2AB7034F7DB07173698FD9C979) stress spiritual, intellectual, and cultural development. Both catalogue entries for [Serving humanity; working for progress; betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and [Serving humanity; working for progress, betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80) connect education with community advancement. [Wealth inequality and its moderation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A8B7BAB865C1EDFF1E086B0B1C1C8B38) adds the social conditions that affect children’s lives. Together these connections place children’s education within family, community, service, justice, and spiritual development.
