# inability of the Manifestation; Prophet to comprehend God

The Manifestation of God does not comprehend the divine Essence or control God’s ultimate will. This limitation preserves the distinction between the unknowable Creator and the created human reality of the Prophet, even while the Manifestation possesses a unique revelatory station. Divine authority expressed in revelation thus coexists with humility before the unknowable source of that revelation.

The limits of prophetic knowledge point to [the transcendence and unknowability of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) and clarify [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5), whose authority does not erase the Creator-creature distinction. This distinction is central to [the Manifestation’s relationship to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB) and to accounts of [the physical limitations of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12119CC76EF849FF97AEEF4858C87628). The limits of reason are addressed in [the intellect’s limits](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=048D1BC9980545A2BF50BCE956D33C2C), placing the Manifestation’s inability to comprehend the Essence within a broader recognition that finite minds cannot encompass divine reality.
