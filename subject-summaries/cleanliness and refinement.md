# cleanliness and refinement

Cleanliness and refinement join bodily care with spiritual freshness, beauty, and independence of spirit. Washing soiled garments, observing utmost cleanliness, and refining one’s surroundings express this ideal; harmony and symmetry also bring pleasure to heart and spirit. Purity and order thus shape both practical life and the conditions in which prayer, beauty, and spiritual attention can be experienced.

Refinement concerns [beauty and aesthetics](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=05E420B98BD2E4B67D13B89EF7D1AFDF) and is guided by [order, tact, and deliberation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4925B84D51C04E3DB0D1AB40021DA116).
