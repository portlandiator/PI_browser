# life and death

Life and death have both bodily and spiritual meanings. Physical death ends the body’s earthly life, while spiritual life refers to awakening through knowledge, love, and divine guidance. The contrast therefore does not make bodily existence unreal; it distinguishes material vitality from the soul’s spiritual condition.

The imagery of spiritual renewal connects with [living waters and the water of life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992), which represent the life-giving influence of divine guidance.
