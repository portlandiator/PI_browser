# mindfulness; concentration; focus

Mindfulness, concentration, and focus gather scattered thought so that intention can become effective action. Unfocused thinking produces no result, while concentration on one point can bring fruits; mindfulness in every condition helps direct attention to service. Concentration is therefore not withdrawal from the world but a way to direct mental energy toward purposeful, sustained work.

Focused attention draws on [the mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C), the capacity to investigate and comprehend realities.
