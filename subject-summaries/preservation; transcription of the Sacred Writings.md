# preservation; transcription of the Sacred Writings

Preservation and transcription of the Sacred Writings require vigilance because Tablets can be lost, seized, or exposed to hostile hands. Believers are urged to guard them more carefully than their own eyes, designate secure storage, and ensure verses are transcribed and printed for distribution. Safeguarding the writings is both a practical duty and an act of reverence toward words understood as proceeding from the Most High.
