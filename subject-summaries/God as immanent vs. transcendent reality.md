# God as immanent vs. transcendent reality

God's presence fills creation through His signs, attributes, and sustaining bounty, while His Essence remains beyond every creature's grasp. The sun and its reflections offer a guiding image: light shines in countless mirrors without the sun descending into them. Divine nearness therefore does not erase the distinction between Creator and creation. The Manifestations of God make His guidance and perfections accessible, directing human recognition beyond imagined pictures of the divine toward a living revelation of His qualities.

[Transcendence and the unknowability of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) explain the limits of human comprehension, reinforced by the principle that [the lower cannot encompass the higher](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C957F3FF62F84A3A8DBA4B23EA26E8CC). The [Word of God and human limitations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A867E142C1FC454488A95DEED1B39E95) brings those limits into the encounter with revelation, while the [Word's freedom from time and place](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=523CD2C496054AA682E01449BD6EE537) points beyond the conditions of earthly existence. [God's absolute freedom and independence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82A5773ED5884220A919FDF77F608B7B) preserve His distinction from everything dependent upon Him. [Nature and God's will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=879087898C9746AA88A2BE11CA9F1331), [divine attributes within every atom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B996EA76F6EC401A9148E1F0507C179A), and the [human soul as a mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) illuminate the complementary theme: creation bears divine signs, and human capacities can reflect them more fully through spiritual development. The question of [God as personal or impersonal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0905F934B5D4402E84FCD0F0E325E0A7) asks how language drawn from human experience can speak of such a reality. The [impossibility of fully adequate worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=173632E7BF104AB486EFBB383AF8DEAD) encourages humility in that language and turns devotion toward the Manifestations, through whom the unknowable God is recognized.
