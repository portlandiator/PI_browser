# the Word of God; influence and centrality of

The Word of God is the central expression of divine revelation and the source of spiritual illumination for humanity. It gives form to religious teachings, awakens capacities, and guides the development of civilizations. In Bahá’í terminology it is closely associated with the Manifestation of God, through whom divine guidance reaches the world, while remaining a created expression distinct from the unknowable Essence.

The Word’s revelatory and spiritual force connects to [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853), [Christ and Christianity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FA444A710AD4FADBE7A093449A77252), and [the power of words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A) and [speaking in the voice of Divinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25), while its historical reception includes [rejection by the people of the Bayan](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1219A74FADB7E7059B143DAF131CB386) and [opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E).
