# preexistence of the Manifestation of God

The preexistence of a Manifestation concerns a spiritual or revelatory reality that precedes its historical appearance, not the essential preexistence belonging to God alone. The distinction allows the Manifestations to be described as continuous across dispensations while preserving the temporal limits of their human lives and missions. “Preexistence” therefore has a relative or spiritual sense here, distinct from God’s absolute eternity.

The distinction between divine eternity and created preexistence connects with [infinity and eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6BB666C0D9224ED7AE3400FC770A3496) and [existence, nonexistence, and preexistence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EFDFB11C5F3247618BA648A0DD0EB4B3). [The station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) clarifies the different senses in which spiritual and human realities are discussed, while [the Word of God sanctified from time and place](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=523CD2C496054AA682E01449BD6EE537) offers a related account of a reality beyond temporal location. Together these links caution against assigning God’s essential preexistence to a Manifestation, even when its spiritual station is described as preceding a particular earthly mission.
