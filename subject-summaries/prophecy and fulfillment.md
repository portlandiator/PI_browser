# prophecy and fulfillment

Prophecy and fulfillment concern the recognition of a promised revelation in events and in the language of earlier scripture. Bahá’í interpretation holds that words once withheld from Christ’s disciples are now disclosed, that what was inscribed has come to pass, and that peoples long awaiting the Promised One can recognize the Day of God. Fulfillment is both scriptural interpretation and a present summons to respond.

Fulfillment is centered on [the Bab and Baha’u’llah as the Promised One](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1BD3D83CFE5BED467DA6187D16824CF5), understood through [the truth of earlier religions and scriptures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=193E825FA96F443BBA9ABC0E163E0F11), [the Seal of the Prophets](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B550C93F4C28EFA9AAAACB38F0F99623), and [interpretation of scriptural passages](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=58B72013D935E92718D07A6AB1DCFB78). Its proclamation is voiced to [the peoples of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F9036AE7751AE9E7820A58D34211FEFC), [the people of the Bayan](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E24FE7CCD7B1E27FD3CD60548C20CE2D), and [kings and rulers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6206D325290FE8E7EC332EBD6D88A238), with [the Bab’s station and claims](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3633354029CFE10F551F7CA219518DAC) and [the Manifestation as the sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) part of the recognition. The new message is [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), whose [greatness in this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7) is linked to the [growth of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22EED87D5EEC4CA68E16AB238733762E). Fulfilled expectation also touches [prophecy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E), [the Day of Resurrection](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4A0212925E1641028AE59AFCC9784431), [Jabulqa and Jabarsa](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=335D74632561E2EEA7E795D40A3FECE9), and [shrines](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=69863262A551E3763501D5846A98329B). Recognition calls for [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), [prayer for spiritual recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), and turning from [heedlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9EC159259B874FABB63915C45183582D); resistance brings [rejection and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), while the Promised One’s suffering recalls [imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) and [Christ’s rejection](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B2340566123BECE11972A4501C75B8C1). Its transforming force also relates to [the House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41), [light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994), and [the power of speech](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A). The promise was also read within [Judaism and the Torah](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=93585856EDADED995488E210D42FFF2E), took form amid [life in Adrianople](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6580A1098A7242F9B29D4916F5B4F414), and bears on [the future of Haifa and the Holy Land](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5AC79BF370C1E403616347F043187025).
