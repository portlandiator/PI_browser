# purity of heart; sincerity of intention; sanctity

Purity of heart, sincerity of intention, and sanctity describe inward integrity that must be joined to conduct. Purity shapes chastity, prayer, service, and freedom from self-love; the soul becomes a mirror in which divine attributes may be reflected. A sincere heart is not self-congratulation but readiness for spiritual transformation, disciplined action, and love of God.

Purity is connected to [chastity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FEC681A8D62B4EA3B73B57371D4A34C7), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [righteousness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E5E0436334E8467AA2757EF389C2161A), and [prayer for purity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BDF8B75365C1E9D95EAB6706CE6373DF). It supports the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), and release from [selfishness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C1D65E1D0354A79817EF2304DA940E8). The [soul as a mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) reflects divine light; purity is a [prerequisite for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) and depends on [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E). It appears in [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946) and grows through [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649).
