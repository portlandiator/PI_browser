# the Twelfth Imam; the Qa'im

The Twelfth Imam, or Qa’im, is a central figure in Twelver Shi’i expectation: the Imam is believed to be in occultation and to return as a divinely guided leader. Bahá’í interpretation connects the expectation of the Qa’im with the Báb’s declaration, while differing from literal expectations of a future return in the same historical form. This expectation raises questions of prophecy, recognition, and religious renewal across interpretive traditions.

[Rejection, opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) connects expectations of the Qa’im with the opposition faced by a figure understood by followers as fulfilling prophecy. The link points to the risks and disputes that can accompany religious recognition, while the specific beliefs about the Twelfth Imam remain rooted in Shi’i tradition.
