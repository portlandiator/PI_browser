# hypocrisy

Hypocrisy is the gap between professed virtue and actual motive or conduct. Religious language can conceal self-interest, vanity, or disloyalty, but outward forms alone cannot establish sincerity. Hypocrisy may appear in personal conduct or in those who claim to serve a religious cause while undermining it through their actions.

The critique of [empty learning and false spirituality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2A60928585F2ED134ACCA47EE14C6A73) similarly warns that a display of knowledge or piety can mask vanity. [Stories and anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) offer concrete illustrations of how character is disclosed by conduct rather than by self-description.
