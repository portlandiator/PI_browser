# The Primal Will

The Primal Will is a universal reality through which divine command and creation are expressed. It is associated with the Word, the First Intellect, and the source of contingent forms, yet is created by God and distinct from the divine Essence. In accounts of revelation, this spiritual reality is manifested in successive dispensations. The concept thus bridges cosmological language and the historical role of revelation.

The distinction between source and expression is developed in [emanation versus manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26BFCA2FC4E44906BAAB903F56228AB0), while [the self-generation of the Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C470397E9BCC44ECB45F21E1521E18C1) addresses how this reality is described as originating through divine command. Cosmological sequences appear in [seven stages of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3FF051A223694384AA2AA4EDE96BC144) and [creation of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E7BFAD48788E4AFE9B6EA752F7671AB1). The association with revelation and sacred utterance is taken up in [the Primal Will, the Word, Remembrance, and Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA), while [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) considers its historical expression. Finally, [nature and God’s will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=879087898C9746AA88A2BE11CA9F1331) explores a related vocabulary for describing creative order. These connections show the Primal Will’s role as an intermediary concept, preserving the distinction between God and creation.
