# parents and children; youth; families

Families provide a setting for love, responsibility, and the education of children and youth. Parents guide and care for the young, while children and youth also bring energy and capacities that communities should nurture. Family life therefore joins affection with duties that extend beyond the household. The well-being of children depends on both close relationships and the wider moral and social environment in which they grow.
