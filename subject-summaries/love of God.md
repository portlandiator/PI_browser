# love of God

Love of God is the force that draws the heart beyond material limitation and toward spiritual life. Believers are called to attune the heart to divine love and seek its fire as a source of spiritual renewal. Love is not only devotion but a power for transformation, knowledge, service, and the overcoming of self-centered desire.

Divine love is pictured as [fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D) and as a [power that moves the heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D64346EB795847B3817AB6A1A38C8ED9). It transforms the interplay of [light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994), underlies [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and prepares for [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67). Love also shapes [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), [symbolic imagery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450), and [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B); it joins [unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700) with [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and advances [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218). It is expressed in the Manifestation [speaking with divine authority](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25), in [devotional gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), and in [prayer for others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3) and the [good pleasure of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=034E6E7433614B24B4A7683E29D0C241). Love enkindles [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=023F2C5312FE4C9BA59624CDFA1998E1), calls to [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), redirects the heart from [transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), and animates the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). It appears in [reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137) and the sacrifices of [martyrs](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), and inspires [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and [joy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26). The [power of speech](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A) can convey love across [prejudice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D6982B984B945D3A13E98CA9FB0FE57), while the [pain of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A230B24E88D943A79A527E42B4172FE0) may deepen [nearness to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C22A37A43832E506617EB2F4434E855F) and [self-sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0). Love connects hearts through [spiritual communication](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E), draws on [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and [effort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB047A0483F7446F98969E0BB0A9A2A0), and informs [mystical themes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6259D6333FC7EE132D6AD0D77800E8FC) and [wine symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102). It extends to [prayer for the departed](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6F48F0918CDBEBB76DD545292C255B68), [fire and light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7112D8A18D3C4C7BB98681C6DE3D94E0), [Covenant firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633), [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). Its expressions include the [pathways of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=552E8D5180B04AAB91BE86621F37CD9B), orientation toward the [purpose of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=857627CDE8FBEF82708BB212CB362A7F), [honesty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), and recognition of the [true believer’s station](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9A8742BB523FE7870795B9A71B370B0A). Love is also fundamental to [religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7824F7FB65A4B75A74AC15AFD08EC5C), reflected in the [soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F), guided by [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B), and related to the [Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA) and the soul’s [higher and lower natures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5410D4DAE5454E8D81B13EC3E9DA2F23). It calls for [purity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098), affirms [human spiritual distinction](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB), and helps one endure [trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4) while [serving humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and [working for its betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80). Personal [prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343) and [submission to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA) complete this field of loving action.
