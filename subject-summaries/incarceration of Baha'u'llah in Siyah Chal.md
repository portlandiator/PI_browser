# incarceration of Baha'u'llah in Siyah Chal

Bahá’u’lláh was imprisoned in Tehran’s Siyah Chal in 1852 after the attempted assassination of the Shah by a group of Bábís. He was held in appalling conditions, chained among other prisoners, and subjected to severe hardship. During this imprisonment he experienced the onset of his prophetic mission, which he later described as a divine summons. The event marks a turning point in his life and in the emergence of the Bahá’í Faith.
