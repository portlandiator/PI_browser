# absolute freedom; independence of God

God is perfectly free and self-sufficient. All things depend upon Him; He depends upon nothing. Neither our worship nor our rejection adds to or diminishes His being, and no power can frustrate His will. His independence is inseparable from wisdom, justice, and generosity: He sustains and guides creation without needing anything in return. Obedience and devotion therefore meet our need for spiritual growth, not a need in God.

The three neighboring subjects place this independence within a wider account of divine reality. Its connection with [power and sovereignty of God; omnipotence of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E) emphasizes the efficacy of God's will: His freedom is inseparable from the power to create, sustain, and ordain. The broader subject [God as immanent vs. transcendent reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6) explores how creation can reveal divine attributes without containing the divine Essence; its image of sunlight reflected in a mirror helps express manifestation without a descent or division of God. The other broader subject, [transcendence; unknowability of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4), concerns the limits of human comprehension before that Essence. Together, these connections distinguish dependence on God's sustaining gifts from possession or comprehension of His own reality: divine signs may be encountered throughout creation, while God remains independent of the beings that receive them.
