# praise and encouragement

Praise and encouragement are forms of spiritual address that affirm devotion, strengthen resolve, and console believers amid difficulty. Praise recognizes divine qualities and favors, while encouragement urges people toward faith, service, and perseverance. Together they join devotional expression with moral support and purposeful action.
