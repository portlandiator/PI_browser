# continual renewal of creation

Creation is continually renewed: forms pass away and reappear, and each revelation can inaugurate a new spiritual cycle. Renewal may be the succession of similar forms that gives an appearance of permanence, or the transformation of minds, hearts, and social life when a Manifestation appears. It can describe material processes or spiritual rebirth, depending on the passage. Continuity and repeated change therefore belong together in this account.

Ongoing renewal unfolds within [infinity and eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6BB666C0D9224ED7AE3400FC770A3496), since divine creation is described as without beginning or end. The image also fits the [arcs of ascent and descent](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8CDBF5DFF3E24FB1AAF00AA7099BA2CE), where existence moves through distinct stages and the spiritual ascent follows material development. Renewal is related to [self-description of God; God's self-remembrance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=04F924E0CC51EDF12DBB02871348AD74) through passages in which creation reflects divine self-knowledge and signs. Finally, [divine emanation is ceaseless; eternal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=68137F318C6144AAAF17C8290F33061E) links recurring creation to the ongoing outpouring of divine grace. These subjects connect renewal with time, movement, divine expression, and continuing bounty.
