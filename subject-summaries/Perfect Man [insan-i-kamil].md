# Perfect Man [insan-i-kamil]

Insan-i-kamil, or the Perfect Man, describes an ideal of human spiritual completeness and, in some contexts, the fullest reflection of divine qualities in a human reality. The term can refer broadly to human potential or specifically to the unique station of a Manifestation of God; those uses should remain distinct. Perfection here concerns spiritual capacity and revelation, not an identity between a human being and the divine Essence.

The distinction between human perfection and the unique revelatory station connects to [the essential distinction of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4657EFC24412422099AA26E26F483158) and [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5). Broader accounts of humanity’s place in creation appear in [man as the sum and pinnacle of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBA5068DF2C24AE6A61E7337FF0B529D), while [special knowledge of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E500F3DB980EEA00468CEB52774C33B7) describes a particular revelatory capacity. The theological sense of perfection is further related to [infallibility and sinlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=46FD5CCF1C43454DB91FC516E5D3B7F7). Together these links distinguish the potential of human spiritual development from the singular station attributed to the Manifestation.
