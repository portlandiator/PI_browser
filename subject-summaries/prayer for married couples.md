# prayer for married couples

Marriage joins two people in a bond meant to sustain affection, mutual support, and shared spiritual life. Prayers for spouses ask for harmony, enduring love, protection, and a household shaped by worship and service. Many also hope for children who will contribute to the good of others. Their ideal of unity does not erase the partners’ individuality; it asks that companionship help both grow in fidelity, joy, and care for one another.

These petitions belong to the broader [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), giving a marriage its own devotional expression of unity and mutual support.
