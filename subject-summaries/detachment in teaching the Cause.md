# detachment in teaching the Cause

Teaching the Cause calls for freedom from worldly reward, personal advantage, and attachment to outcomes. The teacher’s purpose is to make God known, share the message kindly, and accept whatever response follows; steadfastness and reliance on divine assistance sustain that effort. Detachment does not mean harshness: a kindly tongue, respect for a hearer’s choice, and goodwill accompany energetic service. The work’s spiritual effectiveness is tied to the teacher’s purity, independence, and wholehearted devotion.

Detachment and [pure intentions in teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=93D1005763EE41F7B571D3F007DF5704) meet in the demand that the teacher’s heart be independent and the purpose wholly directed toward God.
