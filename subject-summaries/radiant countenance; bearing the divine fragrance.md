# radiant countenance; bearing the divine fragrance

A radiant countenance reflects an inner spiritual life through visible joy, composure, and conduct that attracts others to what is good. “Bearing the divine fragrance” extends the image beyond appearance: character and actions should diffuse kindness, faithfulness, and the teachings wherever a person goes. A face may be treated as revealing qualities, but the emphasis is not cosmetic beauty; it is inward orientation made perceptible in a person’s presence and behavior.

A radiant presence may accompany [joy and happiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), while its ethical substance is grounded in the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), whose actions make inner faith perceptible.
