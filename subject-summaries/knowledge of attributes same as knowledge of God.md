# knowledge of attributes same as knowledge of God

Recognition of a Manifestation is knowledge of God because divine qualities such as wisdom, power, and mercy become visible through that revelation. This knowledge concerns God’s signs and attributes, not direct comprehension of the unknowable divine Essence. The distinction keeps claims about knowing God within human limits while affirming that divine qualities can be recognized in the Manifestations.

The distinction between the Essence and its knowable signs leads directly to [God’s knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D49918E5C4014E13A7AD43BCB1E89561) and [the names and attributes of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9AB88215A3AC410692D350AE3BEB3A54): the former asks what can be said of divine knowing, while the latter concerns how qualities are disclosed without making the Essence comprehensible. The question of attributes and creation is sharpened by [God’s attributes requiring the existence of objects](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E64D5F25E34797A55642F2919C4317), and by accounts of divine influence present throughout creation in [divine attributes within all things](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B996EA76F6EC401A9148E1F0507C179A). These themes converge in [the Manifestation as the only route to knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3855920D804CC38601F6D85145019F), where knowledge is framed as recognition of divine revelation rather than access to the hidden Essence.
