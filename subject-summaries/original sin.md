# original sin

The doctrine of inherited guilt from Adam is rejected as incompatible with divine justice: descendants cannot fairly be punished for an ancestor’s act. The spiritual account instead interprets Adam’s “fall” as the attachment of spirit to material existence, a condition passed on as a tendency that can obscure humanity’s spiritual nature. This is a symbolic explanation of sin, not a claim that each person is personally culpable for Adam’s transgression.
