# love as fundamental; spiritual foundations of religion

Love is the spiritual foundation shared across religions and the force that binds humanity to God and to one another. It heals, consoles, and awakens virtue; without it, religious forms lose their purpose. Divine love can consume selfishness and illuminate the heart, while human love should appear as kindness, unity, honesty, and service. Religious laws may change across ages, but love endures as a fountain of spiritual life and the fruit expected from human character.

Love’s reach is reflected in the [power of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D64346EB795847B3817AB6A1A38C8ED9) and in the [pathways of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=552E8D5180B04AAB91BE86621F37CD9B), extending into [unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700). Its fire can purify the heart ([love as fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D)), while [truthfulness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and the [virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) give it ethical and spiritual form.
