# being a channel for the Holy Spirit in teaching the Cause

A teacher is a channel through which the Holy Spirit may quicken a seeking soul; the teacher does not control another person’s spiritual awakening. Consecration, humility, devotion, and the lived Bahá’í life prepare the individual for this service. Capacity develops through teaching itself, as knowledge and character grow with practice. Personal contact and trust matter, while divine confirmation—not the speaker’s talent alone—is regarded as the source of spiritual effect.

This work depends on [divine confirmations and spiritual capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), supports the wider [call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), and is centered on the activity of [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853).
