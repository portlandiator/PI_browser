# spiritual meetings; gatherings; devotional gatherings; feasts

Spiritual meetings bring people together for worship, fellowship, consultation, and shared service. Gatherings can strengthen love and unity while giving communities a setting for collective learning and action. Devotional meetings, feasts, and other occasions serve different purposes, yet each can nurture a sense of belonging and spiritual life. Their value comes from the quality of participation as much as from the meeting itself.
