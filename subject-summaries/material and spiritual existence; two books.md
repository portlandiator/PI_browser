# material and spiritual existence; two books

Material and spiritual existence are distinguished as related dimensions of life, each with its own needs and effects. Physical health, learning, civilization, and the created world belong to material existence; love, moral qualities, spiritual insight, and the Kingdom describe a spiritual register. The “two books” image presents creation and revealed scripture as corresponding records of divine meaning. Human well-being depends on attending to both dimensions, with spiritual life guiding material knowledge and action.

The “two books” image belongs with the layered [realms of being; three realms; five realms](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53907D2638954598879977C301F16196), since both describe dimensions or planes of existence. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and [second birth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8C84F900ED34DA98391B4A8593684E1) describe movement from material orientation toward spiritual life. [Symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450) offers a way to read the world and scripture as signs, while the references to [Saint Peter and Saint Paul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4A93A1AFA0B44305BB29CFD7BF83CFC4) and [tests and trials; sacrifice and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4) bring the contrast into narratives of faith and endurance. The [connection between material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61) directly addresses their relation, and [music and singing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4027C84B01D8438E9C7D92535DF6BAB7) appears as one form of spiritual nourishment. The texts also set spiritual conflict alongside [forces of light and darkness; integration and disintegration](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994), and social critiques alongside [critique of Western values; culture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC28DBB6808BE947DE1EE25ECB14F544). Ethical practice links this distinction to [detachment; severance; renunciation; patience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), while healing has both [health and healing; material and spiritual healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55) dimensions. [Spiritual communication; connections of the heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E), [walking the spiritual path; spiritual wayfaring; the virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and the continuing [soul; spirit after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313) carry the spiritual life into relationship, conduct, and what follows bodily existence. The distinction also resonates with [two aspects of the human soul; the higher and lower natures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5410D4DAE5454E8D81B13EC3E9DA2F23) and the [material world a reflection of the spiritual](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2863570D944B4AFB91F6F5A9413971B7). These links approach the material-spiritual relation through cosmology, ethics, experience, and symbol.
