# assistance to the poor and lowly; the orphan; the sick

The poor and vulnerable are entrusted to the care of those around them. Assistance means more than charity: it includes defending dignity, protecting rights, educating children, comforting the sick, and refusing to turn away from people in hardship. Wealth brings obligations, and generosity should relieve need without condescension. Orphans, the ill, the down-trodden, and the destitute deserve a refuge and practical support; communities are also urged to address conditions that allow poverty to deepen.

Care for the poor is a direct form of [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), often supported through [gifts and contributions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78) and sustained by [children’s education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4). The issue also touches the [moderation of wealth inequality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A8B7BAB865C1EDFF1E086B0B1C1C8B38) and broader [work for humanity’s betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed by a related entry on [serving humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80).
