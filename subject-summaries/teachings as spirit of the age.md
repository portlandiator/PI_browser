# teachings as spirit of the age

A new age brings new spiritual needs. Bahá’í writings describe the teachings of Bahá’u’lláh as a fresh life and a light suited to humanity’s present stage, offering principles that address modern conditions and unite peoples. This does not mean that every truth begins anew: the teachings renew enduring spiritual foundations while calling people beyond blind imitation toward investigation, knowledge, and action.

The greatness and influence of the Cause of this Day gives the Revelation’s wider context: its teachings are presented as a force for humanity’s renewal. The connection concerns the same age and source, while this topic stresses the teachings’ relevance to its needs.
