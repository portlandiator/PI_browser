# Word of God the cause of creation

The Word of God is the cause through which creation is brought into being by divine command. This creative Word is not ordinary speech, but the expression of divine will by which realities receive existence and order. Its role links cosmology with revelation, where the Word also gives life to spiritual understanding.

The creative Word is related to [the centrality and influence of the Word](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and to [its procession and appearance from God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=191AFB3E5AAB4E3BB80BBCC24D349113). Its connection with the Primal Will and revelation is explored in [the Word, Remembrance, and Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA). Together these subjects describe the Word as both a principle of creation and a vehicle of divine guidance.
