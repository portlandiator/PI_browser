# pure intentions in teaching the Cause

Pure intention gives teaching moral force. A teacher’s heart should be free of hypocrisy and self-interest, steady in purpose, and sincerely concerned for humanity’s good. Inner refinement is an ongoing effort rather than a claim to perfection; the teacher must work on personal faults before presuming to correct others. Love, truthfulness, peace of mind, and detachment help words reach listeners, while conduct should embody the same care as speech.

Purity of motive is closely tied to [detachment in teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5074FD02BF4C47E68CE9773D10D7AB7C), which frees the teacher from self-interest and supports sincere service.
