# origin of the soul; the soul as emanation

The soul originates through divine creation and emanation, proceeding from God without being a portion separated from the divine Essence. Its emergence in the body marks a new mode of existence, while its spiritual origin explains its capacity for knowledge, love, and return toward God.

The soul’s appearance in embodied life is further considered in [the relationship of the soul to the body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=52B1C3F2B9574C37BAF0ECCC8D5AB8C1), which distinguishes the soul’s reality from its bodily instrument.
