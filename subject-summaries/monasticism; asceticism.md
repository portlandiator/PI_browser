# monasticism; asceticism

Monastic withdrawal and severe physical austerity are not required for spiritual growth. Bahá’í guidance calls people to live among others, care for their health, and serve humanity through useful work; it also allows someone to devote time to teaching or service without turning that choice into monasticism. Detachment means freedom from selfish desire, not rejecting lawful blessings or punishing the body.

[Sacrifice of self](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0) describes devotion expressed through giving up self-interest for a higher purpose, and [work as worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D) shows how ordinary labor can become spiritual service. Neither requires retreat from society or contempt for material life.
