# day of rest

Friday holds a particular place as the designated day of rest in the Bahá’í calendar. The choice belongs to a wider religious pattern in which particular days acquire significance through the history of a dispensation, as Saturday did in Judaism and Sunday in Christianity. A day of rest sets a rhythm within ordinary life, linking relief from labor with remembrance, renewal, and the spiritual meaning of time.
