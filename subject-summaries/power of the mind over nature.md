# power of the mind over nature

Human intelligence enables people to understand and use natural forces rather than remain wholly subject to them. Through reason, knowledge, and invention, humanity can transform materials and conditions for constructive purposes. This power does not make people independent of nature or its laws; it reveals a capacity to investigate and act within the created order.

The mind’s capacity to understand natural processes is explored in [the mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C), while [human distinction from animals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB) concerns the rational and spiritual capacities that make such action possible. Together these subjects connect knowledge with humanity’s distinctive ability to respond creatively to nature.
