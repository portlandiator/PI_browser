# divine attributes are within all things; every atom

Every created thing bears a sign of God. From the smallest atom to the human soul, existence reflects divine qualities according to its capacity. The world becomes a book of wisdom, beauty, and mercy for those who learn to read it. These reflections reveal the Creator without making creation identical with Him. To recognize them is to look more attentively at the world and more respectfully at every being within it.

[Immanence and transcendence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6) clarify how divine signs can fill the world without the Essence being contained in it. [Emanation versus manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26BFCA2FC4E44906BAAB903F56228AB0) examines the language used to describe this relationship, distinguishing a source from what proceeds from it. The [all-pervading bounty of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9) explains why no being is excluded from receiving a share of divine gifts, while [interconnectedness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2FC11A99D6A34DFD93993D59F31450E8) places each being within a mutually dependent order. The [human soul as a mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) develops the special capacity to reflect the divine names and the need to cultivate that capacity. [Knowledge of attributes as knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A7BDCD83AE14B19B4D78332D456B1D3) brings these strands together: recognition grows through the qualities revealed in creation and, most fully, in the Manifestations, while their source remains beyond our grasp.
