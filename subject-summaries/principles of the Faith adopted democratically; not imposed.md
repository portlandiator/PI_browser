# principles of the Faith adopted democratically; not imposed

Bahá’í teachings are to be adopted through conviction and lawful, democratic choice, never imposed by seizing political power. Social transformation takes time as principles take root and mature. Believers may explain and exemplify the teachings, while individuals and communities remain free to decide whether to accept them.

No recorded one-hop connections are listed for this subject. The selections themselves distinguish personal adherence from a society’s lawful decision to adopt principles through its own constitutional processes.
