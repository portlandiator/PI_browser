# rejection of Muhammad

Muhammad’s mission met opposition from contemporaries whose expectations and interests were challenged by His message. He endured hardship as a new religious community formed around His teachings. The pattern of rejection resembles that faced by other Manifestations, while the circumstances of His mission and the history of Islam remain distinctive.

[Suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) connects Muhammad’s experience with the hardships endured by religious figures. [Suffering; rejection of Christ](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B2340566123BECE11972A4501C75B8C1) provides a parallel from Christian history, showing a recurring pattern of resistance to new revelation. The two subjects concern distinct communities and histories, but together invite comparison of how opposition shapes religious memory.
