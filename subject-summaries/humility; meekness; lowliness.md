# humility; meekness; lowliness

Humility and meekness recognize dependence on God and refuse pride in one’s own attainments. Humility belongs to faithfulness; the earth underfoot is the source of worldly prosperity, and humility in the Kingdom is a station of honor. Lowliness does not deny human potential; it releases self-importance so that service, learning, and spiritual growth can proceed without superiority.

Humility is expressed through [submission to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA), obedience to [laws of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=752881F17C2B4EE0BDA239B9D6517A45), and [kindliness in teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F7D359A1DBBF46B08E7168C2272A3D99). It accompanies [prayer for others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3), forms a [prerequisite for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), and can be shown through [gratitude for gifts](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78) and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). It depends on [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), leads to [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), and is strengthened by [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) along the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). [Stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) illustrate the attitude, while [spiritual emotions and susceptibilities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) guards against [pride](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=432DEC8FB4F94515B86F2E5396D09FB4) and deepens [self-knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3ED3EB2EFEBF43C9A36134E26FDB38EE). Humility nurtures [fellowship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A), may be voiced in [poetry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91840AE122AEC232D80E33023EC88DF), shapes the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), and receives [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B). It also appears in [prayer for forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0137285CF3DEE3ABF3193E5E857AE7DB), endurance of [trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and recognition of [‘Abdu’l-Baha’s station and authority](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315).
