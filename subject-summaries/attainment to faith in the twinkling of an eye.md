# attainment to faith in the twinkling of an eye

Attainment to faith in the twinkling of an eye describes a swift inward passage from spiritual remoteness to reunion with God. Divine bounty is astonishingly near, like an ocean; a single step of the spirit can traverse distances human effort alone cannot cross. Faith’s rapid attainment depends on willingness to respond to grace and turn decisively toward the revealed Word.

This swift attainment belongs to [walking the spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549).
