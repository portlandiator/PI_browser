# station of the Manifestation of God

The Manifestation of God is the human bearer of divine revelation, whose spiritual station makes God’s guidance known without making the divine Essence comprehensible. The concept includes both a human reality and a unique revelatory authority, and explains how divine attributes are reflected in a particular life and teaching. Successive Manifestations share a spiritual source while appearing in distinct historical circumstances and addressing the needs of their age.

The Manifestation’s relation to God raises questions of [personal versus impersonal divine reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0905F934B5D4402E84FCD0F0E325E0A7), [unity in the degrees of tawhid](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0CA85F9D98D4467C8454CE42CA5BB10D), and the difference between outpouring and disclosure in [the holy and most holy outpourings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5A1748B3FB14D8EA1B9992E6E0409BF) and [emanation versus manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26BFCA2FC4E44906BAAB903F56228AB0). Its universal spiritual source connects with [the Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D9002D8700F4A2D893B0869236D586F) and [the Word, Remembrance, and Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA). Further links explore [identity and relationship to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB), the sun and mirror metaphors in [Manifestation as sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35), [power of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0E46A9D425ED4F89AB54563B472E6EF6), and [Manifestation as mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D941821F406B4A20B1E60AD343B49717), and the revelatory voice in [speaking in the voice of Divinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25). The distinct authority and limits of this station are considered in [essential distinction from others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4657EFC24412422099AA26E26F483158), [obedience and authority](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10C0903A3A7048C193248AAA8629D4D4), [inability to comprehend God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B492C6CABFF847E5814269886AE0C4E9), and [twofold or threefold station](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=568978583E424499943612635F17DB7F).
