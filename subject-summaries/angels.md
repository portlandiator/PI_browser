# angels

Angels are spiritual beings and messengers who appear in sacred texts as agents of divine command, protection, or assistance. Some references may also use “angels” symbolically for spiritual powers or human capacities. Interpretation therefore depends on the passage and its scriptural setting.

The meaning of angelic language connects with [literal interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=87CAE620F6194480B20F2C8B053A5841), which considers how scriptural references can signify spiritual realities, and with [divine grace and spiritual capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), which describes forms of assistance attributed to the spiritual world.
