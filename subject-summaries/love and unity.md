# love and unity

Love and unity widen concern from self to family, community, nation, and ultimately all humanity. Love gives unity its warmth and motive; unity gives love a social form through cooperation, mutual aid, and the healing of discord. This ideal joins intimate kindness with a universal horizon, urging people to wish well even for strangers and adversaries. It does not require sameness: fellowship can embrace varied peoples while building a common human life.

[Human oneness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives love a universal scope, and that outward turn can produce [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) rooted in [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649). It calls for [encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) and practical [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), alongside [forgiveness and forbearance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810). Everyday [kindness across communities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80) supports [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed in a closely related topic on [human progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80), while [unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) preserves difference within fellowship. Such bonds are a basis for [world peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675) and require moral [counsel](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B). The unity of human purpose is linked to the [oneness of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD), with [love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7824F7FB65A4B75A74AC15AFD08EC5C) at its spiritual foundation and religion’s capacity to foster [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FFC8B795919D48FD82F6DD702B6A4A3E). It also demands [love for enemies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D614A134412E9DA43B77542E47091BD) and reflects the [universal reach of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25D5D8F955CC436AA7A1B20E51D912F3).
