# mind as intermediary between soul and body

The mind acts as an intermediary between the soul and the body, translating spiritual capacities into thought and bodily action. It apprehends realities and directs faculties, allowing a nonmaterial soul to operate through physical life. This role places the mind between spiritual and material dimensions without reducing either to the other.

The mind’s mediating function connects to [the interaction of material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61), [the relationship of the soul to the body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=52B1C3F2B9574C37BAF0ECCC8D5AB8C1), and [the human soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=65B32249C87F46A6BEA80B4FE5F9C1EE). Together these topics distinguish the functions of soul, mind, and body while explaining their coordinated activity in human life.
