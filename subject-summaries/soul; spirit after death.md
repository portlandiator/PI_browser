# soul; spirit after death

The soul continues to exist after bodily death and enters a spiritual mode of life that cannot be measured by physical conditions. The transition is described through images of progress, nearness, and recognition, while the quality of that life depends on spiritual capacities cultivated here. Death therefore changes the soul’s conditions without ending its existence.

The afterlife is framed by [chastisement and requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118), [eulogies and reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137), and [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), while Qur’anic language about passage between stages appears in [scriptural interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=19377C1C79EB4023BAD88C9FEA93B741). Spiritual progress connects to [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [detachment from the transient world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), and [the interaction of material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61). Further relations include [spiritual communication](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E), [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), [prayer for the departed](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6F48F0918CDBEBB76DD545292C255B68), and [fellowship among the friends](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A). Images and practices around death include [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450), [education and capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EEEFCF8268224145B6028CD4848913D1), [self-improvement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B33E14F5074E73B319D8CE6A17DDFAC), [the soul-body relationship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=52B1C3F2B9574C37BAF0ECCC8D5AB8C1), and [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Questions of continuing life relate to [the soul’s immortality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C691C4FF81064ADC8EE804AF197F35A9), while mourning and consolation are reflected in [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14). The broader distinction between [material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), the soul’s [second birth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8C84F900ED34DA98391B4A8593684E1), cautions around [occult and psychic phenomena](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=666ED6B326A8EDED8D4E3FF383F97184), and [proofs for immortality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9776E3373AB6EADEF00584AE6CE4505D) further frame what can be known about life after death.
