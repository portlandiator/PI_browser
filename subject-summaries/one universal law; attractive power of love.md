# one universal law; attractive power of love

Creation displays an order in which distinct beings remain connected through laws, relationships, and attraction. Attraction binds physical things, while love joins human hearts and sustains cooperation. Their relationship invites reflection on the unity of a world whose parts influence and depend upon one another. Unity preserves this diversity rather than erasing it. Physical attraction and spiritual love operate differently, yet both illuminate the importance of connection to the life of the whole.

The link to [teleology; goal-directed motion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B0B2077A5A44699B8F756D6A1F5CC7D) brings order into conversation with development: its example describes movement through degrees of perfection in the physical world. [Diversity an attribute; requirement of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D311B27723C741DCB7348644B473637F) supplies the complementary point that multiplicity and distinction belong to existence itself, rather than being defects to eliminate. The cited passage there describes composition and decomposition as continuing forms of material existence. [Law of cause and effect](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DF7D0EA999F44DBCA4F5C695B3851674) adds a reasoning pattern in which effects imply conditions of their existence. Together these connections situate universal law and attraction within an ordered, varied, and causally related world, while the excerpts differ in how explicitly they name a divine regulator.
