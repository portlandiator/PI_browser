# attaining the life of the spirit

Attaining the life of the spirit means living through spiritual realities rather than merely existing physically. Spiritual perfections are humanity’s birthright, and eternal life differs from outward bodily life; scripture’s raising of the dead can signify awakening to spiritual existence. This life is received through guidance and recognition, and it becomes the basis for happiness, service, and continued growth.

Life in the spirit is linked to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417). It entails [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218), the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), [living water](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992), and the action of [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853).
