# order; organization; tact; deliberation [tadbir]

Order, organization, tact, and deliberation make collective work effective without allowing procedure to overwhelm its purpose. Flexibility in secondary administrative matters protects the Faith’s aims from being obscured by minute details. Good organization creates clarity and coordination; tact adapts decisions to circumstances, while deliberation keeps attention on the principles and people the system is meant to serve.

Order is linked to [cleanliness and refinement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=930E4F8D8225422881E16A841A80DE02) and to [personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2), where careful arrangements guide action.
