# kindliness; humility; self-effacement in teaching the Cause

Teaching calls for kindliness, humility, and self-effacement. A teacher should not assume superiority or treat a listener as ignorant; truth can be explored together with respect, patience, and gentle explanation. If an invitation is refused, the teacher should avoid contention and ill feeling. Humility does not mean timidity: it allows confident service while directing attention away from personal status and toward the truth being shared and the dignity of the other person.

The central virtue in this practice is [humility and meekness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), which tempers speech and makes teaching inviting rather than coercive.
