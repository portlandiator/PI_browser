# relationship of the soul to the body

The soul and body are distinct but connected: the body serves as the instrument through which the soul acts in the material world. The soul is not simply a product of bodily parts, and its identity and powers exceed physical processes. Their relationship explains how spiritual capacities operate through embodied life.

The interaction of embodiment with spiritual life connects to [the material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61) and to [the soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313). The soul’s origin and relation to embodiment appear in [the soul as emanation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9A628005EF1D41DBB6DECA0975FD85FD), while [proofs for immortality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9776E3373AB6EADEF00584AE6CE4505D) address its persistence. [The human soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=65B32249C87F46A6BEA80B4FE5F9C1EE) and [the soul as a mirror of divine light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) describe its powers and potential, and [mind as intermediary between soul and body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E5D97C507CBE9BC48712BC3E937BE35) examines a mediating faculty.
