# symbolism

Symbolism uses concrete images to express spiritual realities that cannot be conveyed directly. Light, water, fire, journeys, and other figures can carry meanings beyond their literal forms, but interpretation depends on context and should not become arbitrary. Symbolic language connects scripture with inner transformation and lived spiritual practice.

Symbolism informs [interpretation of scripture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=58B72013D935E92718D07A6AB1DCFB78), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649).
