# sacrifice of the Manifestation of God redeems the world

The Manifestation willingly endures suffering and loss for the spiritual renewal of others. Imprisonment, abasement, and even death become occasions for revealing steadfast love and freeing people from the bonds of selfishness and division. The example of Christ illuminates a pattern of self-offering that also appears in other revelations. Redemption bears fruit as people respond to that love through changed lives, purified motives, and a deeper capacity for unity.

The emphasis on endured ordeals links this theme with [suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A), which appear as concrete forms of the Manifestation’s self-offering. The deeper pattern is named in [sacrifice of self; mystery of sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0): loss becomes an act directed toward the life and transformation of others. The connection is thematic—the passages here focus on the redemptive effects ascribed to the Manifestation’s sacrifice, while the neighboring subject gathers the wider spiritual language of sacrifice.
