# spiritualization of humanity in the future; a new race of men

The future spiritualization of humanity involves more than private piety: it means individuals and communities learning to live by justice, service, and the oneness of humankind. Bahá’í writings envision a new way of life supported by spiritual transformation, just institutions, and social development. The phrase “new race of men” points to transformed human character and relationships, not a biological category or a claim of superiority over other peoples.

Personal spiritual transformation is the inward aspect of this development; the unity of humanity expresses its collective basis. The forces of integration and disintegration describe the wider historical process in which such change takes place, while prophecies relate to expectations about its future. These connections keep the vision focused on ethical and social transformation.
