# astrology and numerology

Astronomy can describe real movements of the stars, including eclipses; astrology’s forecasts of personal fortune or coming events have no such authority. Numerological claims likewise confuse a number with what it counts: twelve apostles may be significant, but the number itself is not a hidden force. Divination lacks authority, even though created things influence one another within regular cycles.

The regularity of celestial movement belongs with [cycles in the physical and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E10CB3367945B283E91F7CC23BFD22), while the broader image of creation as an interdependent chain connects to [interconnectedness and the chain of being](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2FC11A99D6A34DFD93993D59F31450E8). These relations do not validate horoscope predictions or lucky-number beliefs.
