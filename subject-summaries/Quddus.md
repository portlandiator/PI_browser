# Quddus

Quddus was a leading Bábí figure, counted among the Letters of the Living and remembered for his devotion, learning, and role in the early movement. He accompanied the Báb on pilgrimage and later became an important leader among the Bábís. His life ended in the upheavals at Shaykh Tabarsi, and he is remembered as a martyr. The details of his role belong to the formative history of the Bábí Faith.

[Tahirih](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FEC8373274E6EBEF66637D65D1974647) was another prominent figure in the early Bábí community. Their connection places two influential leaders within the movement’s formative period, though their roles and histories were distinct.
