# all things are in motion; kinds of motion

Creation is dynamic: bodies move, organisms grow or decline, and inward life changes through effort and spiritual development. Bahá’í writings distinguish physical motion from the soul’s progress, sometimes describing continued effort as ascent and cessation as decline. Change is not random in this account but occurs within an ordered cosmos; devotional counsel and philosophical explanation each retain their own meanings.

Motion is related to heat in [heat and motion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50ABBFF644124BF0AD0846FDCB67B56C), where warmth is described as a cause of movement, and to [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), which places dynamic language in a devotional setting. [Degrees of spirit and the kingdoms of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D03BB2731AA9495289A5738021BFA48E) distinguishes kinds and levels of life, while [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) connects motion with moral betterment and concern for others. These links show how the idea moves from physical change toward spiritual development without collapsing those senses into one.
