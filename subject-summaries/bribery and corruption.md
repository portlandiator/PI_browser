# bribery and corruption

Bribery and corruption betray public trust and compromise justice. Officials should perform their duties with integrity, independence, and detachment from private gain; gifts or favors that influence decisions are not harmless courtesy. The standard extends to transparency in personal and public conduct, so even unfounded suspicion of fraud or intimidation is avoided. Fair administration depends on refusing corrupt inducements, while lawful compensation for service remains distinct from being bought to abuse authority.
