# study; deepening

Study and deepening call for patient, repeated engagement with scripture and its inner meanings. The guidance is to investigate the Holy Books word by word, seek the pearls within the ocean of divine words, and understand both the outward sense and the spiritual significance. Deepening is an active intellectual and devotional practice: it expands understanding while connecting the letter of a teaching with its effect on life.

Study is joined to the [importance of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAA2181CBE0F4BFFAAFD92CC0FCB75B4) and to [acquiring knowledge and the sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=157B1606343947178806DFE21D30B32B).
