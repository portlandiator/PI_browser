# steadfastness; perseverance; faithfulness

Steadfastness, perseverance, and faithfulness hold a person firm through opposition, uncertainty, and prolonged effort. Firmness is like an iron mountain and a magnet for divine strength. Believers must hold fast to truth, detach from distractions, and continue serving despite trials. Steadfastness is active constancy, sustained by prayer and trust rather than stubbornness or self-reliance.

Faithfulness includes refusing [Covenant-breaking](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42) and following the [Tree of Life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7CC32A1E2224C06A744204F2B89557C) toward [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775).
