# knowledge; recognition of God

Knowledge and recognition of God form the essence of religion and the foundation of spiritual understanding. Knowledge begins with divine unity and recognition that God’s Essence exceeds human comprehension; divine reality is known through its signs and attributes. True knowledge therefore joins intellectual understanding with humility, faith, and response to revelation.

Knowledge issues in the [twin duties of recognition and obedience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF1460687A9249CCAFEDE564FF3B9F06), and calls forth [gratitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD4052A3136042C4B86176AB2F552C55) and [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668). It equips a person for [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) and gives direction to [life’s purpose](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=857627CDE8FBEF82708BB212CB362A7F). Knowledge is animated by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), expressed in [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and deepened through [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). It provides a [foundation for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) and for [true spiritual knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=294776C0D86D489387DACD94A9927CEB), leading toward [life in the spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AE0799C6978C430E8B793BC7101E1A12) and recognition of the [human reality created in God’s image](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8EEDB198EF554EDAA21873DEC17AF79C). It is symbolized by [wine](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102), cultivated along the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and aided by [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E). [Living waters](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992) nourish it, while [worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85) obstructs it; [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8) and the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) guide it. [Reasoned argument](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12FD56FFF2C74384AF52D04E7CEE255F) has a role, though [true worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=173632E7BF104AB486EFBB383AF8DEAD) and the [presence of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82D2161C8BB4E087647B84F29B41D6D3) point beyond purely discursive knowledge.
