# superstition

An imagined omen has no power simply because people fear it: the evil eye and the meaning assigned to sneezing are called superstition, not reliable forces. Freedom from such beliefs clears the heart for assurance and spiritual perception. The rooster story makes the same point playfully: mistaking a coincidence for the cause of sunrise gives an ordinary creature an influence it does not possess.

The story’s mistaken attribution of sunrise to a rooster contrasts with the genuine [law of cause and effect](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DF7D0EA999F44DBCA4F5C695B3851674), where effects depend on real causes rather than fearful imagination.
