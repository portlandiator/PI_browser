# proclamation of the Cause (general or uncategorized)

Proclamation of the Cause calls people across religious communities—and women in particular—to hear the new message and enter its spiritual possibilities. Silence gives way to proclamation, the Call is raised, and the Truth summons all creation. Imagery of awakening, light, and glad tidings culminates in a vision of human solidarity and peace. Its scope is universal, while its invitations address distinct audiences directly.

The proclamation’s appeal to all peoples finds its social aim in [unity and oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D).
