# holy and most holy outpouring [fayd-i-aqdas and fayd-i-muqaddas]

Fayd-i-aqdas and fayd-i-muqaddas name two stages or modes of divine outpouring in philosophical accounts of creation. The most holy outpouring is associated with archetypal realities in divine knowledge, and the holy outpouring with their manifestation in the contingent world. Any emanated reality remains wholly dependent on God and does not share the pre-existence or unknowability of the divine Essence. The terminology belongs to specialized discussions and includes provisional renderings.

These stages belong to the wider discussion of [divine emanation and all-pervading bounty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9), and their account of inner articulation connects with [God’s love for His own Essence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=592F9B4AA5B5491F827732B4D4675681) and [oneness in ahadiyyih and wahidiyyih](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6B2E2534F05E41069B3E1A18F5B552D4). The boundary between divine Essence and creative act is central to [essential versus actional attributes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B8ED66ABF42C4C94B1950954F56B46F0), while [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) concerns the historical disclosure of divine guidance. The distinction between emanation and disclosure is also taken up in [emanation versus manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26BFCA2FC4E44906BAAB903F56228AB0). Together, these links situate the two outpourings within a vocabulary for describing creation and revelation while preserving the distinction between God and what depends upon God.
