# presence [liqa'] of Manifestation of God is presence of God

The presence or meeting of a Manifestation of God constitutes the presence of God because the Manifestation perfectly reflects divine attributes and bears divine revelation. This language concerns encounter with God’s signs and guidance through the Manifestation, not physical access to or comprehension of the unknowable Essence. It gives scriptural references to meeting God a revelatory meaning.

This sense of encounter follows from [the relationship of the Manifestation to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB) and [the Manifestation as mediator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=83C2E82C31DB4115A3CDF5884D0CB19E). [The Manifestation as the only route to knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3855920D804CC38601F6D85145019F) explains why meeting the Manifestation can be described as meeting God, while [knowledge and recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218) gives the broader theological context. These links preserve the distinction between divine Essence and its disclosure through revelation.
