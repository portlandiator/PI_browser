# procession; appearance of the Word from God

The procession or appearance of the Word from God describes divine utterance as proceeding by command without separating a part of God from the divine Essence. It is likened to speech proceeding from a speaker: the Word has a real relation to its source yet remains distinct from that source. This language explains how revelation and creation can proceed from God while preserving divine transcendence.

The Word’s creative role is developed in [the Word as cause of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3C7DBDDAC30A4CEDA9AF6EA4D195C374), while [the influence and centrality of the Word](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) concerns its spiritual effect in the world. Together these topics distinguish the Word’s procession from God from the separate question of how it shapes creation.
