# serving humanity; working for progress, betterment of the world

Human progress calls for service that benefits the whole human family. Its aims range from education and medical care to peace, social development, and the easing of hardship. The work is practical as well as spiritual: personal effort and visible deeds matter, and community initiatives can improve both Bahá’í life and the surrounding society. Social action has its own value and can complement teaching without being reduced to a tool for attracting converts.

This neighboring subject on [humanity’s progress and betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) shares a broad concern for service, while [assistance to the poor and lowly, orphan, and sick](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B118E279E521421DA6DE76C3D326B7A6) gives that concern an immediate form. [Guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1), good character as a [prerequisite for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), and [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) accompany [urgent action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), patience, and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C). [Trustworthiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3) supports this work, whose reach reflects the [universality of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25D5D8F955CC436AA7A1B20E51D912F3) and the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Its social horizon is [human oneness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), expressed through [kindness to people of every religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80), the example of [true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), and [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700), alongside the [oneness of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD). Care also includes [health and healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55). The work draws on the [principles of the Faith](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) and [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), including [children’s education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) and [curriculum](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DA23248649CEE53DD015F93A520C3B49), and seeks [universal peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675). [Useful occupations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C307803DE6C7E45E17BD19C083DE4620) connect livelihood with service; [science and religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=99656E1B1D7344D8930ACA530FDE210B) both advance knowledge. [Joy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), [encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), personal [service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), and broader [civilizational progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=15E8B3AB36AF495684116E6C5150950A) belong to the same vision, sustained by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), [prayer for others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343), and religion’s [social and personal purpose](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=29AF567C76864D208EA3BD39BC5CC7CF).
