# gambling and games of chance

Gambling is forbidden as a practice that risks wealth and can foster harmful habits. Games of chance are placed beside opium among conduct to avoid, reflecting concern for human well-being. The rule addresses gambling itself; it does not imply that every form of recreation or play is prohibited.

The recorded link to [alcohol, drugs, tobacco, and opium](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7D29F9186A9D4091811A4EA61499CEC8) reflects a shared concern with practices that can impair judgment or harm the body and social life.
