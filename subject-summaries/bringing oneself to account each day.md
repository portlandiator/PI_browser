# bringing oneself to account each day

Bringing oneself to account each day means weighing one’s actions regularly against justice and spiritual responsibility. A person is urged to examine every day and moment before the final reckoning, compare present conduct with yesterday’s, and assess decisions through equity. Daily self-accounting applies to rulers as well as individuals: authority carries a duty to judge fairly and direct others wisely.

Self-accounting is measured against [justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34), both personal and social.
