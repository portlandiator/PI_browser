# man is the sum; pinnacle; fruit of creation

Human nature gathers capacities found throughout creation and adds the power of conscious understanding and spiritual choice. The image of humanity as creation's fruit points to the unfolding of these possibilities, while the image of a summit emphasizes responsibility as well as distinction. Human worth does not depend on every person displaying equal abilities. It rests in a shared spiritual potential that education, effort, and divine guidance help bring into expression.

[Microcosm and macrocosm](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EA626E1123AE4CA9BDB62193BD55DE62) compares the inner human world with the larger cosmos, giving a parallel for the idea that many realities are reflected in humanity. [Man’s distinction from the animal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB) identifies capacities said to distinguish people, while [Evolution; human evolution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F7EC8954BFA749B4ACA3C4353E8A96CB) traces changing manifestations across human development. [Perfect Man](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=77E5DDB689E847A198C9D181A95A2B27) treats human form as a symbol of spiritual perfection. Together these connections explore humanity’s place through cosmic analogy, distinctive powers, development, and spiritual ideal.
