# Baha'u'llah

Bahá’u’lláh, founder of the Bahá’í Faith, is regarded by Bahá’ís as a Manifestation of God whose teachings renew religion for the modern age. Born into a noble Persian family, he became a follower of the Báb and later declared an independent mission after imprisonment and exile. His writings address spiritual life, social unity, justice, and the establishment of a peaceful global civilization. His life and teachings are central to Bahá’í identity.

[Early years; early ministry of Bahá’u’lláh](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F8F9C8FC270E02F85790BAA9EAE1BDE) traces his life before the later exiles. [Incarceration in the Siyah Chal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1F941B251E10EA4F406FDD64819512C9) and [Banishments of Bahá’u’lláh](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=78DB0A81C7FFE1423784B68876BBD5D4) describe the periods of imprisonment and exile that shaped his ministry. [Bahá’u’lláh in Sulaymaniyyih](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2E0BDF1117A9E8E28B95887851BB65EC) and [Banishment to; life in Baghdad](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD6AD7D972DCE3881CF23778EE78C347) cover his time in Iraq before his declaration. [Declaration of Bahá’u’lláh](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3411B39633A8E7CBBEE7284A878D8283) marks the public emergence of his mission. [Life in Constantinople](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7F59847655ADEAE2D09CC9CC9F5F4DE1) and [Life in Adrianople](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6580A1098A7242F9B29D4916F5B4F414) follow the successive exiles, while [Rebellion and misdeeds of Mirza Yahya; the Azalis](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=161EB5A0748AED25642FA886964B6304) describes conflict within the Bábí community. [Rejection by the people of the Bayan](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1219A74FADB7E7059B143DAF131CB386) concerns responses to his claim, and [Banishment to; life in ‘Akká](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4FAFE46E3FC7417BAC3D18A3C7D260C2) covers his final place of exile. [Mount Carmel](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DB38CEAE0E40EAE9C4AAFC8ADE5D2C13) connects his later life with the Holy Land, and [Ascension of Bahá’u’lláh](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AA92E315F90E73014DE6159A4A9C633) with the close of his earthly life. [Greatest Name](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C01E406DEFE6475ABF35B7FABEA3D636) relates to a central symbol associated with his revelation. Together these links trace his life from early years through revelation, exile, community conflict, and legacy.
