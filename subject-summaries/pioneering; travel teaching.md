# pioneering; travel teaching

Pioneering carries the teachings beyond familiar surroundings. Travel teaching can bring guidance to communities without teachers, open new areas to spiritual life, and place a believer in the role of a stranger far from home. The work calls for detachment, endurance, and willingness to go where needs are greatest; movement itself can sow spiritual seeds. When direct teaching would cause difficulty, encouraging and educating local believers may better serve the community.
