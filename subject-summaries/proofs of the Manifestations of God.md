# proofs of the Manifestations of God

A Manifestation's mission becomes recognizable through His revelation, His character, and the power of His teachings to transform people and society. Revealed verses hold a central place among these proofs, alongside rational arguments and signs within human life and the wider world. Evidence alone does not guarantee recognition: the seeker's openness and freedom from prejudice also matter. The search for proof therefore calls for attentive inquiry and an honest examination of one's own assumptions.

The image of the [Manifestation of God as sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) presents revelation as a source of light whose effects can be observed in spiritual and social life. The link to [authentication; disposition of the Sacred Writings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5DC49AA51AAA48EFACCCC2272661585B) points to the textual evidence through which a claim may be examined. Proofs also occur within a history of progressive revelation, connecting to the [duration of the Baha'i cycle; future divine revelations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1DECA0446C2D4E74BF311F15691FB7A7), where the forms of evidence for future Manifestations are discussed. The [lack of formal education of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=ABCDEC1823254906B8333F43C16877E4) is offered in some accounts as evidence that revealed knowledge did not arise through ordinary study. Finally, the [Word of God; influence and centrality of](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) gathers the central role of revealed utterance, including verses described as a direct proof. These links represent varied lines of argument rather than a single test applied uniformly across every passage.
