# teaching the Cause; call to teach

Teaching the Cause is a spiritual responsibility carried out through wisdom, kindness, clear speech, and exemplary conduct. It does not depend on eloquence alone: teachers are urged to embody the principles they share, prepare themselves inwardly, and persist without coercion. Prayer and trust accompany practical effort, while teaching must respect each hearer’s freedom and avoid creating division. The task reaches beyond recruitment toward service, unity, education, and the diffusion of a message intended for all humanity.
