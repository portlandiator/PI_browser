# condition of search

The condition of search requires patience, sustained effort, and freedom from whatever distracts the seeker from truth. The Valley of Search sets no quick deadline: a wayfarer must not lose heart even after a hundred thousand years. Seeking is likened to diving for pearls in an ocean whose treasures require personal striving; the seeker must loosen attachment, examine reality, and pursue truth with all available ability.

Search develops [capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8867A9B4875C48BB846B800856E218D7) and seeks [guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1), [certitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C306A880A1774D0FBF02956CCEAF45FF), and [independent investigation of reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30A42A15DD044876BA9A0BA8D1D60330). It demands [sacrifice of self](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0), [effort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB047A0483F7446F98969E0BB0A9A2A0), [detachment and patience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), and commitment to the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Success depends on [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and brings [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
