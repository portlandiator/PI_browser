# Theosophy

Theosophy is a modern spiritual movement that explores hidden realities, religious wisdom, and the relationship between humanity and the divine. Bahá’í writings and guidance sometimes address Theosophists as people open to spiritual inquiry, while encouraging respectful engagement and dialogue. The traditions remain distinct, and shared interest in spiritual questions does not make their teachings identical. Theosophy has also encountered Bahá’í ideas through dialogue and shared interest in spiritual inquiry.
