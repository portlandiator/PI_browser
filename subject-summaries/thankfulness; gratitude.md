# thankfulness; gratitude

Thankfulness and gratitude recognize the bounty received and respond with praise, service, and generosity. Even constant thanksgiving could not discharge the full obligation owed for divine bestowals. Gratitude is therefore more than polite acknowledgment: it turns recognition into a sustained orientation that strengthens faith, supports others, and remains present through hardship.

Gratitude deepens [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218) and is pictured through [wine as transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102). It can acknowledge a [gift](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78), become [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and motivate [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). It sustains [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) through [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and [Covenant firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633), including amid [trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and [imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A). Gratitude also opens toward [forgiveness and mercy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5CF47B6B008F461986A90EF7E6BF176E).
