# poems and quotation from poetry

Poems and quotations from poetry use rhythm, image, and compressed language to express spiritual insight, grief, love, and moral counsel. Poetry can make abstract ideas memorable and emotionally resonant, while quotation places a new utterance in conversation with an existing literary tradition. Its figurative language invites attention to both sound and meaning.

Poetry appears in [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) and offers [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) amid [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14). Its spiritual tone connects with [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [the material world as reflection](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2863570D944B4AFB91F6F5A9413971B7), [mystical discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623) and [mystical themes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6259D6333FC7EE132D6AD0D77800E8FC), as well as [the pain of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A230B24E88D943A79A527E42B4172FE0) and [tests and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4). Historical and ethical links include [‘Abdu’l-Bahá’s youth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B09DF372555FE0EF51DAE5C0119AD51E) and [self-sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0).
