# degrees of faith; certitude

Degrees of faith describe successive inner stations, from initial search through love, sanctification, and service. Four stages of the heart and several kinds of mystic wayfarer mark distinct degrees of faith. Faith is therefore not simply present or absent; its depth develops through recognition, purification, surrender, and increasingly active service to God.

The degrees unfold through the [condition of search](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA), deepen [knowledge of self](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3ED3EB2EFEBF43C9A36134E26FDB38EE), and take place in the [inmost heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6843E7D91F1248EE96FCE5A4E6B06155). They can lead to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
