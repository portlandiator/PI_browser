# confidence; courage; faith in teaching the Cause

Confidence gives a teacher courage to speak with dignity even when alone, uncertain, or surrounded by apathy. Reliance on God supplies strength beyond personal capacity, and prayer for spiritual assistance can steady the speaker before beginning. The expected success may not be immediately visible; the image of a seed becoming a fruit-bearing tree encourages hope and perseverance. Courage here is cheerful conviction, not forcefulness or disregard for listeners.
