# laws of marriage and dowry

Bahá’í marriage law regulates consent, the marriage contract, dowry, and the timing of the wedding. The Kitáb-i-Aqdas addresses marriage and limits on wives, while later authoritative interpretation explains how the condition of justice bears on those provisions and directs monogamy. Other rules specify the spouses’ presence, consent, and witnesses, with dowry varying under stated conditions. Provisional renderings differ, so the revealed law and authorized interpretation should be distinguished from individual translations.

These marriage and dowry rules are related to [Islamic law and jurisprudence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4EC3B48779BCE95B8D63B0C30835E863), within a broader history of religious legal traditions.
