# proofs for the existence of God

God’s existence is inferred from order, causation, human dependence, and signs of perfection in the world. Rational inquiry can establish that a Creator exists without comprehending the divine Essence. This distinction leaves room for spiritual perception and faith alongside reasoning, and directs attention to evidence rather than inherited claims alone.

The emphasis on evidence leads to [rational arguments](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12FD56FFF2C74384AF52D04E7CEE255F), which treats reasoned inquiry into divine questions, and [the mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C), which describes investigation as a means of uncovering realities in nature. Together these subjects help clarify the role assigned to human reasoning: it can infer causes and study signs, yet it does not contain the reality of God within a concept. The excerpts also distinguish material investigation from inquiry into spiritual realities, presenting both as fields where disciplined attention matters. The resulting argument is affirmative about evidence and cautious about claims to comprehend the divine Essence.
