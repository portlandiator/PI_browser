# Primal Will and the Word; Remembrance; Manifestation of God

The Báb’s title “Remembrance” links the Primal Will and Word with a Manifestation of God in a particular dispensation. Recognition of the Remembrance is urged through signs in scripture, the world, and human hearts. The Remembrance is distinguished from God while occupying a central role in revelation and guidance. References to the Báb and provisional renderings retain their particular textual settings.

This subject directly joins [the Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D9002D8700F4A2D893B0869236D586F) with [the Manifestation as mediator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=83C2E82C31DB4115A3CDF5884D0CB19E), whose role is described as a veil through which the worlds are known. The creative significance of divine utterance appears in [the Word of God as cause of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3C7DBDDAC30A4CEDA9AF6EA4D195C374), while [God’s self-description and self-remembrance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=04F924E0CC51EDF12DBB02871348AD74) concerns divine testimony prior to creation. The title’s historical association with the Báb is explored in [the Báb as the Primal Point](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3633354029CFE10F551F7CA219518DAC), and its broader revelatory meaning connects with [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5). Finally, [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853) appears in related accounts of revelation and spiritual confirmation. Together the links place “Remembrance” at the intersection of cosmology, scripture, and the Báb’s mission.
