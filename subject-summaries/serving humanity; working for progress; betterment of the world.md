# serving humanity; working for progress; betterment of the world

Humanity’s betterment joins spiritual purpose to concrete work: education, health, social development, peace, and the relief of need. Service reaches beyond one town or nation, seeking the welfare of all people; it also calls for patient effort and deeds rather than words alone. Community development can strengthen both a Bahá’í community and its wider surroundings, while remaining a valuable undertaking in its own right, not merely a means of enrolling believers.
