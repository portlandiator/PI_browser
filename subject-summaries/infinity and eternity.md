# infinity and eternity

Infinity and eternity describe the boundlessness of God’s existence, power, and creative activity. Beginninglessness and endlessness are also applied to creation, with divine attributes such as Creator and Sustainer implying an ongoing relation with created beings. Some distinguish spiritual eternity from temporal cycles within the material world. The topic therefore considers both the infinite reach of the worlds and the continuity of creation, while preserving the distinction between God and what depends on Him.

Eternity is linked to [continual renewal of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C213F67401F9445F8429A5F2621563E5), since an unending creative process takes the form of recurring renewal. The [God's attributes require the existence of objects](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E64D5F25E34797A55642F2919C4317) theme frames creation as a condition for the eternal manifestation of divine names and attributes. The [preexistence of the Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAC65ADBDE024CAFA9F155EDDCCA0C1F) applies beginninglessness to a particular spiritual station, while [celestial spheres (aflak); planets; astronomy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EC85462E1B8045CBBD2710350E2D29CD) connects cosmic extent with physical worlds and cycles. Finally, [divine emanation is ceaseless; eternal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=68137F318C6144AAAF17C8290F33061E) describes the continuing outpouring of divine grace. These relationships address different dimensions of eternity—creation, revelation, cosmic scale, and divine bounty.
