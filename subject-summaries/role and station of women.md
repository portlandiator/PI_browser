# role and station of women

Women have a full role in spiritual life, family, education, and the advancement of society. Their capacities and contributions should not be limited by inherited customs that treat them as inferior. Equal dignity requires practical opportunities to learn, lead, and serve. Social progress depends on allowing women to develop and exercise their abilities in every field.

[Equality of men and women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55D2FC30AFA84B35ACE7F2ADC2E8DD0E) states the principle that underlies women’s full participation. [Praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) reflects the direct affirmation of women’s spiritual lives, while [Critique of Eastern values; culture](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F9C794BCA51BED7F0C2DC8620BC743F9) situates the issue within changing cultural practices. [Education of children; moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) connects women’s role with the development of the next generation, and [Teaching the Cause; call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) points to their active part in community life. These links connect women’s station with equality, education, cultural change, and service.
