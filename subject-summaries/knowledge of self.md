# knowledge of self

Knowledge of self grows through turning toward God and serving other people rather than endlessly searching inward. The selected examples include Christ’s teaching that the Kingdom is within, a philosophical account of the soul’s longing for the Good, and counsel that service deepens self-understanding. Self-knowledge therefore involves humility and spiritual discipline, not a claim to secret insight or pride in one’s attainments.

Self-knowledge relates to [degrees of faith](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C306A880A1774D0FBF02956CCEAF45FF), while warning against [pride and claims to inner knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=432DEC8FB4F94515B86F2E5396D09FB4). It involves [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), and [self-discipline](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3B33E14F5074E73B319D8CE6A17DDFAC), and is tested through [suffering and trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4). Its deepest horizon is [God’s love for His own Essence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=592F9B4AA5B5491F827732B4D4675681).
