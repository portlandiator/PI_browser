# religion as source of love and unity

Religion should unite hearts, cultivate spirituality, and heal social conflict. When it produces hatred, injustice, or division, it contradicts its own purpose; teachings are likened to medicine whose value lies in restoring health. Fellowship and nearness to God are therefore practical tests of religion’s effects. Its shared principles across traditions should lead to love and agreement, while scientific reason and moral reality guard against empty profession or harmful dogma.

Religion’s unifying aim is part of its [personal and social purpose](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=29AF567C76864D208EA3BD39BC5CC7CF), expressed through [Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) and respect for the [truth of earlier scriptures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=193E825FA96F443BBA9ABC0E163E0F11). It should produce [love and unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700) and conform to [reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D3C0575BFA942CA95AB98EA453F1294).
