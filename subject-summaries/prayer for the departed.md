# prayer for the departed

Prayer for the departed entrusts a deceased person to divine mercy and asks for forgiveness, spiritual progress, and nearness to God. It also gives mourners words for grief: sorrow is acknowledged, while patience and consolation are sought for family and friends. The prayers often remember a person’s faith and service, yet their central appeal is not a human verdict but the boundless generosity and pardon of God.

These prayers are an instance of the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), and they reflect belief in the continuing life of the [soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313). Their requests for pardon and their comfort for survivors directly meet [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) amid [grief and lamentation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14). Memorial [eulogies and reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137) may accompany them, recalling a life shaped by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) and placing earthly loss against the [transience of the physical world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C). Many petitions seek [forgiveness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0137285CF3DEE3ABF3193E5E857AE7DB), honor a departed person’s [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and use wine imagery associated with [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102).
