# degrees of spirit and the kingdoms of existence

Mineral, plant, animal, and human life are distinct degrees within a connected order of existence. Each kingdom has characteristic powers and limits, and those differences shape how spirit and capacity are described. Hierarchy marks distinctions of kind or degree, not simply a moral ranking. This framework makes it possible to consider continuity across creation while recognizing limits that prevent one kingdom from being reduced to another.

[The animal kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2E53F4A56442E16B5B7BC0902E36C585) provides concrete examples of one degree, including comparisons between animal and human capacities. [All things are in motion; kinds of motion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4CB12B78171B4DC2A79D934CD45898C5) describes development and ascent across different levels, giving the hierarchy a dynamic dimension. [Realms of being; three realms; five realms](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53907D2638954598879977C301F16196) extends the discussion to broader spiritual classifications. Together the links connect kingdom distinctions to both change and larger maps of reality.
