# twin duties of recognition and obedience

Recognition and obedience are twin duties: the believer must acknowledge the Manifestation of God and follow what has been ordained. Faith is not complete in recognition alone, since revealed truth calls for action and observance; nor is obedience detached from recognition, because its meaning comes from knowing the source of divine command. Together they join belief, law, and lived commitment.

The paired duties grow from [knowledge and recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218), relate to [the laws of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=752881F17C2B4EE0BDA239B9D6517A45), and require the [spiritual qualities needed for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67).
