# prayer for justice and fairmindedness

Justice gives each person what is due and resists the use of power to oppress. Prayers for justice ask rulers and communities to act fairly, seek truth without prejudice, protect the vulnerable, and replace tyranny with equity. They may also ask that wrongdoers recognize their conduct and change. Fairmindedness here is both a personal virtue and a public standard: right judgment should shape relationships, institutions, and the treatment of those with less power.

These petitions join the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) to a concrete moral demand: that fairness govern conduct and public life.
