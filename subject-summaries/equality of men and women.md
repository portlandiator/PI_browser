# equality of men and women

Women and men are equal in spiritual worth and should have equal opportunity to develop their capacities. A society cannot reach its full potential while one half of humanity is held back through custom or lack of education. Equality calls for changes in family, education, and public life, while recognizing that equal dignity does not require identical experience. The aim is a balanced human community in which both women and men contribute fully.

[Role and station of women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=95E0DCD23ED9E61EE2BC99ECDBC8D8C0) explores women’s contribution and standing in religious and community life. [Lists; enumerations of Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) places equality among a wider set of teachings, and [House of Justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A801789EBB804ABB86C36BB8CAB50FED) raises the distinct rule reserving membership of the Universal House of Justice to men, alongside the affirmation that this does not imply male superiority. [Unity; oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives equality a broad social foundation. [Teaching the Cause; call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) and [Greatness and influence of the Cause; of this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7) show women participating in the life and spread of the Faith. Together these links connect equality to personal agency, community structures, and humanity’s shared life.
