# health and healing; material and spiritual healing

Health involves the body and the spirit, and healing may draw on both medical care and prayer. Physical illness calls for competent treatment, while spiritual life can offer courage, comfort, and meaning. These approaches are complementary rather than interchangeable: prayer does not replace medical knowledge, and medical care does not answer every spiritual need. Healing joins practical care with a broader concern for human well-being.

[Manifestation of God as divine physician](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4C00027D22054CDE8AACBF0F817122C2) uses healing as an image for spiritual guidance. [Power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) relates prayer to inward development, while [Being a source of light; guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1) connects healing with direction and hope. The aims of [serving humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2) and [working for the betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80) place health within collective welfare. [Moderation; frugality; simplicity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02B4E611F3EC4C52AD11023CE5588FA7) connects well-being with balance; [Transcending the material condition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=73709CA9F1694378BFE128A306C8D7CC) and [Walking the spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) relate health to spiritual purpose. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) links inner change with service, while [Principle of balance; moderation; equilibrium](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CD9C3EBD7DB44EFF9DDFF8598C7369FF) emphasizes coordination. [Teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), [Happiness; joyfulness; joy and sorrow](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), and [Personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2) offer social, emotional, and practical dimensions. [Prayer for healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D0935698C4CEEBA12E206FCBCA340547) addresses illness directly; [Power and centrality of the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA) concerns spiritual unity, and [Material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B) names the two domains of healing. Together these links connect medical care, spiritual resources, and human service.
