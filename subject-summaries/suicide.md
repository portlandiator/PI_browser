# suicide

Human life is a trust from God, to be preserved even through profound suffering. Bahá’í teaching forbids suicide and urges patience in the face of circumstances that seem unbearable, remembering that hardship can pass. Continued life leaves open possibilities for service, spiritual growth, and renewed purpose. Devotion calls for safeguarding life and using it for the good of others.
