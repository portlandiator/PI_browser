# power of imagination; thought; right intention

Imagination and thought can shape intention, attract spiritual confirmations, and influence the results of action. Good intentions and sound thoughts attract confirmations; devotion to one’s vocation achieves much, and good thoughts belong with good words and deeds. The mind’s inward images matter because they orient conduct; right intention becomes fruitful when it is embodied in diligent, constructive work.
