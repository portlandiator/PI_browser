# mission of the Manifestation of God in the world

The Manifestation of God comes to guide, educate, and renew humanity. His mission is not self-aggrandizement or praise but the delivery of divine teachings, the awakening of spiritual capacities, and the liberation of people from narrow material impulses. Through revelation, a new spirit and understanding can enter human life, advancing civilization, peace, and the unity of religions. The sufferings of the Manifestations are borne for humanity’s benefit, while their authority and example direct people toward God.

This mission serves [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) and makes the Manifestation an [educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=54135CB53EEE434D8B77F1C91F81A26C), awakening the soul as a [mirror for divine light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F). Its purpose gives meaning to [suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) and relates to the [mission of ‘Abdu’l‑Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315).
