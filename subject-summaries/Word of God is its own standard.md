# Word of God is its own standard

The Word of God is its own standard: its truth and authority are not measured by human conventions or by the approval of its hearers. Its teachings provide criteria for understanding and evaluating religious claims, while human interpretations remain subject to examination. The principle directs attention to the Word itself as the source of guidance.

The Word’s independent authority contrasts with [the transience of the physical world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), orienting judgment toward enduring spiritual guidance rather than passing standards.
