# effort; striving

Effort and striving turn spiritual intention into sustained action. Believers are urged to labor ardently, persevere in the Cause, and devote their abilities to God and the needs of others. Striving is not merely exertion for its own sake: it is directed toward service, steadfastness, the search for truth, and the development of a life responsive to spiritual guidance.

Striving bears fruit in [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE). It belongs to the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) and [condition of search](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA), draws on [living waters](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992), and is animated by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649).
