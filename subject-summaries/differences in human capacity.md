# differences in human capacity

People differ in aptitude, temperament, and readiness, while education and experience influence how those capacities develop. Difference does not establish moral worth: people may learn and act in distinct ways while sharing human dignity. Social arrangements should accommodate varied abilities without turning them into rigid hierarchies or barriers to participation.

[Maintenance of degrees in society](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDA2B50C1774485DAFFBA307BEC98470) considers differentiated roles in social life, while [Diversity an attribute; requirement of existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D311B27723C741DCB7348644B473637F) places difference within the broader variety of creation. [Divine grace, bounty, confirmations; spiritual capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) connects capacity with spiritual assistance, and [Unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) asks how difference can coexist with unity. [Progressive revelation; renewal of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A868B8CCFDF44B2890211A0FAFE9F2B9) links readiness to changing historical capacities; [Development of capacity and readiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8867A9B4875C48BB846B800856E218D7) emphasizes cultivation. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) gives development an ethical direction, while [Human reality created in the image of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8EEDB198EF554EDAA21873DEC17AF79C) grounds human worth in spiritual potential. [Physical limitations of the Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12119CC76EF849FF97AEEF4858C87628) illustrates that capacity can vary by station, and [Education and the development of capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EEEFCF8268224145B6028CD4848913D1) shows how learning can bring abilities forward. Together these connections distinguish innate variation, development, social organization, and shared dignity.
