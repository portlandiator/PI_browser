# love, unity, and fellowship among the friends; in the Cause

Love among the believers gives the Bahá’í community its spiritual strength. Genuine fellowship joins hearts in mutual service, makes hardship easier to bear, and opens possibilities beyond the resources of any individual. It grows through generosity, consideration, shared joy, and the refusal to dwell on one another's faults. Such closeness has a wider purpose: a community united in love can help demonstrate and extend the oneness of humanity.

The [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and the [virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) make fellowship a daily practice, strengthened by [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), [self-sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0), [forgiveness and forbearance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F28151459776E3BE109C95C138177810), and [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B). [Service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) gives affection practical form, while [exhortations and counsels](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B) and the [call to unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FE8395896269EDE56E9A05BA5E15262E) repeatedly direct attention to reconciliation. [Firmness in the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633) and [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) sustain shared commitment; [consultation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25A32FCFEDFE4DDF82D196BFF21A9DFC) and [unity of thought and belief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A4AA0909671F483EB793112083AAB33D) help turn different views into common action. The [Nineteen-Day Feast](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=57BF058414414E9B8ADC4026619BDFCB) and other [spiritual gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) nurture [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) and a collective capacity to receive [divine grace and confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), supporting [community building](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D2A37FC072734EADAA2D0F4A2BC2B5B0). The [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) and [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) depend on this living example. The connection with [fellowship with the wayward and ungodly](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C8C2297FED2AE873B7C3387A09CF2ADB) raises the discernment needed where companionship undermines spiritual life, alongside the wider obligation of [kindness and love toward all](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F8339A44D4ED49B8A05EF89719DBCE80). Spiritual bonds also give meaning to reflection on the [soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), reaching beyond physical proximity. Ultimately, fellowship within the community serves the [oneness of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D), expanding love beyond the circle of fellow believers.
