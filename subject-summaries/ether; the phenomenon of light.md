# ether; the phenomenon of light

In historical physics, ether was a proposed subtle medium through which light, heat, and electricity could travel. This hypothesis belongs to older scientific models, not current consensus. Light also names spiritual understanding: physical illumination and the light of faith are distinct realities, even when one serves as a metaphor for the other.

[Light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B68A1DE8700E8697ABFF0E02CF7F845) provides the closest conceptual companion: its example uses the image of light dispelling night, which can function both literally and spiritually. The present subject’s excerpts likewise move between visible illumination and inward understanding, while also offering a physical account of light through etheric vibration. Together they show how one phenomenon can support several kinds of explanation and metaphor, without making the physical and spiritual meanings interchangeable.
