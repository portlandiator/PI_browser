# two aspects of the human soul; the higher and lower natures

The human soul has higher spiritual capacities and lower attachments associated with material desire. These aspects describe competing tendencies within human life rather than two separate souls: people can turn toward reason, love, and spiritual growth or become absorbed in appetite and worldly concerns. Moral and spiritual development strengthens the higher capacities.

Human development across stages is considered in [evolution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F7EC8954BFA749B4ACA3C4353E8A96CB), while [human distinction from the animal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB) concerns capacities that enable reason and self-transcendence. [Love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), [material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) relate to the soul’s higher orientation, while [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) represents the spiritual horizon toward which that development can turn.
