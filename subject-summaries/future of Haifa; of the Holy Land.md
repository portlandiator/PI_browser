# future of Haifa; of the Holy Land

Bahá’í writings anticipate a future in which Haifa, ‘Akká, and the Holy Land flourish and become destinations for pilgrims. They speak of gardens, expanded shrines, and rulers coming to visit sacred places. These images combine hopes for physical development with the spiritual significance of the region; they are future-facing expectations, not descriptions of present conditions.

Shrines and holy places are the focus of pilgrimage and sacred geography in these expectations. The future of monarchy appears in the image of rulers visiting the shrines, while prophecy and fulfillment connect the anticipated transformation with scriptural expectation. These links concern religious meaning and future hopes, not verified present-day events.
