# chastity and purity

Chastity and purity mean turning away from sensual desire and guarding the heart for spiritual purpose. Chastity is shown by passing through a “city of pure gold” or encountering beauty without being ruled by appetite; guidance for youth joins purity with sound judgment, parental advice, and tolerance. Such restraint is a positive discipline of freedom, not contempt for people or the body.

This discipline depends on [purity of heart and sincerity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098) and is one expression of [walking the spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549).
