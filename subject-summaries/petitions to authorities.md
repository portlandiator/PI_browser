# petitions to authorities

Petitions to authorities appeal for protection, mercy, and justice in concrete situations. They protest mistreatment by government, plead for innocent people facing death, and ask sovereigns to secure the petitioners’ safety. Other requests seek the recovery of property from usurpers. Such petitions use the language of supplication while making specific claims about public responsibility and the protection owed to vulnerable people.

These appeals address the [status of kings and the future of monarchy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2CA524B216BC4EB4BA63A2832033F918), whose authority carries responsibility for the safety and welfare of their subjects.
