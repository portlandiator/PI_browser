# fewness of numbers not regarded in teaching the Cause

A small number of teachers does not limit the reach of a spiritual message. Sincerity, energy, and divine confirmation matter more than headcount; a modest gathering can grow over time and influence many communities. Early religious history offers examples of beginnings made by only a few believers. Rather than be discouraged by limited numbers, teachers should attend to the quality of hearts and the work before them, trusting that growth can follow faithful effort.
