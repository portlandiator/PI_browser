# Christian doctrine and practice

Bahá’í interpretations of Christianity address the Lord’s Supper, Christ’s command to love enemies, vicarious atonement, the Paraclete, church hierarchy, and changes in worship such as the introduction of bells. Some arguments dispute later Christian theology or institutional practice directly. These claims reflect a Bahá’í perspective and should not be taken as neutral descriptions of what all Christian communities believe.

[Christ; Christianity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FA444A710AD4FADBE7A093449A77252) provides the broader religious and historical setting for specific doctrines. [Idolatry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6342253C2012E68DB1F250FB4CDDD104) connects doctrinal discussion with questions about worship and the proper object of devotion. Together these links situate Christian teaching within a wider comparison of belief and practice.
