# prayer for assistance; intercession

Supplication asks for aid, confirmation, protection, and wise outcomes for oneself and others. Its language joins humility before divine strength with active hope: a petitioner seeks not only relief but the ability to serve, remain steadfast, and do what is good. Intercession extends this concern beyond the individual, as one person may pray for many. Requests remain entrusted to divine wisdom, whose answer may fulfill a need in a way the petitioner did not foresee.

Intercession participates in the wider [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687): the same communion that strengthens an individual can hold loved ones and communities in its concern.
