# composition and decomposition

Material forms arise as elements combine and pass away as those combinations separate or change. Birth, growth, and dissolution belong to this changing order, where the organization of parts gives each composite thing its particular form. The disappearance of a form need not mean the absolute annihilation of everything that composed it. Composition and decomposition thus illuminate both the transience of individual things and the continuity underlying their transformations.
