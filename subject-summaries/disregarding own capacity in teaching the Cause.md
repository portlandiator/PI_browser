# disregarding own capacity in teaching the Cause

Teachers are urged to begin before they feel fully qualified. Awareness of personal weakness should not become an excuse for inaction; striving to serve can itself develop capacity, while reliance on God keeps success from becoming a matter of self-credit. The need of people to hear the message is greater than the teacher’s sense of inadequacy. Patience matters too: effort may bear fruit slowly, and perseverance is valued even when immediate results are limited.

This counsel belongs to the broader [spiritual prerequisites of teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), emphasizing trust, humility, and active service.
