# Satan; the Evil One

Satan or the Evil One appears in religious language as a figure of temptation, opposition, or spiritual adversary. Interpretations vary: such language may be read literally in some traditions or symbolically as a description of human error and harmful impulses. The meaning depends on scriptural context and should not be assumed to describe an independent power equal to God.

The question of how to read this figure connects with [literal interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=87CAE620F6194480B20F2C8B053A5841), which considers when scriptural language signifies a spiritual or symbolic reality.
