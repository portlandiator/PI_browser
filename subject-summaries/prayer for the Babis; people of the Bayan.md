# prayer for the Babis; people of the Bayan

The people of the Bayan occupy a distinct place in prayers that ask for their protection, blessing, and victory. These petitions remember the Bábí community and the Letters of the Living within a devotional language of divine aid. Their focus is communal: the prayer seeks support for a group facing adversity and invokes the fulfillment of its spiritual purpose, rather than treating the Bayan merely as a historical label.

These community-directed petitions belong to the wider practice described under the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), where supplication encompasses both individual needs and collective hopes.
