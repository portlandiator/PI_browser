# loving community spirit in teaching the Cause

A loving community can make faith visible before formal teaching begins. Prejudice-free friendship, unity, and practical service create warmth that helps people feel welcome and open to conversation. The community’s inner relationships therefore have public consequences: if believers treat one another as family, their fellowship can attract others more powerfully than rules or preaching alone. This love must extend beyond the community to strangers, other religious groups, the poor, and even those who behave unjustly.
