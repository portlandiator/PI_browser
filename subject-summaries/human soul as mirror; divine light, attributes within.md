# human soul as mirror; divine light, attributes within

The human soul is likened to a mirror in which divine light and attributes can become manifest. Its capacities are latent and require spiritual education and purification to shine forth, just as a mirror must be polished to reflect light. The image affirms both human potential and dependence on divine bounty.

The mirror metaphor links the soul’s capacity to [the Manifestation as mirror](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D941821F406B4A20B1E60AD343B49717), while [the Manifestation as educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=54135CB53EEE434D8B77F1C91F81A26C) and [the Manifestation’s mission](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25E17ABCAE6B4195844602A0178DB4E5) describe revelation’s role in developing spiritual capacities. The need for readiness is explicit in [capacity and readiness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8867A9B4875C48BB846B800856E218D7), and light’s symbolic language appears in [color and light symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5DF1FBB2026CE6E1050C257923FD3853). Inner receptivity connects with [purity of heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098), [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69); the human capacity to reflect divine qualities appears in [humanity created in God’s image](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8EEDB198EF554EDAA21873DEC17AF79C). Further links include [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), [the soul as man’s essential reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D9D74C90C8344208B1361977A8FC544), [spiritual communication](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E), [the soul-body relationship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=52B1C3F2B9574C37BAF0ECCC8D5AB8C1), [divine immanence and transcendence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6), [divine attributes within all things](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B996EA76F6EC401A9148E1F0507C179A), and [divine emanation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9).
