# music and singing

Bahá’u’lláh calls music a ladder for the soul and warns against using it to inflame passion. Other passages describe melody as spiritual nourishment that can move the heart, and encourage singing prayers and songs of love at gatherings. Music is therefore welcomed as a means of devotion and fellowship, with its effect shaped by its words, setting, and use.

[Holy Days and the Bahá’í calendar](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1FED56E998B24C76A6E68CC456452598) includes music within cycles of communal observance. [Spiritual meetings; gatherings; devotional gatherings; feasts](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) provides settings where song and shared worship can strengthen fellowship. [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41) connects music with sacred spaces, while [Praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) reflects music’s affirming role. [The Concourse on High](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C2659CEFAA5E6114DDA8A2D71F13AF2) and [Mystical themes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6259D6333FC7EE132D6AD0D77800E8FC) connect musical imagery with spiritual realities. [Autobiographical account; narrative of Abdu’l-Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710) and [Banishment to; life in Baghdad](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD6AD7D972DCE3881CF23778EE78C347) provide historical settings. [Service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) and [Call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) bring communal purpose into view. [Material and spiritual existence; two books](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), [Connection between material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61), and [Spiritual emotions and susceptibilities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) relate audible forms to inward life. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) connects devotion with action, while [The Word of God; influence and centrality of](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) concerns the spiritual source of illumination. [Predictions and prophecies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E) shares the wider language of hope and future fulfillment. Together these links show music at the intersection of worship, history, feeling, and community.
