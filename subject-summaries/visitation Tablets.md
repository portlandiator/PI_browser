# visitation Tablets

Visitation Tablets are devotional prayers for recitation at a sacred resting place or in remembrance of a holy figure, including the Báb, martyrs, and other revered believers. They offer salutations, praise, testimony, and petitions for mercy, sometimes asking that remembrance bring spiritual benefit. Detailed ritual directions guide some shrine visits; proxy visitation provides a way to honor the departed when distance or circumstance prevents travel.
