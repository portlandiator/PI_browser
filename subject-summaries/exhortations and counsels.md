# exhortations and counsels

Exhortations and counsels urge people toward wisdom, honesty, patience, detachment, and service. They combine spiritual principles with practical direction for conduct, emphasizing that faith should be visible in action. Counsel can console or warn, but its aim is constructive guidance and the betterment of individual and community life.

Counsel is grounded in [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B) and often calls for [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) and [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Its ethical foundations include [honesty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), [the qualities of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), and [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF). Spiritual discipline appears in [piety](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E5E0436334E8467AA2757EF389C2161A) and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), expressed through [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [avoiding contention](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48768953025E40D1B1F978FF61180A24), [love and fellowship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A), and freedom from [worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85). The counsels also invoke [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CCBA0D8726E9D920DC566B92556700), and [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and emphasize [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946), [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and the relation between [words and deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A).
