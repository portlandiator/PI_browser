# trust and reliance in God; turning to Him at all times

Trust and reliance in God mean turning to divine protection and guidance in every condition. A person is urged to trust in God and cling to the “Sure Handle,” combining confidence with active perseverance. Reliance is not passivity: it steadies action, helps a person withstand tests, and releases anxiety about outcomes beyond human control.

Trust sustains a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) and [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), offering [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F) and grounding [submission to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA). It appears in [Abdu’l-Baha’s narrative](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710) and draws on [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E); [work as worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D) and awareness of [requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118) give it ethical direction. Trust keeps earthly loss in perspective through [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), reflection on [transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), helping believers endure [tests](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4).
