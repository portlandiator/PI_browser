# self-improvement; self-perfection; discipline

Self-improvement and discipline begin with learning, honest self-government, and humility. Perfection is impossible without humility, and moral life consists in governing oneself. Growth is gradual: study, reflection, and practice help a person bring conduct into closer accord with spiritual principles. The aim is not self-absorption but transformation that equips a person to serve and contribute to the lives of others.

Personal development appears in [Abdu’l-Baha’s autobiographical narrative](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710), supports [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), and requires [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). It is strengthened through [education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EEEFCF8268224145B6028CD4848913D1), informed by the [soul’s life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), and practiced along the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549); its inward dimension includes [knowledge of self](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3ED3EB2EFEBF43C9A36134E26FDB38EE).
