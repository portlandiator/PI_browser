# spiritual foundations of true knowledge

Spiritual foundations of true knowledge join learning with love of God and clear discernment. Knowledge resembles a tree judged by its fruit, and keenness of understanding is a kind of vision. Learning gains spiritual value when it recognizes God and bears fruit in a life of service rather than remaining detached from moral purpose.

True knowledge is rooted in [knowledge and recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218) and finds expression in [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF).
