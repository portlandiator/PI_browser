# humor; jokes

Humor can soften conversation, expose pretension, and make a serious point memorable. ‘Abdu’l-Baha jokes about “wineky,” answers a minister’s challenge about Muhammad’s ascent by turning it back on Christ, and compares a demanding child with a politely persuasive young man. Everyday misunderstandings and playful exchanges show laughter serving warmth and perspective rather than ridicule.

Jokes overlap with [stories and anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8), since a brief incident can carry the humor, and with [biblical interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1EBA961BF35048008A08B489196F1A6E) and [Qur’an and Hadith interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=19377C1C79EB4023BAD88C9FEA93B741), where playful replies engage religious claims. Humor also appears amid [Abdu’l-Baha’s travels in the West](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6E786EE894E9EA130794C22592689E71), his [autobiographical narrative](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710), and experiences of [life in ‘Akka](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4FAFE46E3FC7417BAC3D18A3C7D260C2) or [Adrianople](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6580A1098A7242F9B29D4916F5B4F414). Lightness can accompany [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=369DAA81C51F4587B8F070614833A216), [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), [joy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), and [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B). The comic voice does not erase difficulty: it sits alongside [opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) and [tests and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), and may make room for human closeness under pressure. A comparison of religious figures also touches [the cross and the Trinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2E899D7A2A2DE9B09ABA9A4160C9D3F8).
