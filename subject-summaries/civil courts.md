# civil courts

Civil courts can distinguish the rights of parties, adjudicate disputes, and impose proportionate consequences under law. Bahá’í writings reject personal revenge and emphasize impartial justice, while warning that bribery and corrupt judgment defeat the purpose of a court. Fair procedures and competent judges help protect both the oppressed and the accused and contribute to public calm.
