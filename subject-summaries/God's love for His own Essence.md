# God's love for His own Essence

God's love for His own Essence is eternal and the source of every other love. He knows and beholds His own beauty without depending on any creature to reflect or recognize it. The image of the Hidden Treasure links this divine love with creation and revelation: love brings beings into existence and draws them toward their Creator. Human life bears the imprint of that love, even as God's inmost reality remains beyond our understanding.

The neighboring subject of [four or five pathways of love](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=552E8D5180B04AAB91BE86621F37CD9B) places this inward divine love within a larger account of love's relationships. [Self-description and self-remembrance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=04F924E0CC51EDF12DBB02871348AD74) develop the same theme through God's knowledge and praise of Himself, while [transcendence and unknowability](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) explain why such language does not imply human access to the divine Essence. [Knowledge of self](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3ED3EB2EFEBF43C9A36134E26FDB38EE) brings the discussion toward the human search for identity and God, without equating human self-knowledge with divine self-knowledge. [The self-generation of the Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C470397E9BCC44ECB45F21E1521E18C1) connects love with the appearance of beings through divine bounty. [Eternal archetypes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C9B2EC0371C49F7A3566365EC1285B8) and the [holy and most holy outpouring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5A1748B3FB14D8EA1B9992E6E0409BF) supply further language for exploring the relation between divine knowledge, revelation, and creation. Their selections also contain qualifications and criticisms of philosophical formulations, so these connections invite careful comparison rather than a single, settled account of how God's inward love becomes manifest.
