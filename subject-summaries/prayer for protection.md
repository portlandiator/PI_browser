# prayer for protection

Protection is a plea for safety from violence, illness, temptation, hostile forces, and whatever threatens spiritual or bodily well-being. The prayers ask God to shelter individuals and communities, preserve them through changing circumstances, and strengthen them against harm. Protection can mean rescue from external danger, but it also includes freedom from self-deception, passion, and despair. These petitions hold vulnerability and trust together, asking for help without claiming that adversity will never come.

Protection is sought through the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), particularly where danger involves the community’s fidelity and unity, as in prayers about [Covenant-breakers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42). It is closely related to endurance through [tests and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4), including the memory of [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), and to the [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) needed under pressure. Prayers for safety can voice [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14) amid [persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), and include petitions for [women](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FE19F5905B90E62AF750E4476660CAEC) or particular [individuals and groups](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343).
