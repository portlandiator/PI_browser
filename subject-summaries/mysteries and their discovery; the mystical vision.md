# mysteries and their discovery; the mystical vision

Mysteries and their discovery concern the hidden meanings and realities that spiritual perception can disclose. Gates open to divine mysteries, and human reason can uncover realities once hidden. Such discovery depends on attention and inquiry, but also reaches beyond surface appearances toward the inward significance of things and the spiritual order linking them.

Mystical discovery is joined to [intuition and inspiration](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F9048B210D354CDE8C29D32A2F92E9B1) and broader [mystical themes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6259D6333FC7EE132D6AD0D77800E8FC). It may find communal expression in the [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41) and [devotional gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), and turns the heart toward the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) and the link between [material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61). It can contemplate the significance of [letters and numbers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=03732171C89EE6423D686FB6C1E39F42), deepen [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and bring [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and [guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1). Discovery prepares [spiritual prerequisites for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), and a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), while [contemplation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DA67DEF4ED6E470BA9C5A666A3697E80) and the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) cultivate receptivity. It depends on [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8) and [divine capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), and turns to [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), whose [superiority](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CFB58B5793CB4B10BCCDDE3EC1B51C19) calls for careful interpretation. [Poetry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91840AE122AEC232D80E33023EC88DF) can give voice to these insights, and [prophecy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E) may disclose a future beyond ordinary sight.
