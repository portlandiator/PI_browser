# passage of elementary matter through degrees of existence

Material constituents pass through changing forms and participate in different kingdoms of existence. Elements that enter mineral, plant, animal, and human bodies continue through processes of combination and dissolution. This philosophical account of nature emphasizes continuity beneath the succession of forms and uses that movement to reflect on development more broadly. Its spiritual significance lies in the relationship between persistence and change, rather than in supplying a modern scientific model.

[Divine philosophy and natural philosophy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=43F0402F0B1F4706BC577B7A7A79BFFD) places accounts of nature beside distinct claims about spiritual civilization, showing that developmental imagery can serve different kinds of explanation. [Evolution; human evolution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F7EC8954BFA749B4ACA3C4353E8A96CB) focuses on changes across stages of human life and the emergence of capacities, including the distinction between bodily development and the manifestation of spirit. Together these links show how passage through degrees can be discussed materially and in relation to human development, without assuming those accounts are identical.
