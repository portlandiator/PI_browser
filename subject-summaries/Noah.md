# Noah

Noah is remembered across scriptural traditions as a prophet associated with warning, response, and the flood narrative. The story has been interpreted as both a historical account and a spiritual lesson about renewal and human conduct. With only a small number of excerpts available, this introduction remains general and avoids claims about details not supported by the supplied material.
