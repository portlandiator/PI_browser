# essential distinction of Manifestation of God from others

The Manifestation of God occupies a unique spiritual station that ordinary human advancement cannot attain. His role is more than exceptional learning or moral excellence: He reveals divine guidance and possesses the power to educate humanity. Other people can make extraordinary spiritual progress and reflect His teachings, yet this does not make them Manifestations. Recognizing the distinction clarifies both the authority of revelation and the possibilities of growth open to its followers.

This distinction appears in the account of the Manifestation as [educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=54135CB53EEE434D8B77F1C91F81A26C): a universal teacher must possess a capacity beyond ordinary human limits. The [need for an educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A9FB0DFA63874B4E9177CA70ED30CFEA) gives the argument its premise, and [universal education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=94AE855C19A34B0D9F7D09FA46079FB3) names the reach of that role. The topic also connects directly to the [station of the Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5), which the sources describe as categorically distinct, and to the [Perfect Man (insan-i-kamil)](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=77E5DDB689E847A198C9D181A95A2B27), an image used for the Manifestation’s comprehensive human and spiritual perfection. The distinction has a practical consequence in the [obedience to; authority of the Manifestations of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10C0903A3A7048C193248AAA8629D4D4), where the unique station grounds a unique authority. These related themes illuminate the same hierarchy from its educational, spiritual, and devotional sides.
