# power of love

Love has power to draw the heart toward God and transform the way a person perceives and lives. Love is the light of the world, and the lover’s heart is more precious than restless sagacity. This force exceeds ordinary calculation: it gives spiritual life, loosens material limits, and is understood as fundamental to religion rather than a passing emotion.

Love’s power is rooted in [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) and in [love as a foundation of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7824F7FB65A4B75A74AC15AFD08EC5C).
