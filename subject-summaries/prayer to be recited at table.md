# prayer to be recited at table

A prayer at table turns a shared meal into an occasion for gratitude, blessing, and spiritual nourishment. The petitions give thanks for food while asking that it sustain more than the body: participants hope to receive a share of divine bounty that strengthens them for service and care for the world. Eating together thus becomes a moment of fellowship and remembrance, linking everyday provision with generosity, community, and a higher purpose.

This table prayer is one expression of the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), bringing gratitude and shared devotion into an ordinary daily act.
