# Letters of the Living

The Letters of the Living were the first eighteen followers of the Báb, who recognized his station and helped spread his message. The Báb himself is counted among the nineteen, making the group a symbolic as well as historical community. Their lives illustrate the rapid formation of the Bábí movement and the devotion of its earliest adherents. Later Bahá’í symbolism also draws on their number and collective role.

No accepted one-hop connections are recorded for this subject in the catalogue. No direct catalogue relationship is available for the first followers of the Báb.
