# living waters; water of life

Living waters and the water of life symbolize spiritual teachings that renew, guide, and sustain the soul. A fountain gives everlasting life, and an inward torrent flows when the heart is freed from earthly obstruction. This water is not physical nourishment; it represents revelation and divine grace that awaken the spirit and help it move from spiritual death to life.

Living waters point toward [the Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE). They invite [prayer for recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8) and flow through the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), sustaining [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) and [life in the spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AE0799C6978C430E8B793BC7101E1A12). Their reception entails [belief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1B9173457DB3E756B5CA9F035EFD7CC9), contrast with [spiritual death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=064A242494DCE29D5CBAA6D792D7670B), and a call to [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), [effort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB047A0483F7446F98969E0BB0A9A2A0), and the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). The response includes [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), reliance on [divine grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), preparation for [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218), and [forgiveness and mercy](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5CF47B6B008F461986A90EF7E6BF176E).
