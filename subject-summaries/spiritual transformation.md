# spiritual transformation

Spiritual transformation renews the heart and changes how a person lives. Divine love acts like an elixir, bringing forth capacities that selfish habits and material attachments obscure. The Word of God awakens spiritual life within the soul. This renewal enlarges concern for others, expressing itself through changed conduct, stronger relationships, and wholehearted service.

Transformation spans sacred history and human experience, from [Abraham, Isaac, and Ishmael](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FDA9B7CC77849A9B4E383D68116F833) to the relation between [material and spiritual existence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B), [second birth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8C84F900ED34DA98391B4A8593684E1), and the [Manifestation as the Sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35).
