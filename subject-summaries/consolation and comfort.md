# consolation and comfort

Consolation and comfort address grief, hardship, and uncertainty with spiritual assurance and practical care. Comfort may come through prayer, remembrance, companionship, and confidence in divine mercy; it does not require denying suffering or grief. Compassion for those in distress is joined with hope in spiritual progress and life beyond death.

Comfort speaks to [opposition and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), and extends to [the soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313).
