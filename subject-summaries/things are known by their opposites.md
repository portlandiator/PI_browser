# things are known by their opposites

Contrast makes qualities discernible: light stands out against darkness, and presence against absence. By noticing differences, people recognize realities that might otherwise escape attention or remain difficult to describe. Opposites therefore help perception and understanding, whether the contrast concerns physical conditions, experiences, or moral qualities. Using contrast in this way does not make its two sides morally equivalent; it clarifies what distinguishes them and sharpens awareness of their different effects.
