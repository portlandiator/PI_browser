# spirit of faith [ruh-i-'iman]

The spirit of faith is a divine power that brings recognition, certitude, and spiritual perception to life. It is mercy from God and a force that helps the soul apprehend revelation, attain hidden visions, and remain established in certainty. Faith is thus more than assent: its spirit enables a person to see, respond, and give thanks for the bounty received.

The spirit of faith shapes [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), helps attain [God’s good pleasure](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=034E6E7433614B24B4A7683E29D0C241), and participates in [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
