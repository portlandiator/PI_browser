# zeal and fervor in teaching the Cause

Zeal and fervor propel teaching beyond good intentions into sustained effort. Enthusiasm, dedication, and courage can help a believer explore new approaches, endure hardship, and speak with conviction; imagery of fire and movement conveys the urgency of the task. This ardor should be grounded in a clear understanding of the mission and a life shaped by its principles. Its purpose is to share hopeful teachings and revive hearts, not to seek attention or personal comfort.

Zeal serves the wider [call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), lending energy and persistence to that work.
