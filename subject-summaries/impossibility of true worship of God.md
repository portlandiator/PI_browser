# impossibility of true worship of God

No act of worship can do full justice to God. Our words, thoughts, and devotion arise within the limits of created life, while His reality exceeds them all. This inadequacy does not silence love: the heart still longs to pray, praise, and draw near. Recognizing our inability to worship God as He deserves becomes itself an act of humility, freeing devotion from the pretence of fully knowing its Beloved.

[Transcendence and unknowability](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) explain why worship cannot encompass God's Essence, while the [limits of the intellect](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=048D1BC9980545A2BF50BCE956D33C2C) show why even the most refined ideas remain inadequate. The connection with [God as immanent and transcendent](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6) shifts attention toward the signs through which an inaccessible reality can nevertheless be recognized. [Knowledge and recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218) therefore concern the revelation available to us, rather than possession of God's inmost being. The [state and dynamics of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7329FDEC013DEAD42EBB1EE8D4348E33) give this distinction its lived expression: a person may confess the inadequacy of every word and still be impelled by love to speak. These connections make the subject an invitation to humble devotion, rather than a dismissal of prayer or a demand that worship cease.
