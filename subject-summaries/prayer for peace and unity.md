# prayer for peace and unity

Peace requires more than the absence of fighting: it depends on reconciliation, justice, and a recognition of humanity as one family. Prayers for peace ask that war cease, hatred give way to affection, and nations and communities live in security and cooperation. Some plead for immediate relief for people caught in conflict; others envision lasting international concord. Unity is the moral foundation of that peace, calling people to overcome estrangement without erasing their differences.

These hopes are voiced through the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), which becomes a petition for an end to conflict and the growth of human solidarity.
