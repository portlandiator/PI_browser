# Night of Power (Laylat al-Qadr)

Laylat al-Qadr, the Night of Power, is a sacred night in Islam associated with the revelation of the Qur’an and divine decree. It is observed during Ramadan, with special devotion and prayer. Its spiritual significance is understood to surpass ordinary time.
