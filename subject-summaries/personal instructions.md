# personal instructions

Personal instructions guide particular people through concrete choices: where to travel, whom to meet, how to conduct an errand, or what response to make. Visits and return journeys are arranged, messages may be relayed through others, and advice responds to particular circumstances. Such counsel joins practical judgment with spiritual purpose; it is individualized guidance rather than a general rule for every situation.

Private counsel can direct service and teaching, linking [personal instructions] to [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), and [review of publications](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B362A06F749D49B8A9B4080391A83C2E). It may support perseverance through [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B), and [submission to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA), while helping with [health and healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55), [freedom of thought and action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3AF5CD058F82471FA2236DF51BDF9F38), or the demands of [order and deliberation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4925B84D51C04E3DB0D1AB40021DA116). Instructions also touch relationships and practical affairs: [gifts and contributions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78), [business and property](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8C309A3E20044543A9A72F36981F7D2A), [naming individuals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B71D6E7CFCC8E05A303BFE9F83981E31), and [permission for pilgrimage](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=71CA0BAD93CAE678CC1BA773DF8A5EC8). Personal encounters occur amid [Abdu’l-Baha’s Western travels](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6E786EE894E9EA130794C22592689E71), [his autobiographical narrative](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710), and [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8); counsel may respond to [imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) or invite [love of enemies](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D614A134412E9DA43B77542E47091BD). It can illuminate [guidance as light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1), offer [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), or draw on [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22).
