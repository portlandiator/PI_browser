# prayer for steadfastness; obedience

Steadfastness is the resolve to remain loyal to God’s love and Cause under changing conditions. These prayers ask that tribulation, opposition, doubt, and the pull of other attachments not turn the believer aside. Obedience gives this firmness a practical direction: the worshipper seeks to hold to revealed commands and act on them, rather than treating faith as sentiment alone. The desired constancy is sustained by divine aid and expressed in service and praise.

Prayer for steadfast obedience draws on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) to cultivate broader [perseverance and faithfulness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668). It is supported by [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), especially when facing [opposition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) or [tests and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4).
