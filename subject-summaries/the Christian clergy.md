# the Christian clergy

Religious authority does not belong to a separate priestly caste in Bahá’í teaching. Criticism of Christian clergy focuses on particular abuses: clerical control over believers’ understanding, outward status substituted for spiritual insight, and the use of religious office for wealth or political power. Paul and other individual figures are treated distinctly, rather than every minister being judged as identical.
