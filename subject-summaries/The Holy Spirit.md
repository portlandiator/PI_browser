# The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit brings divine power and confirmation to spiritual life, awakening hearts and enabling transformation. Through the Manifestations it is associated with revelation, divine knowledge, and utterance; believers receive its assistance in learning, teaching, and renewing their lives. These forms of spiritual aid share a divine source without erasing differences of station. The Manifestation's revelatory authority remains distinct from the inspiration and support available to those who follow Him.

The Holy Spirit’s relation to revelation connects with [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and with [divine emanation as all-pervading bounty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A73492EB36754162BEE9588EF11F2FD9). Images of spiritual renewal appear in [spiritual baptism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B3EA44B110164A69A3BD4B928633F1E3), [attaining the life of the spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AE0799C6978C430E8B793BC7101E1A12), and [second birth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8C84F900ED34DA98391B4A8593684E1), while [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) describes its ethical effects. The Manifestation’s role as recipient of revelation is treated in [the Manifestation and divine revelation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=108A205E354349B7B235F2EDB2FD6A52); the believer’s role in teaching is explored in [being a channel for the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FEC1A5C44287454E9CA7BCBB4E5EC15C). [The Primal Will, Word, Remembrance, and Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2AEC483F2F894D19A2807A453EDF46DA) places these themes in a wider account of revelation. Together the links distinguish divine revelation from its effects in individual spiritual life.
