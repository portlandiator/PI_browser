# superiority; incomparability of the Word of God

The Word of God is considered incomparable because its source and spiritual authority exceed the measure of ordinary human speech or writing. Its meanings may unfold at multiple levels, and its impact cannot be judged solely by immediate human response. This superiority concerns the revelatory Word’s unique status and power, not a claim that every interpretation of it is equally sound.

The Word’s layered meanings connect to [outward and inward meanings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3568B942A8FE439CBD3238E92AE8D80E) and [multiple scriptural interpretations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB987894E7264233BA0C0C5DAB4D3C5F). Its central authority is explored in [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), while its relation to religious change appears in [the Bayan and the abrogation of its laws](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0C9ECE2BFDC3E344AC68F7EF3A7101F5). The difficulty of its depths leads to [mysteries and their discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623). These links show how incomparability concerns both the Word’s authority and the richness of its interpretation.
