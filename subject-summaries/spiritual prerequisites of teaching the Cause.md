# spiritual prerequisites of teaching the Cause

Teaching the Cause calls for spiritual preparation as well as knowledge. Good character, humility, sincerity, detachment, and love help a teacher speak with wisdom and kindness; prayer and trust in divine assistance support the effort. The hearer’s receptivity matters, so persuasion must not become pressure. Learning should lead to action, and teachers are urged to embody what they share, serve humanity, and persevere through tests while remaining sensitive to their own limits.
