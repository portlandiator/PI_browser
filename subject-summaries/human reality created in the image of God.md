# human reality created in the image of God

Human beings possess the capacity to reflect divine qualities, as a clear mirror reflects light. Knowledge, love, mercy, and other spiritual capacities lie within human nature and develop through education and effort. Being created in God's image concerns this potential, rather than a physical likeness or a share in the divine Essence. The image affirms human dignity while calling each person to cultivate the qualities entrusted to them.

[Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) describes how human action can turn outward toward the betterment of others. [Knowledge; recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218) connects reflection with recognizing the divine source, while [Man’s distinction from the animal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB) identifies capacities associated with human spiritual life. [Differences in human capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=80687FE0F2F74ECB82AD3E83C0CBC790) qualifies the idea by noting variation in how potential develops. [Human soul as mirror; divine light, attributes within](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=221CD3663B0740D49344F071233D1C5F) offers the closest image: latent qualities become manifest when the mirror is prepared. Together these connections relate spiritual potential to knowledge, development, and action without confusing human reflection with divine identity.
