# four elements

Fire, air, water, and earth form a classical scheme for understanding nature through qualities such as heat, cold, moisture, and dryness. Their combinations explain the diversity of forms within that older cosmological framework. The same elements also carry spiritual meanings: fire can suggest love, water mercy, air spirit, and earth the human heart. Reading these images requires distinguishing their symbolic force from their place in historical explanations of the physical world.

The symbolic language of fire and water connects to [fire and light; fire and water](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7112D8A18D3C4C7BB98681C6DE3D94E0), where these elements carry spiritual as well as material meanings. This relation brings the classical scheme of four elements into contact with devotional images of illumination, love, and mercy.
