# the Imamate; the Caliphate

The Imamate and Caliphate describe distinct forms of leadership in Islamic history and thought. The Caliphate concerns succession to political and communal authority after Muhammad; the Imamate, especially in Shi’i traditions, carries spiritual authority associated with the line of Imams. Their meanings differ across Sunni and Shi’i communities and have shaped centuries of debate. Careful distinction between the institutions helps make sense of Islamic history and religious authority.
