# words vs deeds

Words gain credibility when conduct confirms them. Good actions can teach, attract confidence, and make spiritual principles visible; eloquent speech alone cannot establish sincerity. The ideal is not silence, since words joined to matching deeds can illuminate one another, but speech without action rings hollow. A person’s character is known through what they do, and service to others gives faith practical evidence in everyday life.

[Good deeds and actions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946) are the reality behind [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), and character and action are [prerequisites for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67). They help make visible the [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7) and the wider [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10); speech remains powerful when matched by action, as explored under [the power of words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A). [Counsel](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B) and the [laws of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=752881F17C2B4EE0BDA239B9D6517A45) shape the path of action, which belongs to a [virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) and the task of [teaching the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF). Consistent practice can support [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
