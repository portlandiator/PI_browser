# Hands of the Cause

The Hands of the Cause were appointed to protect and propagate the Bahá’í Faith and to support the community’s development. Their service included encouraging believers, defending the unity of the Faith, and assisting the institutions responsible for administration. The institution later gave way to other arrangements for service and protection, while its historical role remains important to understanding the development of Bahá’í administration.

[Spiritual assemblies; administrative matters](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DE6DED75D254D59BFF16E8CEE21F305) is the closest institutional connection: assemblies administer community affairs at local levels, while the Hands served the wider Faith through protection and encouragement. The link connects distinct forms of service within the development of the administrative order.
