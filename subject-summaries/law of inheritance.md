# law of inheritance

Bahá’í inheritance law sets shares for heirs and places estate obligations in a defined order. A will is executed before distribution; debts, funeral costs, and Huqúqu’lláh are settled first. The Kitáb-i-Aqdas assigns categories and portions, while related guidance addresses the residence and special clothing of the eldest son. The system balances statutory shares with testamentary choice, and its detailed rules aim to reduce disputes and ensure that property passes with dignity and fairness.

Inheritance shares connect with the [rights of the eldest son](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDEEC1B26F36E7A24978B407110501F7), whose residence and special clothing are treated separately from the other heirs’ portions.
