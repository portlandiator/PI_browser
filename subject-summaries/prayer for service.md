# prayer for service

Service is an active response to faith: believers ask for the strength, speech, and steadfastness to serve God and care for others. The prayers range from a personal plea for ability to serve the Cause to petitions for teachers and communities working to share the teachings. Service may require travel, endurance, and sacrifice, but its purpose is constructive—supporting people, building fellowship, and making spiritual ideals visible in everyday action.

These petitions seek the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) as confirmation for practical devotion, while the broader idea of [service to others and the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775) names the work the worshipper asks to undertake.
