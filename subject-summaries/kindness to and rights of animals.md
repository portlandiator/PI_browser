# kindness to and rights of animals

Animals share physical sensations and can suffer, so kindness toward them is a moral duty rather than a decorative virtue. Their care calls for protection, gentle training, and practical attention to hunger, injury, and comfort. Kindness extends to birds and wild creatures, and children are urged to learn tenderness early. Opinions about diet vary, while the recurring call is to avoid needless cruelty and treat living beings compassionately.

Care for animals is part of the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and a concrete expression of [compassion and kindness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=210D276A06CCE27ECA75FDC0B8F5205C). Stories and anecdotes about animals offer examples of this practice (see [stories](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8)).
