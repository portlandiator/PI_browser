# literature; drama; humanities; the arts

Literature, drama, and the humanities interpret human experience through language, story, and performance. They preserve memory, invite reflection, and make complex ideas accessible through form and imagination. Artistic work can also serve educational or spiritual purposes, though it has value beyond instruction alone. These fields contribute to a culture’s ability to examine its past, express its ideals, and understand the lives of others.

[Creativity in art](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C607ACD036BEE32B103A75FD10883CF2) focuses on artistic invention and the cultivation of skill. [Praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) reflects the support artists may need to pursue such work. Together these connections relate the broad humanities to creative practice and the encouragement that allows it to flourish.
