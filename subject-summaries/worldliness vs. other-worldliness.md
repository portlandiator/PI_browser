# worldliness vs. other-worldliness

Worldliness competes with remembrance of God by making temporary desires seem ultimate. Thoughts that separate the heart from God and deceptive worldly conditions can block divine bounty. A spiritual orientation does not require denying material life; it calls for placing it in proportion, resisting heedlessness, and directing knowledge and conduct toward lasting purposes.

Worldliness can be overcome through [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), with awareness of [requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118) and the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10). The topic also involves the [critique of Western values](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC28DBB6808BE947DE1EE25ECB14F544) and [materialism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C32DCE1484C6E34B581C0D0C738B7E27), which may mistake external civilization for human fulfillment. [Heedlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9EC159259B874FABB63915C45183582D) is challenged by [exhortation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B) and knowledge of [the transient world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C); resistance to [greed](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7D483E56354A41E7BBF405202EC78AAE) and [servitude and submission to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA) opens the way to [recognition of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218), the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), and [human distinction from the animal](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02605DD1AB7442E19B540C97BE1DD0DB).
