# high station of the true believer

The true believer holds a station of spiritual dignity and responsibility, often greater than the person recognizes. Believers are apostles of Bahá’u’lláh, entrusted with a distinctive rank and called to witness at the Divine Threshold. This honor is not privilege alone: it carries a duty to teach, serve, act with humility, and remain receptive to divine confirmations and guidance.

The believer’s station is linked to [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), the [station of teachers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0620D9E3C183E6CF5D3FD17DD58C9ABC), and the [greatness of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7). It depends on [divine confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), is met with [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and calls for [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649).
