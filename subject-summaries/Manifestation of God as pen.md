# Manifestation of God as pen

The Manifestation of God as pen symbolizes divine revelation expressed through sacred writing and speech. The pen’s movement is attributed to God’s power and decree, presenting the Manifestation as the instrument through which divine knowledge and praise are inscribed in the world. The image emphasizes both the authority of revelation and its dependence on God.

The pen’s instrumental role relates to [the Manifestation as mediator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=83C2E82C31DB4115A3CDF5884D0CB19E) and [the Manifestation as recipient of divine revelation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=108A205E354349B7B235F2EDB2FD6A52). Together these images describe how divine purpose is conveyed through a revelatory agent without identifying that agent with the divine source.
