# spiritual baptism

Spiritual baptism is an inward awakening through the water of life, the fire of divine love, and the Spirit of Truth. Outward life differs from a soul animated by the Holy Spirit; baptism signifies regeneration, guidance, and entry into a path of safety. Its significance lies in spiritual renewal and attraction to truth, not a merely physical rite.

Spiritual baptism signifies [second birth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8C84F900ED34DA98391B4A8593684E1), [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and the action of [the Holy Spirit](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=55CE96F733184F5FBA91482E85085853).
