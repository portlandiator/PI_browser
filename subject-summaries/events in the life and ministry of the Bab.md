# events in the life and ministry of the Bab

The Báb’s life and ministry included a period of trade, the sudden emergence of his prophetic mission, the composition of extensive writings, and years of imprisonment and opposition. His revelation transformed the lives of followers and challenged religious authorities. The episodes of his ministry show both the rapid growth of a new movement and the severe conditions under which it developed. Historical accounts vary in detail, so each episode should be read with attention to its source.
