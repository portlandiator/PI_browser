# House of Worship [Mashriqu'l-Adhkar]

A Bahá’í House of Worship is a place for prayer and reflection, open to people of all backgrounds. Worship is joined with service: the temple stands at the heart of a community devoted to spiritual life and the well-being of humanity. Its architecture and devotional use express unity, while nearby humanitarian and educational activities extend that spirit into practical care.
