# eulogies; reminiscences

Eulogies and reminiscences preserve the memory of people, recount their virtues, and offer comfort to those who mourn. They can transmit examples of service and faith across generations, while placing remembrance within a spiritual view of life and death. Such accounts are devotional and commemorative, and their narrative form matters.

Remembrance relates to [stories and anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8), [the soul after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313), and accounts of [Imam Husayn](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B08DD762C73AED1C83EAE3215CED1D53) and [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977). It gives form to [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14) while sustaining [spiritual communication](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E), [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and [consolation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F). Memorial memory may encourage [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), and [prayer for the departed](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6F48F0918CDBEBB76DD545292C255B68), and preserve examples of [true believer conduct](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) amid [persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E).
