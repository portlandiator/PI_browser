# consultation

Consultation is a disciplined way of seeking truth and making decisions together. Participants share views candidly, listen without attachment to being right, and support a collective decision once reached. Consultation differs from debate aimed at victory: its purpose is to illuminate a question and strengthen unity. It can guide families, communities, and institutions by drawing on diverse knowledge while keeping responsibility shared.

[Unity of thought and belief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A4AA0909671F483EB793112083AAB33D) connects consultation with accepting a majority decision as a conscientious act. [Spiritual meetings; gatherings; devotional gatherings; feasts](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483) offers communal settings for shared discussion, and [Love, unity, and fellowship among the friends](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A) describes the trust that supports honest exchange. Together these links show consultation depending on both process and the relationships that make collective thought possible.
