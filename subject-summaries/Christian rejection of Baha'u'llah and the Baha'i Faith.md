# Christian rejection of Baha'u'llah and the Baha'i Faith

Christian objections to Bahá’u’lláh often rest on expecting Christ’s return as a bodily descent from the literal sky. Bahá’í interpretation reads Gospel images of clouds and darkened stars symbolically and understands the promised Comforter as a later Manifestation. This is a theological response to particular objections, not a description of all Christian belief or of the full history between the communities.
