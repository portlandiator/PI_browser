# contemplation

Contemplation gives sustained attention to spiritual meaning and can open insight into hidden realities. Personal latitude in communion with God leaves room for reflective stillness as a way to discover mysteries. An hour of thoughtful reflection is praised as more fruitful than decades of worship without inward attention; contemplation thus unites freedom, depth, and discernment.

Contemplation supports the [importance of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAA2181CBE0F4BFFAAFD92CC0FCB75B4) and the discovery of [mysteries and mystical vision](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623).
