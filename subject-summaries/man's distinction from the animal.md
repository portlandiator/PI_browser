# man's distinction from the animal

Human beings share bodily senses and needs with animals, yet reason, discovery, moral choice, and spiritual awareness mark distinct human capacities. Those powers bring responsibility: human excellence lies in understanding reality and becoming a source of comfort and benefit to others, rather than serving only personal appetite.

[Limits of the intellect](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=048D1BC9980545A2BF50BCE956D33C2C) qualifies human distinction by recognizing boundaries to unaided reason. [Man is the sum; pinnacle; fruit of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBA5068DF2C24AE6A61E7337FF0B529D) places humanity within a broader account of creation’s culmination. [Human reality created in the image of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8EEDB198EF554EDAA21873DEC17AF79C) connects distinctive human potential with reflection of divine attributes. [Power of the mind over nature](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9239D6E43FFEECDA3B3AC738EC10194E) emphasizes practical influence, while [Two aspects of the human soul; the higher and lower natures](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5410D4DAE5454E8D81B13EC3E9DA2F23) adds the possibility of higher or lower orientation. [Rational arguments](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12FD56FFF2C74384AF52D04E7CEE255F) describes reason’s role in examining spiritual questions; [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) connects intelligence with spiritual motivation; and [the mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C) focuses on inquiry into natural realities. [Worldliness vs. other-worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85) adds the question of where human powers are directed. Together these links keep distinction tied to both capacity and its use.
