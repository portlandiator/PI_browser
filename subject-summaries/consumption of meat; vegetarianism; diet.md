# consumption of meat; vegetarianism; diet

Meat is permitted in Bahá’í law, but a diet of vegetables, grains, fruit, and nuts is preferred when it adequately sustains a person, because killing animals conflicts to some degree with pity and compassion. Wholesome meat may also be eaten, and animal life is difficult to avoid entirely. The guidance favors mercy without imposing vegetarianism; food is also discussed as a means of health and spiritual strength.
