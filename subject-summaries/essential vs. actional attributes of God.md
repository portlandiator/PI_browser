# essential vs. actional attributes of God

Essential attributes concern God's own reality; actional attributes concern His activity and its effects in creation. God's knowledge, life, and power are not separate parts added to His being, nor can we comprehend them as we comprehend human qualities. Names such as Creator and Provider describe His relationship with the world. This distinction helps us speak meaningfully about divine action while preserving the unity and unknowability of the divine Essence.

[Transcendence and unknowability](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) establish why the essential attributes cannot be grasped directly, while [God's oneness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6B2E2534F05E41069B3E1A18F5B552D4) guards against imagining them as separate components of His being. [Names and attributes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9AB88215A3AC410692D350AE3BEB3A54) provide the broader language within which this distinction operates. [God's knowledge](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D49918E5C4014E13A7AD43BCB1E89561) offers a particular example: essential knowledge is distinguished from knowing considered in relation to the world. The subject of [attributes requiring objects](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50E64D5F25E34797A55642F2919C4317) explores this relational dimension, without making the Essence dependent on creation. The [holy and most holy outpouring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B5A1748B3FB14D8EA1B9992E6E0409BF) introduces further distinctions concerning divine revelation and its relation to created realities. Read together, these six neighbors help separate statements about God's inaccessible being from statements about His self-disclosure, while leaving room for the differing terminology and qualifications found in the texts.
