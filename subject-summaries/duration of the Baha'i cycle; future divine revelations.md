# duration of the Baha'i cycle; future divine revelations

The Bahá’í cycle spans an extraordinarily long period, yet it does not end the succession of divine Revelations. Bahá’u’lláh’s Revelation inaugurates a cycle in which future Manifestations will appear, bringing teachings suited to humanity’s changing needs while remaining within the larger cycle He began. This view affirms the enduring significance of His Revelation and the unending process of divine guidance.

The authentication and disposition of sacred writings concerns reliable access to texts that discuss the Faith’s future. Proofs of the Manifestations address how divine Revelations are recognized, while warnings against false claims distinguish future Manifestations from impostors. These connections support careful understanding of succession without making the present cycle an endpoint.
