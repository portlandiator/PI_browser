# interconnectedness of the teachings

The teachings are interconnected: spiritual, moral, and social principles support and illuminate one another rather than functioning as isolated rules. Their coherence reflects a unified view of human life and society, where personal development, community well-being, and the pursuit of justice are mutually related.

This theme belongs to the broader idea of [interconnectedness and the chain of being](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2FC11A99D6A34DFD93993D59F31450E8), where relationships among created things form an ordered whole. It highlights how individual teachings gain meaning through their relation to the wider framework of spiritual and social life.
