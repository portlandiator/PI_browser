# reincarnation

A human soul does not return after death to inhabit another body. Bahá’í explanations distinguish that rejected doctrine from the valid symbolic “return” of spiritual qualities and perfections in a later Revelation: John the Baptist, for example, came in the spirit of Elijah, not as the same individual soul. Earthly life is a singular opportunity for development within a much wider spiritual existence.
