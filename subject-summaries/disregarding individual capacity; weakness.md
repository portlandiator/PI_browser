# disregarding individual capacity; weakness

Disregarding individual capacity and weakness can burden people beyond what they can reasonably sustain. Believers should not measure themselves only by their limitations or assume that everyone learns and progresses at the same rate. Effective guidance recognizes different capacities and relies on divine confirmations, while allowing each person to develop according to their circumstances.

Individual limitations should be understood alongside [divine grace and confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), which enable capacity to grow.
