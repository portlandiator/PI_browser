# proclamation to Jews

Proclamation to Jews invokes the people of Moses and the Torah, announcing the promised time as fulfilled and asking where the Promised One is to be found. Its language joins glad tidings with challenge: signs fill the horizons, yet doubt and discord remain. The address calls for recognition of the new Day while drawing on shared scriptural memory and the expectation of divine promise.
