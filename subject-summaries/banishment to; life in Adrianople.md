# banishment to; life in Adrianople

Bahá’u’lláh was exiled to Adrianople in 1868, where he publicly addressed rulers and religious leaders and proclaimed his mission more widely. The period brought intensified conflict with his half-brother Mirza Yahya and ended with a further banishment to ‘Akká. Despite restrictions and hardship, Adrianople became a pivotal stage in the spread of Bahá’u’lláh’s writings and public claims.
