# Seal of the Prophets

The title “Seal of the Prophets” is an honorific for Muhammad in Islam and is central to Muslim belief about his prophetic station. Interpretations differ over whether the title means finality in a chronological sense, the completion of prophecy, or another theological relation. Bahá’í writings engage the phrase while affirming continuity through progressive revelation. The title raises a question about how religious finality relates to renewal.

[Prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) relates the title to how prophetic language is interpreted across religious traditions. The connection highlights the distinction between a text’s wording and the theological meaning assigned to it in different communities.
