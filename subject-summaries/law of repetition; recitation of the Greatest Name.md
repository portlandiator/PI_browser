# law of repetition; recitation of the Greatest Name

The prescribed daily recitation of the Greatest Name is a brief act of worshipful meditation. Washing, turning toward God, and repeating “Alláh-u-Abhá” ninety-five times combine bodily preparation with remembrance and inward attention. The practice is associated with spiritual enrichment, joy, and connection to the divine. Its simplicity makes it a regular discipline that can be observed each morning, while the repetition anchors the mind in the sacred name rather than serving as empty ritual.
