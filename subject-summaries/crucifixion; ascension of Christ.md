# crucifixion; ascension of Christ

The crucifixion brought bodily suffering and death, but Christ’s spiritual station and mission endured beyond them. Bahá’í interpretation distinguishes the physical event from the ascension of Christ’s spirit and the continuing power of His Cause. This distinction gives the crucifixion and ascension related but different meanings within Christian and Bahá’í understandings.

[Resurrection of Christ](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=46F9949EEF0B4004B93D712D5C0A4366) is closely related in Christian accounts of death and continuing life. [Suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) places the crucifixion within the broader experience of persecution. [Symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450) helps interpret scriptural language about death and ascension, while [Quotation from or interpretation of the Bible](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1EBA961BF35048008A08B489196F1A6E) addresses the texts through which these events are understood. Together these links relate historical narrative, theological interpretation, and symbolic meaning.
