# light and darkness

Light and darkness symbolize knowledge and ignorance, guidance and obscurity, or spiritual life and heedlessness. The contrast can describe both historical change and an individual’s inner condition, while light often marks the appearance of revelation. These images communicate spiritual distinctions without treating darkness as a power equal to divine light.

[The Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) and [the Manifestation as sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) are sources of illumination, while [guidance as light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1) and [belief and denial](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1B9173457DB3E756B5CA9F035EFD7CC9) describe responses to it. The contrast also relates to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), [the phenomenon of light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1DADF08CF0244AD19C8296CF92A08546), [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), [joy and sorrow](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9896C9E13B524C0CB5F9770FF41B6D26), [the purpose of creation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=857627CDE8FBEF82708BB212CB362A7F), and [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Together these links use illumination as an image for guidance and the movement from obscurity toward understanding.
