# high station of the arts and sciences

The arts and sciences are highly valued as ways to discover, create, and contribute to civilization. Scientific inquiry investigates the natural world; artistic and humanistic work gives form to imagination, memory, and meaning. Both can serve human progress when joined with wisdom and ethical purpose. Learning is not limited to practical utility: it can deepen perception and enlarge the possibilities of individual and collective life.

[Call to acquire knowledge; sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=157B1606343947178806DFE21D30B32B) describes learning as a duty and support of faith. [Rational arguments](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=12FD56FFF2C74384AF52D04E7CEE255F) connects inquiry with reasoned consideration of spiritual questions. [High station of learning](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25BCE94ADDAF4B1B89551A0B5A85DE9C) emphasizes scholarship and the integration of knowledge, while [The mind’s power of discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D77315AED654CCC921D8BAC5B21A41C) points to investigation as a source of scientific understanding. [Education of children; moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) links knowledge with early character formation. Together these neighbors join research and art with reasoning, education, and responsible intellectual development.
