# happiness; joyfulness; joy and sorrow

Happiness and joy can coexist with sorrow when spiritual life remains rooted in God. Joy grows with purity, steadfastness, service to the Covenant, and spiritual teachings; happiness also arises through love and fellowship. Joy is not a denial of suffering but a response sustained by purpose, hope, and participation in work that serves humanity.

Joy is visible in a [radiant countenance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F501A1D6AA54C2E8306ED06B66657A5) and arises among [spiritual emotions and susceptibilities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B). It can accompany [imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A), the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), while being sustained by [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and perspective on [transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C). [Love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) give joy purpose amid [tests](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4). Humor may bring [humor and jokes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8D9F026E4760E4C06FB3125FB37BD27E), and [healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55), [light overcoming darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B68A1DE8700E8697ABFF0E02CF7F845), and reflection on life’s [purpose](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=857627CDE8FBEF82708BB212CB362A7F) deepen its meaning. Happiness also serves [humanity and its progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80).
