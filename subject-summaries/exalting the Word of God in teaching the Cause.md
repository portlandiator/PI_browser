# exalting the Word of God in teaching the Cause

Exalting the Word of God means raising its teachings through faithful, thoughtful teaching. The message should be conveyed directly, with quotations and explanations grounded in the revealed text, while respecting both its outward meaning and inner significance. Wisdom and steadfastness matter: a teacher should sow the Word where it can take root, and avoid forcing it where hearts are not receptive. The aim is to nourish understanding and help the teachings bring life to individuals and communities.

This work leads naturally to the [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), urging believers to raise the Word through teaching and steadfast effort.
