# smoking

Smoking tobacco is strongly discouraged in Bahá’í teaching, but it is not forbidden. Concerns about health, cleanliness, addiction, and wasted resources support encouragement to give it up. The decision remains with the individual: abstinence must not be enforced or made a test of faithfulness to the Covenant. This distinction preserves the force of spiritual counsel without turning it into a religious prohibition.
