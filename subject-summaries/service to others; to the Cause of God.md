# service to others; to the Cause of God

Service is a defining response to faith: each person should seek ways to benefit others and promote the Cause through deeds, sustained effort, and care. Service is called a high gift and ask believers to serve every member of the human race, not only their own community. This work can include teaching, charity, education, and practical aid. Its spiritual character depends on humility, perseverance, and action that improves people’s lives.

Service is a hallmark of [true believer conduct](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), extending especially to [the poor and vulnerable](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B118E279E521421DA6DE76C3D326B7A6) and requiring sustained [effort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB047A0483F7446F98969E0BB0A9A2A0). It may include [defending the Faith](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AE513863B45CE049600364AD783BC0EF), while its capacity depends on [grace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E) and serves [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) through [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668) and [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946). Work can be organized around a [House of Worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E52E5452873A452AA14119AAB8CFFC41), supported by [contributions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D9EB77409AAAEE3EEEBB51E542688C78) and connected with [holy places](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=69863262A551E3763501D5846A98329B), whose imagery includes the [water of life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992). It also follows [personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2) and calls for [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), understanding work as [worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CB73A56CDCC6E0484B0C8324B42C7E3D), supporting [children’s education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4), and appreciating biographical examples in the [narratives of ‘Abdu’l-Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710). Its response includes [gratitude](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD4052A3136042C4B86176AB2F552C55) and [encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), recalling historic settings such as [Baghdad](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD6AD7D972DCE3881CF23778EE78C347), and may use [music](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4027C84B01D8438E9C7D92535DF6BAB7) to inspire [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Service is a means of [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) and can include [charitable associations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=654F0A9EF8A7EEFDC749F4577396E5B5) and urgent [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), shaped by [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) and [Covenant fidelity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633). [Humor](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8D9F026E4760E4C06FB3125FB37BD27E) may season conversation; the essential test remains [deeds over words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A). Service can bring [comfort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), respond to moral [counsel](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B), and enact the [Golden Rule](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9E2097943E7F4A8AB3F8A1B03D1E644D), while teaching service may receive [confirmations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7CD2FD413A26449884C53C73A2482877). Its integrity depends on [purity of heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098) and is directed toward the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), even amid [grief](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14), with [eulogies and reminiscences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9F2E188A76D8EB43AE22B4F563C85137) and [wisdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F21CB46DB1E7424F8F52EA6E0C61DD3B). It belongs to the [virtuous path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) and may require endurance through [trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4) and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C); [speech](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A) can further the Cause, and [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977) or [self-sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0) mark extreme, not routine, forms of devotion. Service thrives in [gatherings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53AFF53F02514681A22F4E2FAB467483), is grounded in [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and contributes to [human betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed by a related topic on [social progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80). Its trustworthiness matters (see [honesty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3)); [hospitality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=02AB9A3E1C13E61D5B0AE0F70778680D) and [fellowship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A) make care tangible. [Prayer for service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=62A8DB017E8EEB11B3A3991393795E72) and [ethical readiness for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) accompany the work, as does awareness of [life’s transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C). Supplication draws on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687); allegiance to the [Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA), intercession for [individuals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343) and their [spiritual progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3), [submission](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA), and endurance through [suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A) are also connected.
