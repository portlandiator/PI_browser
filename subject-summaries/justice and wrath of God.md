# justice and wrath of God

Divine justice gives each soul its due, holds wrongdoing to account, and promises that oppression will not have the final word. Warnings of wrath confront tyranny, cruelty, and rejection of divine guidance with the certainty of consequences, both outward and spiritual. Alongside these warnings stands the assurance that God's mercy precedes His wrath and His grace exceeds what people deserve. Justice thus sustains moral responsibility and hope for the oppressed, while prayer seeks forgiveness, protection, and the replacement of tyranny with fairness.

[Chastisement and requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118) concern the consequences of action, including remorse and deprivation of spiritual bounty. [God's power and sovereignty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E) underlie the assurance that even seemingly secure oppressors remain answerable for their deeds. [Rejection, opposition, and persecution](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E3503B9BB6424884B3157B754204073E) give that assurance a concrete setting, while [grief, lamentation, and sadness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B74AA4BF06FE197BC2DFCF83BE17C14) express the pain that calls for justice. [Tests, trials, sacrifice, and suffering](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4) introduce a necessary distinction: hardship can deepen spiritual life and is not, by itself, evidence of personal guilt. [Theodicy and the mystery of evil](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9DEC6BF8F69A406FADF3FCA1C4BFAE33) ask how innocent suffering can be reconciled with divine goodness, directing attention toward human limits and recompense beyond this life. [Present and future calamities, war, and universal convulsion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8CDDA7D99E83E1421D3C3542A49272FA) extend the discussion to collective consequences and renewal. [Heedlessness and ignorance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9EC159259B874FABB63915C45183582D) connect these warnings to the need to awaken, abandon oppression, and respond to guidance before its neglect brings further suffering.
