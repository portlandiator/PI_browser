# necessary and possible being

Necessary being exists through itself and has no cause; possible or contingent being depends on causes and sustaining conditions. The distinction sets divine independence against created dependence without denying that contingent things possess reality in their own degree. Substance and accident, as well as an object’s reliance on conditions, help clarify the difference. The discussion concerns existence both absolutely and in relation to what receives and depends upon it.
