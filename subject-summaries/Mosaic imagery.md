# Mosaic imagery

The rod of Moses, the shining hand, Mount Sinai, and the Burning Bush give spiritual experience a vivid scriptural language. These images evoke divine power, guidance, and the transformation that follows an encounter with revelation. They can illuminate a later revelation without merely repeating an earlier event. Their force lies in helping readers recognize enduring spiritual meanings through the remembered scenes and symbols of Moses' mission.
