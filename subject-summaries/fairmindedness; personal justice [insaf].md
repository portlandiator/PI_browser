# fairmindedness; personal justice [insaf]

Fair-mindedness asks each person to judge with independent eyes, respect others’ rights, and choose for a neighbor what one would choose for oneself. It applies across every station, from ruler to laborer, and demands honesty about one’s own interests as well as others’. Justice is more than personal impartiality: it restrains oppression, protects welfare, and helps sustain social order. Equity, compassion, and consultation temper its practice without excusing injustice.

Fairness is expected in the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and links personal impartiality with wider [social and divine justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34).
