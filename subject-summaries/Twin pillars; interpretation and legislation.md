# Twin pillars; interpretation and legislation

Interpretation and legislation are two complementary pillars of Bahá’í authority. Authoritative interpretation clarifies the meaning of scripture, while legislation addresses matters not explicitly settled in the sacred text and can respond to changing conditions. Keeping these functions distinct supports continuity and adaptation within the Faith. Neither pillar is simply a personal opinion: each has a defined institutional source and role.
