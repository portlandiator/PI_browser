# unchastity and adultery

Bahá’í sexual ethics call for chastity outside marriage and fidelity within it, permitting sexual relations only between a man and the woman to whom he is married. These standards aim to protect marriage and family life. They accompany an equally explicit duty to respect every person: prejudice or disdain toward people with a homosexual orientation violates the spirit of the Faith, and their fundamental rights must be defended against oppression.
