# bringing forth results; fruit

Bringing forth results means allowing spiritual life to produce visible deeds, as a tree yields fruit. Genuine spirituality must lead to action; fruitless idleness falls short of faith. Results may benefit the person and others, and they depend on effort, cultivation, and the conditions that make growth possible. Inner aspiration becomes complete when it bears practical and ethical fruit.

Fruitfulness involves [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), [cultivating the soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D5D98C8A2EDA475EBC48A0E84E2AFE1D), [good deeds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=318AF6512E454733BC17A1B298A41946), and becoming [a source of light and guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1).
