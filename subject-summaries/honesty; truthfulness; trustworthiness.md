# honesty; truthfulness; trustworthiness

Truthfulness makes a person worthy of trust in speech, conduct, and responsibility. It joins sincerity with fair dealing: promises are kept, entrusted property is protected, and neither neighbor nor customer is deceived. Bahá’í counsel treats these qualities as foundations of social confidence and spiritual life, associating trustworthiness with justice, security, prosperity, and the honor of the human station. Faithfulness is not merely a private virtue; it gives integrity to relationships and service.

These virtues belong to the daily discipline of [walking the spiritual path; spiritual wayfaring; the virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), and stories and personal recollections can show them in action through [stories; anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) and the [autobiographical account; narrative of Abdu’l-Bahá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9694E11B244E2587DE1BB0F8D416710). They shape [conduct in finance and business](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8EDE6B0C407DE4EE770AB1CEFF0BCA15), support [serving humanity; working for progress; betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), and reinforce the related [serving humanity; working for progress, betterment of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80) entry. Counsel about character naturally meets [exhortations and counsels](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B), [courtesy; culture (adab)](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF1F45263A44466C9655EE1714DE1B33), and [justice (adl); social justice and divine justice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1021657B1CF64AAB90EC9FBF7FC73B34). Trustworthiness is a mark of the [characteristics and conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10), sustained by [steadfastness; perseverance; faithfulness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668). Its spiritual motive is linked to [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), while [love as fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D) and [love as fundamental; spiritual foundations of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7824F7FB65A4B75A74AC15AFD08EC5C) express love’s transforming and foundational roles. These qualities find practical expression in [service to others; to the Cause of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775).
