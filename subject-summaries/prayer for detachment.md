# prayer for detachment

Detachment frees the heart from exclusive reliance on transient things, self-will, and worldly distraction so it can turn wholly toward God. It does not mean indifference to life or service: many prayers ask to be released from attachment precisely in order to praise, serve, and act with greater fidelity. The desired freedom is inward allegiance, a loosening of possessiveness and anxious dependence that makes room for spiritual attention and love.

Detachment is sought through the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), whose supplication turns attention away from competing attachments and toward divine companionship.
