# primordial Covenant; the day of alast

A primordial covenant links each prophetic mission to a promised successor. The pattern described in these teachings is that a Manifestation foretells another, establishing signs and a bond that endures through the current dispensation until the promised figure appears. The sequence—from Abraham and Moses through Christ, Muhammad, the Báb, and Bahá’u’lláh—frames revelation as continuous rather than isolated. Each covenant asks believers to recognize the next stage of guidance when it comes.

The primordial pattern grounds the Bahá’í Covenant’s [power and centrality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=628D2EB989914C2DBBDDFEB888CA31CA) and the call to [firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633). It supports the [Covenant’s role in preserving unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E521EAE548DE4A4B9E087A00963908D0), defines the stakes of [Covenant-breaking](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42) and its [avoidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A971A1344BAEBC011C3866BFE27B179), and forms part of an ongoing process of [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69).
