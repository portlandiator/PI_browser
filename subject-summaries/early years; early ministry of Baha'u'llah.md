# early years; early ministry of Baha'u'llah

Bahá’u’lláh’s early years in Persia were marked by noble family origins, unusual learning, and a reputation for wisdom. Before his declaration as a Manifestation of God, he was active in charitable work and became a prominent supporter of the Báb. His early ministry included defending Bábís and responding to the movement’s persecution. These years show the transition from his public life in Persian society to his later religious mission.

[Bahá’u’lláh](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3491B0317F6A4084995CD331AD2C278E) provides the broader account of the figure whose early years and ministry are treated here. This connection places the period before his declaration within the wider story of his life and teachings.
