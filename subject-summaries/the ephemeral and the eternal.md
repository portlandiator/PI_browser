# the ephemeral and the eternal

The ephemeral and the eternal distinguish passing material conditions from enduring spiritual realities. The contrast directs attention away from attachment to temporary possessions and toward qualities, relationships, and purposes that retain value beyond bodily life. It does not deny the world’s reality, but places its changing forms in a wider spiritual perspective.

This distinction relates to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and [the transience of the physical world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), which together frame detachment as attention to lasting spiritual values amid temporal change.
