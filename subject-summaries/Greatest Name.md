# Greatest Name

The Greatest Name is a sacred designation associated with Bahá’u’lláh and with the transformative power of divine utterance. Its significance rests in its reference to God and the revelation made through the Manifestation, rather than in a magical force inherent in a word. Sacred language joins spiritual authority with the realms through which divine guidance reaches humanity.

The relation between divine and created realms is developed in [realms of being](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=53907D2638954598879977C301F16196), while the revelatory role of sacred utterance connects to [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22). The name’s specific association with Bahá’u’lláh is reflected in [Bahá’u’lláh](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3491B0317F6A4084995CD331AD2C278E), and its power through proclamation is related to [the power of words and speech](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A). Together these subjects show how a sacred name functions within a revealed religious context: it points beyond itself to the divine source and the person who bears its authority.
