# demons and evil spirits

Popular accounts of demons, devils, and the evil eye often give imagined beings power over human lives. Such control is treated as superstition, while “hidden” realities may be described symbolically and that living souls can affect one another. They distinguish this from claims that dead, spiritually harmful people roam the earth: departed souls do not wield such earthly control, and good is stronger than evil.

The question of unseen powers also appears in [occult sciences and psychic phenomena](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=666ED6B326A8EDED8D4E3FF383F97184), where divination and mediumistic claims are likewise treated cautiously. The excerpts distinguish spiritual influence from supposed material control by departed or evil beings.
