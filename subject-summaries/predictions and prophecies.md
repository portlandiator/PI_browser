# predictions and prophecies

Prophecy speaks of coming change through images of dawn, trumpet, justice, and a world transformed. Prophetic language anticipates martyrs, a Sun of Justice rising across East and West, and the appearance of the Sun of the Cause; some also foretell upheaval and the exposure of wrongdoing. Such forecasts range from spiritual renewal to historical events, with fulfillment understood in relation to the new Revelation.

Predictions of renewal and upheaval touch [the future of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=22EED87D5EEC4CA68E16AB238733762E), [Haifa and the Holy Land](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5AC79BF370C1E403616347F043187025), its [greatness in this Day](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7), and the coming [spiritualization of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7FD4EDEC44AD46EFB11981D4CAC32114), [world unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=91BFC765953C4C6D9943AF7361ADD675), [East–West communication](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DDED88E95778E61DFBBBA823FAF0AE38), and [racial unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=97E01B3F4DF6EC02175D948C7567769E). The imagery of light and darkness relates to [their integration and disintegration](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=494449F7A4D0EFBD315D4C3846620994), while the promised central voice is linked to [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22). Expectations also concern [future calamities and world convulsion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8CDDA7D99E83E1421D3C3542A49272FA), [war and peace](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=50D596B8CDE8EE327D4667F516197AF2), the future of [Iran](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4B7D2672283AE883B6CC489FBC7D4E7A), and a [universal language](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30894349063B41739E0027F705C40651). Prophecy offers [consolation and comfort](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14BC9A91715CE69B9FADD0DC5735323F), call for [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and frame [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977); its moral urgency also bears on [chastisement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118), [submission and repentance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA), and [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B). Its fulfillment relates directly to [prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797), and the specific [Zoroastrian tradition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B1D9153E2094E73B802A8F5BA05D083). Images of visions and hidden meaning connect it to [dream interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D23BC811AC9E800B36CF057A7209E03) and [mystical discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623), while [music](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4027C84B01D8438E9C7D92535DF6BAB7) can sound the promised joy. It affirms [humanity's oneness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D).
