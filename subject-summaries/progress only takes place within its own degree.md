# progress only takes place within its own degree

Progress unfolds within a given degree, while movement from one fundamental kind of reality to another is a different question. Improvement need not erase the nature or limits of what is being improved. The point is applied to human and spiritual advancement as well as distinctions between kingdoms. Progress means fuller realization within a station, rather than unrestricted transformation into any other kind.

[Law of transformation and change](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=77FE3431893F4FD6A31E4BC04DA1BEFC) notes that the soul does not evolve from one degree to another as a mechanical law; its approach to God is described through divine mercy and bounty. That connection sharpens the distinction between change within a degree and a shift of essential kind. Both subjects treat transformation as real while resisting a purely automatic account of spiritual progress.
