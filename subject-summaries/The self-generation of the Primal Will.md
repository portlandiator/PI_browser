# The self-generation of the Primal Will

The self-generation of the Primal Will describes its origin as an act of God’s command rather than an independent or co-eternal source. The Will is treated as a created universal reality through which other things come into being, preserving the distinction between the divine Essence and the creative order. This account connects divine self-knowledge and purpose with the origination of contingent existence.

This account touches [God’s love for His own Essence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=592F9B4AA5B5491F827732B4D4675681) and [God’s self-description and self-remembrance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=04F924E0CC51EDF12DBB02871348AD74), themes of divine self-relation that remain distinct from creation. [The Primal Will](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9D9002D8700F4A2D893B0869236D586F) names the universal reality whose origin is at issue, while [eternality versus origination](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BFA8DCA4C0C24066BAF0F869C88CF522) clarifies the difference between God’s unoriginate existence and created existence. [Creation of the world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E7BFAD48788E4AFE9B6EA752F7671AB1) extends the discussion to the order brought into being through the Will. These connections locate the Primal Will as a created intermediary in cosmological explanation, not a second eternal source alongside God.
