# high station of learning

Learning has a high station because it expands understanding, supports sound judgment, and helps people serve society. Knowledge includes more than collecting facts: it calls for insight, ethical purpose, and the ability to connect fields of thought. Scholarship can clarify teachings and relate them to the questions of the time. Learning is valued both as a human good and as a responsibility to contribute to the common life.

[Present and future expansion of arts and sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB8A8971F2EF480B9179519B95690878) looks toward continuing growth in knowledge. [Call to acquire knowledge; sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=157B1606343947178806DFE21D30B32B) stresses the duty to learn, while [High station of the arts and sciences](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E8D8CB2AB7034F7DB07173698FD9C979) connects learning with civilization and justice. [Education of children; moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4) shows how learning begins early and includes character. Together these links connect scholarship and science with education, social development, and responsible use of knowledge.
