# transcendence; unknowability of God

God's Essence lies beyond every human thought, image, and description. Even our highest ideas of perfection cannot encompass Him. Yet God can be known through His signs, attributes, and Manifestations, as sunlight is known through its reflection in a mirror. This revelation brings us into relationship with God without unveiling His inmost reality. The recognition of our limits deepens humility and wonder, while love moves us to pray and praise what we can never fully comprehend.

The adjacent subjects develop different implications of this distinction between God's Essence and His self-disclosure. [God's absolute freedom and independence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82A5773ED5884220A919FDF77F608B7B), together with [His power and sovereignty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E), express creation's dependence upon a reality it neither controls nor sustains. The [inability of the lower to comprehend the higher](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C957F3FF62F84A3A8DBA4B23EA26E8CC) illuminates why even the [Manifestations and Prophets are described as unable to encompass God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B492C6CABFF847E5814269886AE0C4E9). The [limits of language](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D52576587DA0EB888F569679044D948D) and the [impossibility of fully adequate worship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=173632E7BF104AB486EFBB383AF8DEAD) carry this humility into speech and devotion. The [names and attributes of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9AB88215A3AC410692D350AE3BEB3A54), and the distinction between [essential and actional attributes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B8ED66ABF42C4C94B1950954F56B46F0), explore how divine qualities and creative activity can be described while the Essence remains unknowable. Questions of [immanence and transcendence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6) concern God's presence through His signs without confinement within creation; those of [personal and impersonal reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0905F934B5D4402E84FCD0F0E325E0A7) bring intimate devotional address into conversation with the rejection of humanly fashioned images of God. Finally, [God's love for His own Essence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=592F9B4AA5B5491F827732B4D4675681) and the [cloud imagery of ‘amá](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C8C084ECDBD341D0AA2C1FA5716E66E3) connect the Hidden Treasure with the interplay of concealment, love, and revelation.
