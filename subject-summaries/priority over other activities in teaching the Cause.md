# priority over other activities in teaching the Cause

Teaching the Faith is an urgent duty in times of upheaval, when people seek answers and the message can offer hope. Believers are urged not to hoard its teachings or let other responsibilities eclipse teaching and teacher training. Focused effort during a rare opportunity matters, while care for children, the poor, and community affairs remains necessary and should not displace teaching.

This emphasis reinforces the broader [call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), which carries the message outward to people who have not yet heard it.
