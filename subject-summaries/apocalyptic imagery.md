# apocalyptic imagery

Apocalyptic imagery uses cosmic signs, upheaval, judgment, and renewal to describe the end of an age and the emergence of a new spiritual order. Such language may be symbolic rather than a literal timetable of physical events. Its meaning centers on transformation, recognition, and the renewal of religious life.

The imagery’s revelatory context connects with [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), whose appearance marks the renewal of spiritual guidance and the beginning of a new age.
