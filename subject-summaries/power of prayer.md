# power of prayer

Prayer is communion with God: an inward turning that can be voiced in words, silence, meditation, and supplication. It expresses love and dependence while refining the heart, widening spiritual capacity, and orienting action toward divine purpose. Asking for help does not set aside practical means; prayer and effort work together, while an answer may take a form different from the petitioner’s wish. Its value therefore reaches beyond receiving a requested outcome to the transformation and steadiness it cultivates.
