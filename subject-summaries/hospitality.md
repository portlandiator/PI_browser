# hospitality

Hospitality turns welcome into generous action: opening one’s home, receiving strangers warmly, sharing food, and making a guest feel honored even when resources are modest. Its worth rests less on the luxury of a meal than on the love with which it is offered. This practice extends to visitors, fellow believers, travelers, and the lonely, making a household or gathering a refuge where kindness and fellowship can be felt directly.

Hospitality expresses the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and is a practical form of [service to others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775).
