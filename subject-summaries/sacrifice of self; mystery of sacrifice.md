# sacrifice of self; mystery of sacrifice

Sacrifice of self is the willingness to subordinate personal desire—even life itself—to faithfulness, love, and service. The believer is urged to ascend to this station and offer life in the path of the Beloved; sacrifice can become a source of happiness and spiritual transformation. This ideal does not require reckless self-exhaustion: service remains sustainable when sacrifice is balanced with care and wisdom.

Self-sacrifice must be distinguished from [monasticism and asceticism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70B67FBB46F446D9835324D644050094); it stands against transience through [eternity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70850E6FB45E400FA41773E2557B5CF3), and is symbolized in the stories of [Abraham, Isaac, and Ishmael](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0FDA9B7CC77849A9B4E383D68116F833). It arises in the [condition of search](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA), amid [suffering and imprisonment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=370AC40D2874E1246B99EB8122E23C9A), and through the [Manifestation’s redemptive sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4B803FE7ECD54DB8A0941F918BB42943). The response is a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), shaped by [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) and the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). Sacrifice brings [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), expresses [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and requires [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668); its historical form includes [martyrdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A7271C05C12E4A3180E1E00F1DEBF977), while [love as fire](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9C38FD1F89964E0D9E88ADF23045DB9D) gives an inward image of purification. It supports [spiritual prerequisites for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67) and [fellowship](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A), faces [the transient world](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C), finds expression in [poetry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91840AE122AEC232D80E33023EC88DF), and endures through [tests and trials](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CC093CE37A394C1F81BEAF67251D3FC4).
