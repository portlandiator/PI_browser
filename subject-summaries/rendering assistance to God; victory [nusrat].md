# rendering assistance to God; victory [nusrat]

Rendering assistance to God, or nusrat, means serving the divine Cause through steadfast action and support. Believers are helpers of the Faith, rising to aid the Cause when its need becomes clear. Such assistance does not imply that God needs human aid; it names the honor and responsibility of taking part in work that advances spiritual and social good.

Assistance to God is a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), expressed through loyal service to the Cause.
