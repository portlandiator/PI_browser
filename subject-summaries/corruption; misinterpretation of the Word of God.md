# corruption; misinterpretation of the Word of God

Corruption and misinterpretation of the Word of God arise when sacred teachings are altered, taken out of context, or shaped by inherited assumptions. Preserving the text and interpreting it responsibly are distinct tasks: the first protects its wording, while the second seeks its intended meaning. The topic underscores the need for reliable transmission and careful reading.
