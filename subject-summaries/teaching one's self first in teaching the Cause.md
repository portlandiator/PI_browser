# teaching one's self first in teaching the Cause

A teacher must first live the counsel offered to others. Self-education in the Faith, deep study of its writings, reflection, prayer, and action help speech carry conviction rather than contradiction. Knowledge of history and contemporary issues can aid thoughtful conversation, while spiritual deepening nurtures love and unity. Teaching oneself is a continuing discipline, not a claim of perfection: the aim is to align conduct with the message and remain teachable while serving.

This inner preparation serves the wider [call to teach](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), so that conduct and knowledge support the message shared with others.
