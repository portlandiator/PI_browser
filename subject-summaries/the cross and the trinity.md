# the cross and the trinity

The cross is a central Christian symbol of Christ’s suffering, sacrifice, and redemption. The Trinity is a Christian doctrine describing the relationship of Father, Son, and Holy Spirit, though its formulations vary among traditions. Bahá’í discussions address both subjects through theological interpretation and comparison. They should be treated as distinct topics: one is a symbol and the other a doctrine about divine unity and distinction.
