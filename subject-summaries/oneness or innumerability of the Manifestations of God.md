# oneness or innumerability of the Manifestations of God

The Manifestations of God are one in spiritual reality yet many in their successive historical appearances. Their unity does not erase the distinct missions and names associated with each revelation, while their multiplicity does not imply separate divine sources. This understanding joins the continuity of religion with the renewal of guidance in different ages.

The spiritual unity of successive Manifestations relates to [the return of the Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4723FB63AE3C460CB0D434538890EED6), [divine unity and its degrees](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0CA85F9D98D4467C8454CE42CA5BB10D), and [oneness as unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD). The distinction between a shared spiritual reality and particular historical missions is developed in [differences between the Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=797723473B3647F38FDE65C86DBE2669). Together these subjects frame continuity and change as complementary: revelations share a source and foundation while addressing distinct conditions and bringing renewed teachings.
