# being a source of light; guidance

Guidance is divine direction that helps a person discern a path and act with purpose; becoming a source of light means helping others see that path through example, teaching, and service. The image of a lamp or sun distinguishes reflected guidance from an independent source: believers can illuminate their surroundings by receiving and sharing spiritual light. Personal desire can be mistaken for guidance, so discernment, prayer, and patience matter.

Guidance is pursued through the patient [search for truth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3BE6E42E9BA44C61A8C86EA2B6F82BEA) and understood against the contrast of [light and darkness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8B68A1DE8700E8697ABFF0E02CF7F845). Believers can share it through [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF), while [encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B) and [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) enable that work. Guidance should bear fruit in [service to humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2), echoed in a parallel topic on [human betterment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5A637BBBB913426BA5FEBBC58E0A6BF2-09862a80), and can prompt [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE) or specific [personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2). It is also related to [mystical insight](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623) and prayer for [others’ spiritual progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3). The guided life includes [spiritual wayfaring](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549) and must [produce results](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48DEE65D2ED14EB091B457DDD6377B17), serving the [unity of humanity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D). It depends on [spiritual recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), and may include [healing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FDA358C85EABE1A21EBBED1872E92D55), where spiritual and practical care meet.
