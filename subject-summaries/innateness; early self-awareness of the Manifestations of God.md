# innateness; early self-awareness of the Manifestations of God

A Manifestation’s awareness of His station is described through both innate knowledge and recognition at a particular moment. Several accounts affirm knowledge from the beginning and point to signs of extraordinary capacity in childhood; others describe an awakening or divine summons. Some notes explicitly acknowledge limits on what can be known about the nature of that consciousness. The topic holds together claims of pre-existing spiritual station and varied accounts of its recognition.

Awareness of station is closely related to the [obedience to; authority of the Manifestations of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10C0903A3A7048C193248AAA8629D4D4), since the cited accounts ground their authority in a reality distinguished from ordinary human learning and experience. One passage describes knowledge as innate rather than acquired, while others recount early signs or a divine awakening. The link brings together the question of how a Manifestation knows His mission and the authority attributed to His guidance, without making every source describe that awareness in the same way.
