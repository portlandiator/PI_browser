# spiritual springtime

Spiritual springtime is the renewal that dawns with a Manifestation of God. Like the return of spring in nature, a new Revelation brings fresh life, revives spiritual capacities, and makes possible new growth. The image also allows for recurring cycles: the divine source remains one, while each age has its own conditions and teachings. Spiritual renewal is therefore continuity with transformation, not merely a return to an earlier form.

Cycles in the physical and spiritual worlds supply the seasonal comparison at the heart of the image. The material world’s reflection of the spiritual gives a reason that natural seasons can illuminate spiritual renewal. Corruption and renewal concerns the clearing away of accumulated doctrines and customs, while spiritual transformation describes the changes in people that a new springtime can bring. Renewal is related to, but distinct from, the broader idea of changing religion across successive dispensations.
