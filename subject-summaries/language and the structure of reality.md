# language and the structure of reality

Language does more than label reality: words and symbolic forms shape how people perceive and communicate spiritual and material truths. The structure of language can reflect distinctions between outward expression and inward meaning, while translation carries these meanings across communities and traditions. Yet linguistic forms remain limited before realities that exceed expression.

The relation between expression and reality connects to [the material world as reflection of the spiritual](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2863570D944B4AFB91F6F5A9413971B7) and to [translation and publication of sacred writings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E9239F2F8F30E75D30C97D5222058E75), which carries language across cultures while preserving its meanings.
