# avoidance of Covenant-Breakers

Avoidance of Covenant-breakers is prescribed in strong terms as a safeguard for the community’s unity and faith. The guidance concerns persons identified as violating the Covenant, and urges believers to refrain from association rather than let factional conflict spread. This boundary is connected to spiritual protection, much as one avoids harmful influences, and is paired with loyalty to the Covenant and the example of Judas. It should not be generalized to ordinary disagreement or people outside that defined category.

This instruction follows the [primordial Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=32700D0B9FBB474CA2DF2E334114BC5B), responds to [Covenant-breaking](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42), and draws on the analogy of [Judas](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DC052035304CEABF417ECFC688C5A234).
