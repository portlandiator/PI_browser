# connection between material and spiritual worlds

Material and spiritual realities are related but distinct. Physical life can provide signs, conditions, and analogies for spiritual life, while spiritual influence can shape human perception and conduct in the material world. Bahá’í writings describe this relationship through reflection, correspondence, influence, and communication without reducing spiritual reality to physical processes.

[Mysteries and their discovery; the mystical vision](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623) concerns perceiving realities beyond immediate appearance. [Soul; spirit after death](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=79CD788B08C5E8B37070B3DD452FB313) extends the relationship beyond bodily life. [Spiritual communication; connections of the heart](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=63ABB434FE01EB0C75DABE2680B7A71E) describes inward connection, while [Material and spiritual existence; two books](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B) names the two domains directly. [Music and singing](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4027C84B01D8438E9C7D92535DF6BAB7) links material sound with spiritual nourishment; [Spiritual emotions and susceptibilities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) explores inward responses; and [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) shows spiritual influence expressed through betterment of others. [Material world a reflection of the spiritual](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2863570D944B4AFB91F6F5A9413971B7) states the correspondence most explicitly, while [the Kingdom of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417) gives a spiritual horizon for human development. [Relationship of the soul to the body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=52B1C3F2B9574C37BAF0ECCC8D5AB8C1) and [Mind as intermediary between soul and body](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E5D97C507CBE9BC48712BC3E937BE35) bring the connection into human nature. Together these links show multiple relations between domains without making them interchangeable.
