# material world a reflection of the spiritual

The material world can serve as a mirror or sign of spiritual realities. Outward forms and processes may suggest inward meanings, allowing physical experience to become a way of contemplating what is not directly visible. Reflection is an analogy: it does not make the material and spiritual worlds identical, nor does every physical event carry an obvious spiritual message. The idea instead invites careful interpretation of correspondences between the seen and unseen.

[Language and the structure of reality](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E846CC474C3649F5A86C027F93FA44B2) connects outward expression with meaning, using grammar and relation as a pattern for understanding reality. [Spiritual emotions and susceptibilities](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) describes inward states whose influence can appear in shared life. [Connection between material and spiritual worlds](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E928CB27776B41508EE8B5DE01F40D61) states the broader relation directly, and [Spiritual springtime](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1CCF69C3D48F493F8D2F9C43A2A04CD4) uses seasonal renewal as a spiritual analogy. [Spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) shows inward change expressed through service, while [Material and spiritual existence; two books](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=20396F06B6514E5EAF60E9440C8F2B5B) distinguishes the domains being related. [Poems and quotation from poetry](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F91840AE122AEC232D80E33023EC88DF) links figurative language to the expression of spiritual ideas. Together these neighbors show reflection operating through language, emotion, metaphor, and human action.
