# differences between the Manifestations; station of distinction

Each Manifestation of God has a distinct name, mission, revelation, and historical limitation, even as the Manifestations share a spiritual unity. This station of distinction explains why successive revelations bring teachings suited to different conditions and why their laws may differ without implying disagreement in their divine source. The differences belong to their missions in the world of creation.

The renewal of teachings is the focus of [progressive revelation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A868B8CCFDF44B2890211A0FAFE9F2B9), while [oneness as unity of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD) describes the shared foundation beneath differing dispensations. [Categories of Prophets](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=549FF19A14C34681BDC8A37E58CDEDB8) distinguishes universal Manifestations from other prophetic roles, and [oneness or innumerability of Manifestations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D94FA897F92748119D02B1738E91BD1C) relates distinction to spiritual unity. The differentiated stations of each Manifestation are further considered in [the twofold or threefold station](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=568978583E424499943612635F17DB7F). Together these links show how a common source can yield distinct missions, laws, and historical forms of guidance.
