# prayers (general or uncategorized)

General prayers address needs that do not belong to one narrow category: comfort, guidance, justice, strength, service, gratitude, or spiritual growth. Their variety reflects the ordinary breadth of devotional life, as people ask for help in circumstances that may overlap several concerns. Some are brief petitions; others are extended expressions of longing, weakness, and trust. They remain united by a turn toward God and the hope of receiving grace for life and action.

These varied petitions share the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) and may seek [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), divine [grace and capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E), or [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668). Some ask for [spiritual recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), showing how uncategorized prayer can still serve focused spiritual aims.
