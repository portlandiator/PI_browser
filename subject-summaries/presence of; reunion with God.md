# presence of; reunion with God

Presence and reunion with God name the believer’s hoped-for encounter with the divine, often expressed as longing to stand before the Manifestation and hear the revealed Word. Presence also has a spiritual sense: knowledge, recognition, and inward nearness remain possible when physical access is absent. Such attainment is treated as a supreme bounty, yet it is not automatic; many who desire it may be prevented, while God remains aware of every seeker.

This longing can reshape a person’s life through [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and depends on the inward [recognition sought in prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), extending beyond physical proximity to spiritual knowledge.
