# He Whom God Shall Make Manifest; the Bab and Baha'u'llah

“Him Whom God shall make manifest” is the title the Báb used for the promised Manifestation whose advent was the central purpose of his revelation. Bahá’í writings identify this figure with Bahá’u’lláh, whose declaration fulfilled the Báb’s expectation. The relationship joins two distinct dispensations: the Báb’s mission prepares the way, while Bahá’u’lláh’s revelation inaugurates a new era. Prophetic fulfillment here depends on spiritual recognition rather than outward resemblance.

[Prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) relates the Báb’s promise to Bahá’u’lláh’s declaration and to the interpretation of religious expectation. The connection clarifies how fulfillment can involve recognition of a new Manifestation, rather than a literal return in the same historical form.
