# goodly deeds; actions

Good deeds make spiritual conviction visible. Words, prayer, and study find their purpose when they lead to sustained, constructive action; a teacher’s life should confirm the message spoken. Actions can awaken others, reveal character, and bear lasting fruit. Daily community participation offers a place to practice principles, while deeds done with sincerity can serve both the Cause and humanity. The emphasis is not busyness for its own sake, but conduct whose results bring benefit and reflect inner purpose.

Action defines the [conduct of true believers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DB1352636234AF3A5E69C0111872F10) and gives practical expression to [prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AAA2181CBE0F4BFFAAFD92CC0FCB75B4) and [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775). Spirituality should [bear results](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48DEE65D2ED14EB091B457DDD6377B17) as part of the [virtuous path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), directed toward God’s [good pleasure](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=034E6E7433614B24B4A7683E29D0C241). Deeds are also central to the moral [prerequisites for teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), can foster [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and must accompany [words](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=938239E920FD40BEAE2E8DD30528D54A) when responding to a [call to action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE). Ethical consequences include [requital](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=9418F5521D7B489CA21A536FB126B118), while good conduct helps embody the [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7) and serve the [influence of the Cause](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1026FD55727C4292B730BB0C841E20E7). [Counsel](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3186621C7B97EB4AAD2CCA81C1358C2B), [sincerity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=98B67B70516949FD856C36A52AE23098), and [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=48991B831FEB414988B143D181EB0DEF) further shape the quality and aim of action.
