# critiques of capitalism, communism, socialism

Bahá’í writings critique capitalism, communism, and socialism when these ideologies treat material arrangements as sufficient answers to human need. They question systems that preserve extreme poverty amid great wealth, as well as attempts to create equality through force. The alternative emphasized is a just social order guided by spiritual principles, including voluntary sharing, protection of the poor, and laws that moderate economic extremes.
