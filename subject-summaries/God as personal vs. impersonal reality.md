# God as personal vs. impersonal reality

God cannot be confined to a human image, yet our relationship with Him can be deeply personal. Prayer turns toward a source of love and consolation whose Essence remains beyond comprehension. The Manifestations of God reveal divine qualities in a form human beings can recognize and love. Intimate devotion need not turn God into an enlarged human being, and transcendence need not make spiritual life cold or impersonal.

[Transcendence and unknowability](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F3B3303C979E42428EFC5629B545E4E4) establish why neither personal nor impersonal language can fully define God. [Immanence and transcendence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=183823600ED74219B463809E7A4803D6) ask a related question: how divine signs can be present throughout creation while the Essence remains beyond it. The [station of the Manifestation of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) gives this discussion a concrete focus, for the Manifestations reveal the qualities through which God becomes known and loved. Their [identity and relationship to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB) explain the language of divine presence through the image of a perfect mirror, without equating the mirror with the sun itself. These four connections place personal devotion alongside humility about its object: a believer may turn toward God with trust and affection while recognizing the limits of every mental picture.
