# Manifestation of God the only route to knowledge of God

Knowledge of God is attained through recognition of the Manifestation, since the divine Essence itself is beyond human comprehension. The Manifestation makes divine attributes and guidance knowable without rendering God’s inner reality accessible. This mediating path gives religious knowledge a historical and revelatory form: people encounter divine guidance through the Manifestation of their age.

[The Manifestation as mediator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=83C2E82C31DB4115A3CDF5884D0CB19E) describes the boundary between the unknowable Essence and the created world, while [knowledge of attributes as knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A7BDCD83AE14B19B4D78332D456B1D3) explains recognition as the means of knowing divine qualities. [The presence of the Manifestation as the presence of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=82D2161C8BB4E087647B84F29B41D6D3) extends this principle to the language of encounter. Together these subjects distinguish knowledge of God through revelation from direct comprehension of the divine Essence.
