# prayer for firmness in the Covenant

Firmness in Bahá’u’lláh’s Covenant means remaining faithful to its provisions and center of authority through uncertainty, opposition, and tests. The prayers ask for steadfast hearts, clear recognition, and strength to serve, while some also plead for protection against doubt and division. This commitment is both personal and communal: believers seek stability in their own faith and a united community able to carry forward its spiritual work.

This is prayer’s practical force, drawing on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) to seek endurance. Covenant firmness is distinguished from the related concern of [Covenant-breaking](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=42BA11FC5FE24CBEA769298634A7FA42) and is one form of broader [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668). The more specific topic [firmness in the Covenant](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1A99E9F886AA4DB89E927820FA635633) gathers the same commitment as a subject in its own right. Petitions may be made for [individuals and groups](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343), and the prayers place fidelity alongside [submission and service to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA).
