# firmness in the Covenant

Firmness in the Covenant means remaining loyal to Bahá’u’lláh’s appointed provisions and preserving the unity they are meant to protect. It is tested by opposition, doubt, and the actions of Covenant-breakers, yet believers are urged to respond with steadfastness, prayer, and continued service rather than factional conflict. The Covenant acts as a living force in the community, bringing spiritual assistance and clarity. Its strength appears in fidelity that sustains fellowship and teaching through difficult times.
