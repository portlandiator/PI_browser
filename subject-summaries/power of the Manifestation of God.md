# power of the Manifestation of God

The power of the Manifestation of God is the divine authority and spiritual efficacy expressed through revelation. It is not independent sovereignty apart from God: the Manifestation’s utterance, healing, guidance, and influence derive from the divine source. This power is made evident in the transformation of individuals and societies and in the capacity of revealed words to illuminate human understanding.

The source of this power is [God’s omnipotence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E), while its historical bearer is situated in [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5). The divine authority of revealed speech appears in [the Manifestation speaking in the voice of Divinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25), and the role of utterance in conveying guidance is connected with [the Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22). Together these themes clarify that the Manifestation’s power is both real and derivative: it acts with divine authority while remaining distinct from God.
