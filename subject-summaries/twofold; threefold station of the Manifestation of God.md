# twofold; threefold station of the Manifestation of God

The twofold or threefold station of the Manifestation distinguishes the human body, the rational soul, and the unique spiritual reality associated with divine revelation. The corporeal station is shared with humanity and subject to physical conditions; the revelatory station has a spiritual scope beyond ordinary human capacity. These distinctions help explain how the Manifestation is genuinely human while bearing a unique relation to divine guidance.

The differentiated stations are situated within [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5) and clarify [the relationship of the Manifestation to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=00BA6D4E6EF645E480AB0B732786C2BB). The revelatory voice is explored in [speaking in the voice of Divinity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=618FD8010F10E760CBC53B05C0F1DC25), while the difference between successive missions appears in [the station of distinction](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=797723473B3647F38FDE65C86DBE2669). Together these connections distinguish shared human embodiment from the unique spiritual authority and scope of the Manifestation, without collapsing either station into the divine Essence.
