# church and state in the future

Religion and government have distinct responsibilities, yet each can contribute to social well-being. Bahá’ís are not to seek partisan power or displace civil authority; they should respect their countries’ constitutions. In a future commonwealth, religious and civic institutions may cooperate, a prospect distinct from present-day state governance or clerical rule.
