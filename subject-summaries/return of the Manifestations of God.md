# return of the Manifestations of God

The return of a Manifestation means the renewal of divine qualities, authority, and purpose in a later revelation. As spring returns with fresh flowers, spiritual perfections reappear through a new individual life. The relationship between Elijah and John the Baptist illustrates this meaning of return. Recognition depends on discerning the enduring spirit beneath a changed name and outward form, allowing the continuity of revelation to become visible across different ages.

The account of returning qualities sits beside the theme of the [oneness or innumerability of the Manifestations of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D94FA897F92748119D02B1738E91BD1C): successive figures may be understood as distinct appearances while bearing a shared spiritual reality. The passages also distinguish identity from attributes, which resonates with the proposed connection to [matter; essence; substance and form](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A8FA3FC94D38433295A413FBDA06FC3D), where one reality is described through different forms and names. The comparison is suggestive rather than a claim that these topics are identical: the return passages focus on prophetic perfections across dispensations, while the linked philosophical material considers how reality and its forms are related.
