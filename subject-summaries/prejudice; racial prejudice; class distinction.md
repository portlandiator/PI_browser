# prejudice; racial prejudice; class distinction

Prejudice divides people by race, class, religion, nationality, and inherited status, obscuring their shared humanity. Bahá’í teachings call for equal dignity and active protection of minority rights. Blind imitation perpetuates prejudice unless people investigate truth and deliberately build unity across difference.

[Oneness of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5F1349F0A072481181E9D852C41EC6BD) connects prejudice to the deeper unity among faiths. [Enumerations of Bahá’í principles](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=10F20171888446BAB9501BF1262D49C7) gather teachings into a shared ethical program, while [blind imitation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E0F43FD317AEC54131AD435E2317B05) identifies inherited assumptions as a root of discrimination. [Harmony of science and religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=99656E1B1D7344D8930ACA530FDE210B) protects inquiry from dogmatic bias, and [human unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8677D5CBAD964C7F95A61AF10D5EB72D) gives equality its social foundation. [Unity in diversity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A20E909C76AB45A7A3B172F91D3C4D97) preserves cultural identities without hierarchy; [nationalism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=E5EFFF9275424573B014771437ACCE2C) must not become antagonism toward other peoples. [Race unity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=97E01B3F4DF6EC02175D948C7567769E) confronts racial prejudice directly, [qualities of a Bahá’í](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AD7F1E7F696842D3B9D7E108934676B7) describe the conduct needed to resist it, and [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649) motivates service across divisions.
