# return of Christ; second coming of Christ

The return of Christ is a Christian expectation that Bahá’í writings interpret as the return of the same spiritual reality in a new Manifestation, rather than the literal reappearance of Jesus in the same body. The idea links prophecy, recognition, and religious renewal. Its meaning depends on distinguishing the enduring spiritual station from the outward identity and historical circumstances of a messenger.
