# servitude; submission to God; repentance

Servitude, submission, and repentance turn the will toward God and align action with a higher order. The skilled cook’s effortless movement images action in harmony with the Way; the believer bows before God’s gracious Kingdom. Submission is not passivity or abasement: it is freedom from ego’s obstruction, accompanied by repentance and willing service.

Submission is joined to [humility](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=F98B57795BFF4F67AF474B13EE107703), [prayer for others](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3), and [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C). It calls for [trust](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D1CA07BA971AE54EB4BCE0ABB94841E7), supports [eternity over evanescence](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=70850E6FB45E400FA41773E2557B5CF3), and serves [teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67), [transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69), and the [spiritual path](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549). It can be strengthened by [prophetic expectation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FF9A4A182AA5EB45D01627817D176E5E), [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), and rejection of [worldliness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BD88D70EC7AEE658A00C66FE24E0CA85), and expressed through [personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2). Its roots lie in [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), while [prayer for individuals](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343), [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), and [prayer for Covenant firmness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C988A6875D85E5BE715D95DDF7075DB4) sustain it. The soul also seeks [recognition](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CF9408FBB1DDEA91EBA67EA2F1C015D8), [service](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=75315FCCBA5D4B54A22808A1159B1775), and the example of [‘Abdu’l-Baha](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4D2AFD7905384761BB166DCD6782B315), while gaining perspective on [transience](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=CBBEF627F45A45E7850653204203438C).
