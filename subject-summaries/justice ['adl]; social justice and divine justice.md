# justice ['adl]; social justice and divine justice

Justice means giving people their rights, judging fairly, and arranging society so that power and resources do not serve only the strong. It has personal, social, and spiritual dimensions: individuals must act justly, institutions must protect rights, and rulers are accountable for the welfare of those they govern. Justice supports unity and peace, while mercy tempers judgment with compassion. The principle applies from everyday dealings to the organization of public life.
