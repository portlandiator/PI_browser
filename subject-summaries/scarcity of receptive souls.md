# scarcity of receptive souls

Spiritual receptivity can be uncommon: many may hear an invitation or encounter teachings, yet only some recognize their significance and respond. This rarity can discourage teachers, but small numbers do not imply insignificance; early believers are remembered as a handful whose influence later spread widely. The image of scarce precious things suggests that receptive hearts are valued, while other passages urge continued invitation and service rather than resignation over those who do not yet listen.
