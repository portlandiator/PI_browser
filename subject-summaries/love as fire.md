# love as fire

Love as fire is a purifying force that exposes and consumes harmful habits. Its first effect may be painful rather than comforting: the soul sees its own weakness in the flame before it can shine more brightly. Yet this fire can spread warmth and light far beyond the individual, turning inward refinement into a force for service and spiritual renewal.

The fire is kindled by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), calls the believer to [action](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=64297813E037448AB17A91940A7008DE), and works through [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Its light is carried by [speech](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2F9E3A01AFCCEAC5262DC3738E50F13A) and [enkindlement in teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=023F2C5312FE4C9BA59624CDFA1998E1), while [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C) helps the soul endure purification. Its imagery joins [fire and light](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7112D8A18D3C4C7BB98681C6DE3D94E0), and its effects include [honesty](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=EF0FF1BFD66BE7BE40FF7CAA4012DDF3), [love as a foundation of religion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C7824F7FB65A4B75A74AC15AFD08EC5C), the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), [self-sacrifice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2BEF6248EF094C6E9F1AE87D842D03E0), and the [spiritual prerequisites of teaching](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8AC80D4CBCF3EC2FB87444DBA8BEBD67). It touches [spiritual emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B) and calls forth [praise](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B).
