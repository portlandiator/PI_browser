# false claims to divine revelation or spiritual stations

A claim to divine revelation or a privileged spiritual station requires discernment, not automatic belief. Self-appointed claimants may use fabricated words, predictions, or assertions of hidden knowledge to gather followers and create division. They also set the specific Bahá’í safeguard that no new Manifestation may be acknowledged before the completion of the promised thousand-year period, while urging compassion if a claimant recants.

The time boundary here is connected to the [duration of the Bahá’í cycle and future divine revelations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1DECA0446C2D4E74BF311F15691FB7A7): the assurance of future guidance does not authorize an immediate claimant to present themselves as the promised successor.
