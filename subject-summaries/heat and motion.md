# heat and motion

Heat can cause or accompany physical motion, while warmth also serves as an image for divine speech and love that quickens the heart. Some explanations use active and receptive forces to account for heat; others associate warmth with ascent, lightness, or released energy. These cosmological formulations and spiritual metaphors are related through imagery but are not identical claims.

The immediate link is [all things are in motion; kinds of motion](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4CB12B78171B4DC2A79D934CD45898C5), whose passages frame motion as a general feature of existence and distinguish forms of physical and spiritual change. Another link, [the Word of God; influence and centrality of](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22), clarifies the theological register: an example there calls the Word a center of illumination for thought and morals. Read together, the subjects trace how a physical image of heat and movement can also express the life-giving influence attributed to divine utterance, while preserving the difference between explanatory metaphor and accounts of natural processes.
