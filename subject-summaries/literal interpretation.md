# literal interpretation

Literal interpretation reads a text according to its surface wording, but sacred language may also use symbol, metaphor, or spiritual reference. A purely literal reading can miss the intended meaning, while abandoning the text’s wording can invite arbitrary interpretation. Sound reading attends to both language and the reality the passage signifies.

The interpretation of sacred symbols connects with [angels](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=2A6D62207284EAED0144C1FDBFABE5CD), [color and light symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5DF1FBB2026CE6E1050C257923FD3853), [Satan and the Evil One](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=193C91CD0633EC4761A43270D9F9E963), and [symbolism](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BF2B7E937407E648DD80C2528263A450). Historical and scriptural contexts include [Judaism and the Torah](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=93585856EDADED995488E210D42FFF2E) and [the rejection of Christ](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B2340566123BECE11972A4501C75B8C1). Questions of textual change relate to [corruption and renewal of religious dogmas](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A3B6DA84931BE1A9E2A91755E047538C), while [outward and inward meanings](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3568B942A8FE439CBD3238E92AE8D80E) and [multiple scriptural interpretations](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB987894E7264233BA0C0C5DAB4D3C5F) describe readings beyond the surface. [Miracles and their interpretation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3E78A8B4557E4886A5D3BC8F84BFB09E) also shows how scriptural language may carry meanings not captured by literal description alone.
