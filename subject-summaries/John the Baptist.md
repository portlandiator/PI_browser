# John the Baptist

John the Baptist is a prophetic figure whose announcement prepares the way for Christ in Christian scripture and Bahá’í interpretation. His role stands at a transition between religious dispensations, linking expectation with the appearance of a new revelation. Accounts of John emphasize witness, readiness, and the response of those who heard him. His significance is best understood within the different scriptural traditions that remember him.

[Stories; anecdotes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=7A8DB05467DBEF2954FD9B30F84AA0E8) provides a narrative setting for accounts of John and other religious figures. The connection places his life within remembered episodes without claiming that every anecdote is a canonical account.
