# naming of children; of individuals

Naming a child or individual can express prayer, honor, and a sense of spiritual identity. A newborn may receive a recommended name; names also have a spiritual dimension, and people are called ‘Abdu’l-Baha or ‘Abdu’l-Husayn. A name may thus serve ordinary identification while carrying devotional meaning and a wish for the person’s future life.

Naming may be part of [personal instructions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D639D12CD679E3ED02A0F7A8D7835DB2), linked with [praise and encouragement](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A61D5813EBB7E2C5981ECFA84987639B), or included in a [prayer for specific people and groups](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=26E85C22B274E5695DA94BA1A3A0A343).
