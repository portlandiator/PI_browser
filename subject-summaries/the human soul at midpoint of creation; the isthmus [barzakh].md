# the human soul at midpoint of creation; the isthmus [barzakh]

The human soul occupies an intermediate station between the material and spiritual realms, often described as an isthmus or barzakh. It participates in bodily life yet can perceive spiritual realities, so it is neither purely material nor wholly detached from embodiment. This middle position helps explain the soul’s role in human perception and spiritual development.

The soul’s intermediate station relates to [the realm of similitudes](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4B07E7C749314DBEB8C4158C26A02453), a mode through which spiritual realities may be perceived in symbolic forms. Together they distinguish spiritual experience from ordinary physical perception while locating human consciousness between material and immaterial worlds.
