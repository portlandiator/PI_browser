# courage

Courage and valor are spiritual qualities as well as physical bravery. Courage marks the truthful and servants of God; it consists in steadfastness when opposed. A person renowned for bodily valor is urged to become known instead for spiritual courage and detachment. Courage therefore appears as fidelity under pressure, guided by truth and directed toward a higher purpose.

Courage is one quality of [the spiritual wayfarer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549).
