# unity of thought and belief

Unity of thought grows from a shared spiritual purpose and the willingness to act together. Differing opinions can enrich deliberation, but an Assembly's decision calls for wholehearted support, even from those who preferred another course. Respectful requests for reconsideration and appropriate appeals remain possible. Common action, freed from persistent contention, allows experience to reveal mistakes and helps a community correct them without sacrificing its unity.

[Consultation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=25A32FCFEDFE4DDF82D196BFF21A9DFC) provides the practical setting in which varied views can contribute to a common decision. [Independent investigation of truth](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=30A42A15DD044876BA9A0BA8D1D60330) calls for sincere understanding rather than blind imitation, while [love, unity, and fellowship among the friends](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B002D95B61D1427697DF9636DF3B6E0A) guard the relationships within which inquiry occurs. Together these connections join freedom to think with responsibility for collective action: conviction need not become contention, and cooperation need not imply that every participant began with the same opinion.
