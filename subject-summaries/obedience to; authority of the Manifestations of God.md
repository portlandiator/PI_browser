# obedience to; authority of the Manifestations of God

Obedience to the Manifestations of God follows from their authority as bearers of divine revelation. Their teachings guide individual conduct and establish religious laws suited to a dispensation; their authority is understood as derived from God rather than as independent sovereignty. The obedience joins spiritual allegiance with the practical guidance and education of communities.

The authority of the Manifestations rests on [the power and sovereignty of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B4480B0CDB52EF0534E437134978DC1E) and is expressed through [the station of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=703AB631CB76E66408E7D6F7160F01F5). Its relation to [infallibility and sinlessness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=46FD5CCF1C43454DB91FC516E5D3B7F7) helps explain the reliability of revealed guidance, while [the essential distinction of the Manifestation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4657EFC24412422099AA26E26F483158) identifies the unique status behind that authority. The origin of this station is considered through [innateness and early self-awareness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=8C7168F542A84B6CB338D26C0D301AC9), and its purpose in human life through [the Manifestation as educator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=54135CB53EEE434D8B77F1C91F81A26C). Together these links connect obedience with revelation, authority, and the spiritual training of humanity.
