# Covenant-breaking and Covenant-breakers

Covenant-breaking threatens the unity and integrity of the Baha'i community, and believers are instructed to avoid those formally identified as violators. This boundary is understood as protection of the Covenant and the Faith, not a license for hostility toward ordinary disagreement. Historical narratives connect the issue with tests, persecution, and internal disputes; the response urged is firmness, prayer, and loyalty to the Covenant's established authority.
