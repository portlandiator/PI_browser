# idolatry

Idolatry includes worship of carved images, but the term also reaches inward: desire, self, and vain imaginings can become idols when they displace recognition of God. The call to shatter idols therefore asks people both to turn from material objects of worship and to release false conceptions that obstruct recognition of divine truth. Christian examples concern mistaken worship, not a blanket judgment of Christians.

The recorded link to [Christian doctrine and practice](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=3923920E5182EE6A6F0687657820B538) reflects passages that discuss Christian worship and the distinction between outward forms and recognition of spiritual truth.
