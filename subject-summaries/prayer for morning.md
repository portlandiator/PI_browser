# prayer for morning

Morning prayer begins the day by turning attention toward God before ordinary demands take hold. Dawn becomes a time for praise, remembrance, and spiritual readiness, when worshippers ask for guidance and renew their intention to live faithfully. The practice may include prescribed words, private supplication, or orienting the heart toward the sacred. Its central rhythm is attentiveness: each new day opens with a deliberate effort to receive it in a spirit of devotion.

Morning devotion is one expression of the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), giving spiritual communion a regular place at the start of the day.
