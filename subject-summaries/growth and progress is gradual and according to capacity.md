# growth and progress is gradual and according to capacity

Living beings develop according to their capacities, and progress takes time. Bahá’í writings compare differences in growth to the way the same sunlight reaches a plain, a tree, and a jewel with different effects. Human education and spiritual refinement likewise unfold through effort, opportunity, and divine bounty. This principle resists demands for uniform results: each person and community can advance, but not in identical measure or on an imposed timetable.
