# prayer for spiritual recognition

Spiritual recognition is an awakened capacity to perceive divine signs, hear the call of revelation, and know the Manifestation rather than merely repeat religious words. The prayers ask for illumined sight, attentive hearing, and a heart freed from veils so knowledge becomes lived orientation. Recognition is received through grace, yet it also asks for effort: detachment, remembrance, and faithful action prepare a person to respond to what has been perceived.

The [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687) is itself a means of seeking recognition, while the [Manifestation as the Sun](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B31B67349BA147D8A57CDBAF1EDE3B35) and the [Word of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4E323D7B85E34704B18BD5AB68F84E22) provide the source and center of revealed illumination. Receptivity involves [detachment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=379BEFFCBC0C4B9683034D7269624B3C), the enlivening [water of life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AC6D3AECF43E478BB1E3EC19DB04E992), and [nearness to God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C22A37A43832E506617EB2F4434E855F); it also requires [guidance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=B1D5DEBD4BDBE604BF563353CF2199E1) and can lead to [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69). Recognition is not universal or automatic, so it must be distinguished from [belief and denial](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=1B9173457DB3E756B5CA9F035EFD7CC9). The heart’s response is animated by [love of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=410D6617D11A4266BA52B4B00A48D649), and petitions may seek [others’ spiritual progress](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AB07CB83562E4E8FB296FD5FA74B7DB3). Metaphors such as the [wine of transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=5C70FAED65314EFAA8B49E0D3FEC8102) express the gifts of [grace and capacity](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=6DFF61AF1A174D40865772C3AB40FF7E). The prayers also touch [mystical discovery](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=112D55EA871649C98F620B987B18D623), [wayfaring and virtuous life](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB07A9A48FD421E8F6E02D219149549), [reunion with God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=BB202C3CE69D47D19B5138D2E2FFE802), and entry into the [Kingdom](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=FAF773D669C84BC49FB837C8DC094417), as well as [prophecy and fulfillment](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=A72CD9234D5E4386A7C153F8E08CF797) and the broader [knowledge of God](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=0DD5324F4D5F4083B4CAA0D5360C7218). These ideas meet in the distinction between [nearness and remoteness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DBB0490F3653EEB9948DF8F6B543DC7C); [general prayers](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=738F8F7E4890E6B818B5ACE3C3036604) ask for aid, while [steadfastness](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D3D5A6BA921F4EFD915F7B12D2779668), spiritual [emotions](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=14C5477A6EE041A3B19DAD048E3FBB5B), [submission and repentance](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=AEF71C54D9B047EB8B92DBF1831755FA), and [cultivation of the soul](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=D5D98C8A2EDA475EBC48A0E84E2AFE1D) describe dispositions that sustain recognition.
