# prayer for children and youth

Children and young people need care, protection, education, and room to grow into spiritual and moral responsibility. Prayer for them asks that early life be nourished by love, guidance, and security, and that learning develop character as well as knowledge. Images of seeds, saplings, and a gardener express this gradual cultivation: growth is entrusted to divine care while families and communities help provide the conditions in which young people can flourish.

These petitions draw on the [power of prayer](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=374926D1B4A0440A807608CC09170687), and their garden imagery resonates with the [Manifestation as gardener and cultivator](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=DD46AC953147458BAB7F06770FD865D5). Their hopes for protection and formation lead toward [spiritual transformation](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=C068EBF06ABC4693BF6C2868E2385C69) and the practical work of [children’s moral education](https://portlandiator.github.io/PI_browser/subjects.html?subject=4527819F4272482F94777C3D36326FD4), including organized classes and community support.
