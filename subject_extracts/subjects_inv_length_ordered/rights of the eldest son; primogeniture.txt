The intention of firstborn is the eldest of the surviving male children; it doth not include the eldest of female children, even if she be the eldest of all children. And when He mentioneth male children excluding females, the meaning is that perhaps a person hath ten male children: if the eldest dieth, the second taketh his place; if the second dieth, the third taketh his place; if the third dieth, the fourth taketh his place. Therefore, in the blessed verse it is expressed in the plural.  AB10156
