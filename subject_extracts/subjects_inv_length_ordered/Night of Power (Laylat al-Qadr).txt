We have adorned the Night of Power with the letter "H" that the people of Baha may attain certitude through this Most Great, Supreme Name  BH09470

O my God! This is a holy night which Thou hast blessed with Thy favor and distinguished by revealing therein the Qur'an through Thy grace, making it better than a thousand months through Thy generosity.  BB00076

The Remembrance, the Letter Ha, has indeed descended around the Fire on the Night of Power. Can you perceive aught of the true measure of the Night of Power through the Remembrance's truth? Nay, then nay! The Night of Power is better than a thousand months, and this has been inscribed in the Mother Book.  BB00002

And make not difficult for thyself the truth that in a thousand months there must needs be found a number of Nights of Power. How can mortal mind conceive of those months which God Himself hath equated therewith? Indeed, it referreth to the duration of Umayyad rule - may God's curse rest upon them throughout all ages and eternities - wherein no Night of Power was to be found, for the basis of equivalence is a thousand months from which the Night of Power hath been withdrawn. This is a night which Thou hast magnified, honored, exalted, adorned and sanctified by associating it with Thyself. Thou hast caused to appear therein a sign that proclaimeth it to be a wondrous night of a wondrous month, which Thou hast favored, honored, exalted, magnified, illumined, elevated and loved. Therein do those who call upon Thee and glorify Thee in the watches of the night and the hours of the day, and Thou hast given leave to the angels to raise unto Thee the voices of Thy believing servants, Thy assured ones, Thy chosen ones, Thy beloved ones and those who are detached from all else save Thee.
