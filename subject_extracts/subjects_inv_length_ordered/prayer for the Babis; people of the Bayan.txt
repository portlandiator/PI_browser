O God! Bestow all Thy goodness upon them that have entered the Bayan, and send down, O Lord, the hosts of Thy victory upon the letters of Thy Name, the Most Merciful.   BB00199

Therefore, O my God, by that torrential rain, do Thou send down upon the people of the Bayan the rain of Thy triumph, Thy aid, Thy victory, Thy assistance, Thy support, and Thy succor, and whatsoever Thou hast encompassed in Thy knowledge of the modes of Thy support and the manifestations of Thy victory  BB00371

I beseech Thee to have mercy and to bestow Thy grace upon the people of the Bayan, protecting them from that which may descend upon them from the signs of Thy wrath and the tokens of Thy anger and the manifestations of Thy might in these dark, destructive, temptation-filled, blind, deaf, pitch-black and gloomy days.  BH01771

Therefore, O God, send Thy blessings upon the Divine Point and upon the Letters of the Living, and bestow through that Most Exalted Similitude and Most Resplendent Garment upon me and upon all who have believed in God and His signs, in every condition whatsoever befitteth the loftiness of Thy bounty and Thy gift, and the glory of the detachment of Thy generosity and Thy favor.  BB00254

Therefore I beseech Thee by Thyself - for nothing can equal Thy justice, none can be Thy peer, none can resemble Thee, none can be Thy companion, and nothing can be Thy likeness - and by Thy Glory, Thy Majesty, Thy Beauty, Thy Greatness, Thy Light, Thy Names, Thy Might, Thy Words, Thy Wisdom, Thy Knowledge, Thy Power, Thy Command, Thy Will, Thy Honor, Thy Sovereignty, Thy Dominion, Thy Loftiness, Thy Favor, Thy Grace, Thy Bounty, Thy Generosity, Thy Kindness, Thy Gift, Thy Benevolence, Thy Glory, Thy Transcendence, Thy Utterance, Thy Inaccessibility, Thy Greatness, Thy Independence, Thy Splendor, and Thy Lights, to send down upon Thy loved ones in the Bayan, from the first whom Thou hast witnessed unto the last, that which befitteth the sublimity of Thy holiness and the exaltation of Thy glory.
