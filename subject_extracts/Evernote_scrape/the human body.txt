



The human form is the greatest proof of God in His creation. It is the Book that He has written with His own hands. It is the Temple that He has reared with His wisdom. It is the universal compendium of the forms of all the worlds. It is the epitome of the Well-Guarded Tablet [hiya al-mukhtasar min al-lawh al-mahfuz]. It is the witness of all that is hidden. It is the proof unto all who deny. It is the straight path, and the bridge suspended between paradise and the fire. (Hadith of 'Ali, quoted by the Bab in the Suriy-i-va'l-'Asr, provisional translation)
