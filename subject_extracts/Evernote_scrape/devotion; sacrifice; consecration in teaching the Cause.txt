



The first and most important qualification of a Baha'i Teacher is, indeed, unqualified loyalty and attachment to the Cause. Knowledge is, of course essential; but compared to devotion it is secondary in importance. DG#186 (14 Nov. 1935 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)

Entire and selfless devotion is what is most needful. The brighter our torch burns, the more light will it give and the more readily will it impart its blaze to others.... COC#1935 (3 May 1925 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)

Teaching the Cause meets with the greatest response when the person is completely consecrated to that work, as consecration and devotion bring the confirmations of the Holy Spirit. (30 Dec. 1952 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)

Dedication to the heavenly task, complete consecration to the noble mission, and living the life, are the qualities needed for success in the teaching field. These virtues attract the confirmations of the Holy Spirit, and thus the individual becomes the reed through which that Holy Spirit may confirm the seeking souls. (30 Dec. 1953 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi) (1st sentence in HE.056)

...if one is completely dedicated, seeking each day how to bring some of the spirit of life to a seeker, and sacrifices time, wealth, and person to the Faith he serves, then he will surely win great victories, and his teaching work be confirmed. (10 May 1956 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)

Teaching the Cause of God is the source of divine confirmation; however, teaching is dependent upon complete dedication to the Cause, and complete consecration. If an individual can become a reed through which the Holy Spirit may move to another individual who is being contacted, then there will be true success and spiritual growth on the part of the recipient of the Message. Thus the state of dedication and consecration of the individual is most important in the teaching work. (4 Oct. 1952 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)
