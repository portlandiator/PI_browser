



Prophets are in general of two kinds. Some are independent Prophets Who are followed, while others are not independent and are themselves followers. SAQ#43.2 (was SAQ.164)

There are three kinds of divine Prophets. One kind are the universal Manifestations, which are even as the sun.... Another kind of Prophets are followers and promulgators, not leaders and law-givers... Yet another kind are Prophets Whose prophethood has been limited to a particular locality. BRL.TTT#01
