



'Abdu'l-Baha gives no reason whatever why Friday has been chosen as the day of rest in the Baha'i calendar. He just affirms it. LG#372 (10 July 1939 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)

We Baha'is have a weekly Holy Day, or rather a day of rest. But as we are few in number we up until the present time follow the day of rest of the country in which we are living. In the future the Baha'is will observe their own day of rest. (31 May 1949 on behalf of Shoghi Effendi)
