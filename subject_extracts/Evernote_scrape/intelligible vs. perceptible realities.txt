It [the human spirit] even understands things that have no outward existence, that is, intelligible, imperceptible, and unseen realities such as the mind, the spirit, human attributes and qualities, love and sorrow--all of which are intelligible realities. SAQ#48.4 (was SAQ.186)

...the mind itself is an intelligible reality and has no outward existence. SAQ#74.2 (was SAQ.263)

...the essential reality underlying any given phenomenon is unknown.  Phenomenal, or created, things are known to us only by their attributes... the qualities appertaining to this organism [a flower] are perceptible to us, but the intrinsic elemental reality, or identity, of it remains unknown.
