



And when this process of progressive Revelation culminated in the stage at which His peerless, His most sacred, and exalted Countenance was to be unveiled to men's eyes, He chose to hide His own Self behind a thousand veils, lest profane and mortal eyes discover His glory. This He did at a time when the signs and tokens of a divinely-appointed Revelation were being showered upon Him -- signs and tokens which none can reckon except the Lord, your God, the Lord of all worlds. And when the set time of concealment was fulfilled, We sent forth, whilst still wrapt within a myriad veils, an infinitesimal glimmer of the effulgent Glory enveloping the Face of the Youth, and lo, the entire company of the dwellers of the Realms above were seized with violent commotion and the favored of God fell down in adoration before Him. Fear ye God and entertain no doubts regarding His Cause, for verily, the Mystery of this Gate is shrouded in the mystic utterances of His Writ and hath been written beyond the impenetrable veil of concealment by the hand of God, the Lord of the visible and the invisible. SWB.057

The splendour of His appearance far surpasseth that of any other appearance. His divine concealment is far more profound than any other concealment. SWB.056

The revelation of the Divine Reality hath everlastingly been identical with its concealment and its concealment identical with its revelation. That which is intended by 'Revelation of God' is the Tree of divine Truth that betokeneth none but Him, and it is this divine Tree that hath raised and will raise up Messengers, and hath revealed and will ever reveal Scriptures. From eternity unto eternity this Tree of divine Truth hath served and will ever serve as the throne of the revelation and concealment of God among His creatures, and in every age is made manifest through whomsoever He pleaseth. Praise be to God, the Lord of all worlds, and blessings be upon Muhammad and his pure family. Afterward: Thus speaks the least of God's creation, Ali bin Muhammad, the son of the late Muhammad Rida (may his soul rest in peace): A group of people has attributed to this humble servant the claim to the station of Babiyyat (the Gatehood) of the Imam--peace be upon him. However, I have never made such a claim, nor do I make it now. It is certain that whoever claims such a mighty station must be adorned with all the perfections, both scholarly and practical. Such a person must lack nothing in any of the sciences or formal disciplines, and they must possess comprehensive knowledge of all outward and inward sciences, along with complete insight into every inquiry and detail. They must also manifest extraordinary deeds or miracles, approved by God, not as a potential capacity that exists in all things by God's design--either inherently or through divine bestowal--but as a fully realized and unique mastery. If such a claimant lacks even one matter of knowledge or discipline, or is incapable of performing a single act that transcends the ordinary course of nature, there is no doubt that they do not bear this exalted station. God, the All-Knowing, and the people of divine authority bear witness to this truth. I myself bear no mastery over any branch of knowledge or practice of the sciences of the learned, nor do I possess the ability to perform any supernatural acts or wonders. If words have flowed from my pen, they have done so purely out of natural inclination, and all of them contradict the norms of the community. These words are evidence of nothing. Whoever attributes the station of Babiyyat of the Imam--peace be upon him--to this lowly servant is, as God is my witness, in error and astray. Such a person will face the fire in the hereafter. In this statement, there is neither deceit nor concealment. My inner and outer self testify to the truth of what is written here. This declaration suffices. This letter is written in the hand of Mirza Muhammad Ibrahim Shirazi, and I call God to witness the truth of what I have stated. It was composed while I was in the port of Bushehr.
