



...the lover seated within the heart is better than sagacity in motion. (From a Tablet of Abdu'l-Baha, in B. Nakhjavani, Four on an Island, p. 67)

If the lovers be among them that abide within the precincts of the abode of the Lodestone of hearts, no soul may dwell on this kingly throne save the countenance of love. I am powerless to describe this station or to depict it in words. 'Love shunneth this world and that world too;In him are lunacies seventy-and-two.The minstrel of love harpeth this lay:Servitude enslaveth, lordship doth betray.' This plane demandeth pure love and unalloyed affection. In describing these companions He saith: "They speak not till He hath spoken, and act according to His commandment." In this station, neither the reign of the intellect ('aql) is sufficient nor the rule of self (nafs).... The dwellers of this abode know not the destination, yet they spur on their chargers. They see naught in the Beloved but His very Self. They find all words of sense to be meaningless, and senseless words to be full of meaning. They cannot distinguish head from foot or one limb from another. CDB#7.20-24 (was SV.54-55)
